# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Independent creators, educators, marketers, and small teams turning a Fountain screenplay into a reviewable animatic and downloadable film without creating an account. They need a clear, low-friction workflow that preserves anonymity, explains capacity limits, and never implies that a queued or degraded result is complete.

## Product Purpose

Hollywood Video is a free, anonymous, script-first filmmaking studio. The private-staging vertical slice must provide a reachable path from screenplay submission through durable generation, accountless review, HLS playback, captions, provenance, and MP4 export. Public branding and deployment remain fail-closed until the human launch gates in ADR-0020 pass.

## Brand Personality

Calm, cinematic, trustworthy. Copy is direct about limits, queue state, degraded audio, and approval gates. The interface supports the creative task without pretending that infrastructure or legal gates have already cleared.

## Anti-references

- Signup-first SaaS onboarding, pricing gates, or hidden account creation.
- Decorative movie-industry nostalgia that obscures the screenplay workflow.
- Black-box progress states, fake completion, or silent failure.
- Dense operator language on the creator-facing surface.

## Design Principles

- Start with the screenplay and keep the primary path visible.
- Show durable progress and actionable failure states in plain language.
- Treat anonymity and signed-link access as product features, not footnotes.
- Make review and export evidence explicit before expensive generation proceeds.
- Keep public-launch controls visibly separate from private-staging readiness.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA. Provide keyboard-visible focus, explicit labels, live status announcements, reduced-motion-safe transitions, sufficient contrast, captions on every export, and status communication that does not depend on color alone.
