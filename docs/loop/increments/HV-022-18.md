# HV-022-18 — A screenplay revision's generation held its whole stage cap

epic: HV-022
release: 2 "Voice and crew depth"
gate: none new (no cap or default is raised; less money is held)
spend_usd: 0

## Why

HV-019-06 made every paid render hold what it can actually spend, not its cap. The hold is the
dearest eligible provider's estimate for each shot it generates, times the three attempts its retry
policy allows, and never more than the cap. It did this because a ten-shot film holding $50 of caps
was refused at its own $40 limit.

The living-script generation quote was never changed and still held the cap itself. A revision's
preview whose two stills cost $0.006 at the dearest provider held **$5**. A few screenplay revisions
would fill a film's $40 limit, and after that its ordinary renders were refused as over the limit.

There was also a small defect in HV-019-06's own formula in the render route. `0.4 * 3 * 100` is
`120.00000000000001` in floating point, so a bare `Math.ceil` held $1.21 for $1.20.

## What this increment does

- **`renderHold(maximumEstimateUsd, costCapUsd)`** in `packages/operator/src/film-budget.ts` is the
  one hold rule. It rounds the product to six places before taking the ceiling.
- **The render route and the screenplay-revision route both hold through it.**
- **HV-022-16's refusal test** needs a film limit below what the preview now holds, so its limit
  moves from $0.03 to $0.01. What it checks is unchanged.

## Acceptance criteria

1. **A revision's paid preview holds what it can spend.** The cap is $5, the estimate is $0.006, and
   the hold is $0.02. The preview is admitted and the film holds $0.02.
2. **The hold is the estimate for every attempt, rounded up to the cent.** It is at least a cent and
   at most the cap, and $0.40 holds $1.20, not $1.21.
3. **Both routes that hold for a render do it through `renderHold`.**
4. Reverting the two routes fails criteria 1 and 3.

- Tests: `packages/api/test/living-script-film-cap.test.ts`. The film-budget, living-script, render
  and operator suites pass (129 / 0).

## Known gaps this leaves

- **The retry count of three is written in two places:** the hold rule, and each route's
  `retryPolicy`. A route that changes its retries has to change the hold with it.
