# HV-030-33 — Every sequence of a feature is scored against the picture the worker made

epic: HV-030
release: 3 "Features"
gate: none new
spend_usd: 0

## Why

Release 3's strict-$0 rehearsals 2 and 3 ran "The Tide Clock" on staging, on the mock providers: 202
shots in ten sequences, driven by `scripts/studio-run.ts --format feature --lock WREN,OSWIN
--continuity-repair`. Each sequence is finished as `packages/frontend/src/studio.js` finishes it: the
final, the cast's voices, the ambience, then the Composer's score (`POST /sound-mixes/:cutId`, a
`sound-mix` job).

- In rehearsal 3 the score failed for 9 of 10 sequences, and in rehearsal 2 for 8 of 9. Each failed
  with "The selected picture length changed." (`packages/generator/src/sound-media.ts:55`): the
  picture's measured frames differed from the job's `totalFrames`.
- Each failure was tried three times (queued, queued, failed, code `internal`, 1.3–2.6 s each). Job
  `ab4160d3` (sequence 10's score) failed. Job `7b91e5c4` succeeded.
- The run carried on. The joined feature (12,300 frames in its record) has no music on 9 sequences,
  and nothing in the run's record said so.

**The measured cause.** "The Tide Clock" was run here through the studio flow on the mock providers,
as far as sequence 1 (23 shots). Its score failed the same way.

- The worker assembles a final with a 15-frame dissolve between shots unless one of its shots carries
  recorded speech, and then it cuts (`assemble`, `FINAL_SHOT_CROSSFADE_FRAMES`). A dissolved final runs
  15 frames shorter per join than its shot records add up to. Sequence 1's final is 23 shots of 60
  frames: 1,380 frames in its records, 1,050 in its picture.
- The score's length, `soundBaseFrames`, was the shot records' sum (`dialogueSource(...).totalFrames`).
  The worker measured the picture. A dissolved final could never be scored.
- Sequence 1's final carried no speech because the mock final keeps a rough-cut shot's voice only
  when the two shots are the same length (`roughCutDialogue`). Its three lines ran 80, 149 and 268
  frames in the rough cut and 60 in the final. So the final had no speech, and it dissolved.
- A sequence whose final kept any line cuts, and its score worked. That is the one sequence scored.
- The joined feature's interchange export (HV-023-05) already counted the dissolve. The score didn't.

**Why nobody saw it.** `studio.js` turns a failed score into a note on that sequence's approval. The
next sequence replaced the notes, and the joined film showed only the last sequence's. `studio-run.ts`
reported only those. The Release 3 contract took any film id as a sequence's "finished film", and a
sequence whose score failed is joined as its bare final.

## What this increment does

**1. The score is pinned to the picture the worker made** (`packages/planner/src/feature-film.ts`,
`sound-jobs.ts`, `feature-interchange.ts`, `packages/api/src/sound-ambience.ts`).

- `assembledShotOverlap(stage, records)` is the worker's rule: 15 frames in a final of two or more
  shots with no recorded speech, otherwise none. `assembledShotSpans` gives each shot's span on the
  picture. Every span but the last loses the overlap, so the spans add up to the picture's length.
- `soundBaseFrames` of a film is now its spans' sum. A dialogue or lip-sync version keeps its own
  measured length. So the score's job, its session, the quote's `durationSec` and the studio's
  music cue all run for the picture.
- The ambience route places each scene on the same spans. Before, a dissolved film's scenes would
  have fallen back to one bed.
- The interchange export reads the same rule, so the two can't drift.
- The worker's check is unchanged. A picture that measures any other length still fails.
- Only a dissolved final changes. Its score could never have finished, so no finished score's record
  changes. A cut film's spans are its records, as before.

**2. A failed score is said, and recorded** (`packages/frontend/src/studio.js`, `scripts/studio-run.ts`).

- Each sequence keeps its finishing notes with it (`finishes[n].notes`), the voices' and the score's.
- The joined film's notes name each earlier sequence that fell short, for example "Sequence 3 of 10:
  Composer: the score could not be mixed (…); the film is shared without music." The last
  sequence's notes follow, as before.
- `studio-run.ts` records each sequence's finishing (`voiced`, `scored`, `ambience`, `notes`), lists
  the unscored sequences in `feature.unscored`, and says so on stderr. A failed score still costs a
  note, not the film, as the voices and the titles do.

**3. The contract reads the truth** (`scripts/release-3-run.ts`, `test/release-3-contract.ts`).

- `release-3-run.ts` reads each sequence's film back from the studio (`GET /api/jobs/:filmJobId`) and
  records its stage as `filmStage`, with the notes `studio-run.ts` kept for it.
- Criterion 1's "a finished film" now means the Composer's mix of the final. A sequence whose film
  isn't a `sound-mix` fails: "sequence 3's film was not scored (the studio holds it as final)". This
  holds in a rehearsal too, since mock scores at $0. A record that doesn't say fails.
- The synthetic fixture's sequences say `"filmStage": "sound-mix"`.

## Acceptance criteria

1. **A dissolved sequence is scored.** Through the studio flow on mock, sequence 2 of a feature is 22
   shots and no line. Its final measures 1,005 frames (1,320 in its records). Its film is a finished
   `sound-mix` of that final, its job and its report are 1,005 frames, and its export decodes 1,005.
2. **The check still bites.** Sequence 1's final cuts, and its score is pinned to 180 frames. With its
   picture made to measure 181, the score fails with "The selected picture length changed." and the
   flow keeps the final.
3. **A failed score is said.** The joined feature's notes name "Sequence 1 of 2" and why it has no
   music. `studio-run.ts` records each sequence's finishing, lists `unscored`, and says so on stderr.
   No Editor note is kept as a sequence's finishing.
4. **The contract reads the truth.** `release-3-run.ts` records each sequence's film stage from the
   studio. A sequence joined as its bare final fails the contract by name, as does one recorded as a
   voiced but unscored film, in a rehearsal, and one whose record doesn't say.
5. **Reverting the change fails the new tests.** Restoring the 8 changed source files from `main`
   fails both tests in `feature-sequence-score.test.ts`, the new tests in `release-3-run.test.ts` and
   `release-3-driver.test.ts` (2 there), and the updated front-door test, on their assertions (checked).

- Tests: `packages/api/test/feature-sequence-score.test.ts` (1, 2, 3; 2 pass), `test/release-3-driver.test.ts`
  (3, 4; 11 pass), `test/release-3-run.test.ts` (4; 19 pass, 1 skipped until the real record).
  Unchanged and passing: `feature-finishing-bounded.test.ts`, the API sound-mix, ambience, voices,
  sound key, feature interchange and feature studio suites, `packages/planner/test` (feature
  interchange, feature film, ambience), `packages/generator/test/sound-ambience.test.ts`, the
  frontend studio, feature join and feature sequence suites, and `test/release-3-scripts.test.ts`.

## Known gaps

- **A mock final drops a rough-cut line whose shot ran longer.** The mock final paces each shot to the
  plan (60 frames); the rough cut stretches a shot to fit its line. `roughCutDialogue` keeps a voice
  only for a shot of the same length, so most of "The Tide Clock"'s finals carry no speech, and no
  cast voices are laid into them. That is the mock final's pacing, and is left alone here. The score
  now works either way.
- **A dissolved final's own `totalFrames` is still its records' sum.** The worker sets it at
  admission. So is a joined feature's `totalFrames`, which adds its films' (12,300 in rehearsal 3's
  record). A scored film's is now its picture's. The joined film's measured length is in its
  `provenance.json`, which the contract reads.
- **Narration on a dissolved final** would fail the dialogue route's own length check. The studio
  flow doesn't add narration.
- **The run doesn't stop on a failed score.** It is recorded and fails the contract, as a failed voice
  pass or title is a note. Staging hasn't been rerun. The rehearsal's rerun will show it.
