# HV-027-11 — A finished film could not be exported once another project had spent the month

epic: HV-027
release: 2 "Voice and crew depth"
gate: none new
spend_usd: 0

## Why

Once the month's spend reaches the budget, `CapacityController.decide` refuses every admission:

```ts
if (utilization >= 1) return {action:"reject",reason:"budget_exhausted",message:"We're at capacity right now. Your script is saved — please try again soon."};
```

Five routes ask it, passing the program's spend: deliverables, sound mixes, editorial exports,
assembly exports and motion graphics. None of the jobs they admit holds a cent or calls a provider.
Each admits with `costCapUsd:0` and `budgetReservedUsd:0`. Even so, one project's paid renders
stopped every other creator from reframing, mixing or exporting a film they had already made. The
refusal talked about a saved script, and the delivery offers beside it still said "available":

```
zero-cost deliverable request: 429 {"error":"We're at capacity right now. …","reason":"budget_exhausted"}
offers still say available: reframe-9:16:false,reframe-1:1:true,mezzanine:true
```

Above 80% of the month these routes also queued behind a "budget throttle" that protects money
these jobs never spend.

## What this increment does

- **`decide` takes `requestedUsd`**, the amount the job will hold. A job that holds nothing is not
  refused or throttled by the month's spend. The shot limit and per-project concurrency still apply.
- **Callers that don't pass `requestedUsd` behave exactly as before.** That covers every paid
  route.
- **The five zero-cost admissions pass `requestedUsd:0`.** These are in `delivery-api.ts`,
  `sound-api.ts`, `graphic-api.ts`, and `edit-api.ts` (two admissions: the editorial export and the
  assembly export).

## Acceptance criteria

1. **`decide` for a job that holds nothing, at 100% and at 90% of the month, answers `run`.** A job
   that holds money is still refused. A caller that says nothing still gets `budget_exhausted` and
   `budget_throttle`, and concurrency still queues a free job.
2. **Every capacity decision in the four route files says `requestedUsd:0`**, and none of those files
   reserves any budget.
3. **A deliverable of a finished film is admitted (202) after another project has spent the month**,
   while a paid render of the same project is still refused (429).
4. Reverting `packages/queue/src/index.ts` and the four routes fails all three criteria.

- Tests: `packages/api/test/zero-cost-capacity.test.ts`. `packages/queue`, the delivery route and
  the sound, graphic and edit route suites all pass, apart from `degraded-resume.test.ts`. That test
  times out in this container on main as well, and #252 fixes it.

## Known gaps this leaves

- **The concurrency limit still counts these jobs** against a project's running work. That is
  correct, because they use the worker, but a long mezzanine can still queue a paid render behind
  it.
- **Crew calls** have their own ledger and ceiling, and are not affected.
