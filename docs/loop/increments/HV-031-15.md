# HV-031-15 — Every export carries a signed C2PA manifest beside it, when the host holds the key

epic: HV-031
release: 2 "Voice and crew depth"
gate: G15 item 4 (self-issued ES256 key on the host, approved 2026-09-30)
spend_usd: 0

## Why

Every export's `provenance.json` was JSON in C2PA's shape, unsigned, and said so by its type:
`c2pa-style`. HV-031-02 wrote that down as the open half of FR-018: "no CBOR/JUMBF, no embedding, no
signature, no key". The roadmap's HV-031 line asks for signed C2PA.

On 2026-09-30 Kevin approved signing with a self-issued ES256 key generated on the staging host, mode
600, never printed or moved. Public validators will show the signer as unrecognized. A trust-list
certificate is a Release 4 (G7) item.

The record can't be embedded in the film. Five flows compare a retained export's bytes to the sha256
its record claims (sound, edit, edit-assembly, lip-sync, dialogue), and embedding rewrites the MP4.

## What this increment does

- **A real C2PA manifest store beside the MP4, `provenance.c2pa`.** It is signed with
  `@contentauth/c2pa-node` 0.9.9, the Content Authenticity Initiative's own Node binding, pinned
  exactly. Its hard binding is C2PA's BMFF hash over the MP4's boxes, so one changed byte of film
  breaks it. It asserts `c2pa.created` with IPTC's `trainedAlgorithmicMedia`. It also carries an
  `hv.provenance` assertion naming the spec, issuer, project, assembly time and the MP4's sha256.
- **The MP4 is untouched.** A signed export's MP4 is byte for byte the unsigned one.
- **The key comes only from the host.** `HV_C2PA_SIGNING_KEY` and `HV_C2PA_SIGNING_CERT` are file
  paths. The key may be SEC1 (`EC PRIVATE KEY`) or PKCS#8; c2pa-node takes only PKCS#8, so it is
  converted in memory. Neither file's contents is logged, returned or put in an error.
- **Checked before anything is generated.** The worker checks the signing configuration first
  thing at startup (`assertC2paSigningConfig`) and refuses to start on a half-set, unreadable or
  wrong one. Without that, a bad configuration would fail each export only after its shots were
  generated and paid for. The assembler checks again at its entry, beside the assembly-time check.
  - It refuses a key that isn't P-256 and a certificate that isn't the key's.
  - It refuses a CA certificate, and a leaf without a C2PA signing EKU, naming documentSigning
    (1.3.6.1.5.5.7.3.36) as the fix.
  - It refuses any certificate in the chain that is outside its validity window, naming the date.
  - A native binding that cannot load is reported as that, not as a refused key.
- **The EKU list is explicit.** c2pa-node's default settings allow no EKUs at all. Every certificate
  then reads "missing required EKU", and with verify-after-sign on by default even a correct one
  fails to sign. The five EKUs the C2PA specification names are passed on every sign and every read.
- **Honest when unsigned.** With neither variable set, nothing is signed, no sidecar is written and
  the record stays `c2pa-style`, exactly as before. With only one set, the worker refuses to start,
  because a half-configured host would ship unsigned films its operator believes are signed.
- **The worker signs off the event loop.** It uses the Builder's `signAsync` with a callback signer
  (node's ES256 in IEEE P1363 form). The MP4's digest is streamed before and after, yielding per
  chunk and honouring cancellation. On a 52 MB export the longest event-loop gap was 82–98 ms, where
  the synchronous sign blocked about 1.1 s. A job cancelled mid-sign stopped within 8 ms. The
  synchronous form remains for `assemble()` (benchmarks and local tooling) only.
- **The record names the sidecar.** A signed record's credentials are
  `{type: "c2pa-sidecar", issuer, claim, sidecar: {name: "provenance.c2pa", sha256}}`. It's a name,
  not a path, because public provenance carries no storage paths. The sidecar signs the MP4, never
  the JSON, so neither refers to the other in a circle. `provenanceMatches` accepts exactly the two
  forms. It refuses a signed record with no sidecar or a malformed one, and an unsigned record
  carrying a sidecar.
- **The sidecar goes wherever the record goes.** It goes through the same paths as `provenance.json`:
  - The job records `output.c2paPath`, and `takeClips[].c2paPath` for take groups.
  - The worker publishes the sidecar with the film, the mixed film and each take.
  - The API links it as `c2paUrl` and serves it as `application/c2pa`.
  - Artifact import and restore, the mixed delivery index, the mixed archive check, snapshots, the
    retained-cut receipt and the proof closure all list it beside `manifestPath`. The shared store
    writes the object as `application/c2pa`.
  - Snapshots and artifact import and restore require each sidecar, the export's and each take's,
    to be exactly the `provenance.c2pa` beside its own record. Restore compares the downloaded
    sidecar's checksum to the digest its record names.
  - A signed preview's sidecar is a delivery role in the proof closure, beside its record only. It
    is copied with the preview, and the proof media verifier checks the copy's bytes against the
    copied record.
  - The current-film, mixed and pending-screenplay output validators accept a `c2paPath` only
    beside its own record.
  - The mixed media verifier checks the sidecar's bytes against the record's digest. A signed
    record and a sidecar come together or not at all.
- **`scripts/verify-c2pa.ts <export dir> [--anchor root.pem]`** checks one export the way a person
  checking a contested film would. It checks that:
  - the record says the export is signed and names this sidecar's bytes;
  - its claim is bound to this MP4;
  - the manifest is intact and bound to the MP4;
  - the signed assertion names the MP4's sha256.

  Without an anchor the expected result is `Valid` with only `signingCredential.untrusted`. With the
  host's own certificate as anchor it is `Trusted`.

## Acceptance criteria

1. **A host with a key signs every export with a sidecar a C2PA reader trusts when given the issuing
   root.** The record is `c2pa-sidecar` and names the sidecar's sha256, and the signed assertion names
   the MP4's sha256.
2. **Without an anchor the same export reads `Valid` with `signingCredential.untrusted` and no other
   status**, which is what public validators will show for the self-issued key.
3. **The signed MP4 is byte-identical to the unsigned export of the same inputs.**
4. **One changed byte of film fails the sidecar** with `assertion.bmffHash.mismatch`.
5. **The production path signs from `HV_C2PA_SIGNING_KEY` and `HV_C2PA_SIGNING_CERT`.** The key is
   SEC1, so the in-memory conversion is exercised.
6. **With neither variable set there is no sidecar, no `c2paPath`, no `c2paUrl`, and the record is
   `c2pa-style`.** With only one set, the export refuses.
7. **A leaf without a C2PA EKU refuses before any media is encoded, naming the fix.** So does a
   certificate for another key, and a key file that isn't a key.
8. **`provenanceMatches` accepts the signed and the unsigned record and nothing in between.** The
   literals `c2pa-sidecar` and `provenance.c2pa` are spelled in exactly one source file.
9. **The worker completes a signed job with `output.c2paPath` beside the record, the sidecar on disk
   and named by the record, and a `c2paUrl` link.**
10. **A signed mixed (V3) export passes independent media verification.** It is refused when its
    record is signed but the output has no sidecar, when the sidecar's bytes change, and when the
    sidecar sits anywhere but beside its record. The mixed output validator accepts the sidecar
    beside the record and refuses it elsewhere.
11. **A snapshot carries a sidecar path and refuses one outside its job.**
12. **`scripts/verify-c2pa.ts` passes a signed export as `Trusted` with the anchor and as
    `Valid`/untrusted without, and exits 1 on a tampered film.**
13. **A signed preview's sidecar is an owned, required delivery role in the proof index, beside its
    record only.** Before this, the closure refused it as "an unowned delivery or media role".
14. **A sidecar is stored and served as `application/c2pa`,** and a signed cut's `c2paUrl` serves it.
15. **A record and its sidecar's bytes agree, and every sidecar, the export's and each take's, is the
    `provenance.c2pa` beside its own record.** A snapshot refuses a sidecar path renamed to another
    file in the job.
16. **The worker refuses to start on a half-set or EKU-less configuration.** Nothing is claimed.
    The startup check passes an unconfigured or correct host.
17. **A certificate outside its validity window is refused at load, naming the date.**
18. **A native library that cannot load is named as such** (run with `C2PA_LIBRARY_PATH` pointing
    nowhere).
19. **Signing honours cancellation and writes no sidecar when cancelled.**
20. **Reverting the integration fails 10 of the new tests.** The integration is the assembler, worker,
    validators, storage and API, with `c2pa.ts`, `provenance.ts` and the script kept. The 10 are
    criteria 1–5, 7, 8 (the census), 9, 11 and 12. A full revert fails every new test file.
    Reverting only the review-round sources fails the proof-index test (13), the snapshot test (15)
    and the startup test (16). Three files that import the new functions fail to load:
    `c2pa-sidecar.test.ts` (17–19), `artifacts.test.ts` (the storage half of 14) and
    `provenance.test.ts` (15). The API half of 14 still passes, because `server.ts` already served
    `application/c2pa`.

## Operator steps

These are done once, on the staging host, by the operator. No key enters the chat, the repository or
any evidence file.

1. **The certificate needs a C2PA EKU.** If the host's existing certificate for the approved key
   carries no extended key usage, or only `anyExtendedKeyUsage`, re-issue it for the same key. The key
   does not move and is not printed. A self-signed leaf works and reads `Valid`/untrusted:

   ```sh
   openssl req -x509 -new -key "$KEY" -days 825 -subj "/CN=Rough Cut staging signer/O=Rough Cut" \
     -addext "basicConstraints=critical,CA:FALSE" -addext "keyUsage=critical,digitalSignature" \
     -addext "extendedKeyUsage=1.3.6.1.5.5.7.3.36" -out /root/.config/rough-cut/c2pa-cert.pem
   ```

   If the host already has a private root, sign the leaf with it instead and put the leaf first, then
   the root, in one PEM file. `scripts/verify-c2pa.ts --anchor` with that root then reads `Trusted`.
2. **Set the two paths in `secrets.env`** (mode 600): `HV_C2PA_SIGNING_KEY=<key file path>` and
   `HV_C2PA_SIGNING_CERT=<certificate chain path>`. Both files stay mode 600. Then restart the workers.
   A worker that finds a half-set, unreadable, expired or EKU-less configuration refuses to start and
   says which. Fix the configuration rather than unsetting one variable.
3. **Check one export:** `bun scripts/verify-c2pa.ts <artifact root>/<project>/<job>`. Expect
   `"ok":true,"state":"Valid","codes":["signingCredential.untrusted"]`.

Until step 2 is done, exports stay unsigned and say so.

## Known gaps this leaves

- **The signer is unrecognized publicly.** That is the approved state. A trust-list certificate is
  G7 in Release 4.
- **No timestamp authority.** The signature carries no RFC 3161 timestamp, so a validator can't prove
  the signing time beyond the certificate's validity window. Adding one is a network call to a third
  party, which is a vendor decision.
- **The other six manifest writers stay unsigned.** Edit, edit-assembly, lip-sync, sound,
  dialogue-replacement and graphic exports write their own `provenance.json` schemas, and deliverables
  (reframe, mezzanine, SDH) write their own records. Only the assembler's exports are signed here: the
  film, the mixed film and takes.
- **Readers check the sidecar's digest, not its signature.** Three places compare a sidecar's bytes
  to its record: the mixed media verifier, the proof media verifier and the shared store's restore.
  - `provenanceMatches`, used by the reuse flows, checks the record's shape only.
  - Artifact serving, the local store, snapshots and the ordinary (non-current-film) worker
    completion check the sidecar's path, not its bytes.
  - Running a full C2PA validation on every reuse is left out. `scripts/verify-c2pa.ts` is the full
    check.
- **The native sign can't be interrupted.** Cancellation is honoured between the digest chunks and
  as soon as `signAsync` returns. The longest event-loop gap measured, 82–98 ms on 52 MB, is inside
  c2pa-node.
- **The sync `assemble()` still signs synchronously,** through Builder's `withJson`, which 0.9.9
  marks deprecated. The version is pinned exactly, so an upgrade is a visible change.
- **Some wiring is checked by type, not by a test.** No local test covers:
  - the S3-backed publish/restore path, including the restore digest comparison, because it needs
    an object store that isn't available locally;
  - the take-group export;
  - the v2 current-film and pending-screenplay validators;
  - the proof media verifier's sidecar comparison for a signed non-mixed preview. Its index half is
    tested (criterion 13) and its comparison is `provenanceSidecarAgrees` (criterion 15).

  That wiring mirrors `manifestPath`.
- **Exports made before this stay unsigned.** No backfill: a provenance record isn't rewritten after
  the fact.
- **`@contentauth/c2pa-node` adds a 52 MB linux-x64 native binding to every install.** It ships in
  the npm tarball, so Bun's blocked postinstall (a GitHub download) isn't needed. CI and the staging
  host are both linux-x64 with glibc ≥ 2.34.
