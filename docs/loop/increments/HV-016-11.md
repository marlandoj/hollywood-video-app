# HV-016-11 — A film that was still being made had nowhere to come back to

epic: HV-016
release: 2 "Voice and crew depth"
gate: none new
spend_usd: 0

## Why

Two increments from the same night each named this, from their own side:

> **HV-016-09:** Nothing resumes a render in flight. A project whose final is still running resumes
> to the rough cut rather than waiting on that job, even though HV-030-08 now gives the studio a
> bounded way to wait.

> **HV-030-08:** The studio does not offer to resume. The refusal gives the job id and says asking
> again is free, but nothing takes the creator back to that job.

So the state a creator is **most likely** to close the tab in — the film is rendering, it takes
minutes, they go and do something else — resumed to the step *below* the render, where the only
button renders something. The job was already admitted, already running and already paid for.

HV-016-10 and HV-017-12 made asking again cost nothing, which removes the money from this. What is
left is the experience: the studio knows a film is being made for you, and said nothing about it.

## What this increment does

- **`resume` reports a render in flight as `pending`** — its stage, its job id and its status —
  preferring a finished job over an unfinished one at every step, so a done final is still the film.
- **`waitForPending()` polls it** with the ceiling HV-030-08 gave `pollJob`: thirty minutes without
  progress, derived from the queue's own lease clock, with a message that names the job rather than
  calling it a failure.
- **The three finishing passes are one function now**, shared by `approveRoughCut` and by a resumed
  wait. A film that finished while the tab was closed must be finished the same way as one that
  finished in front of the creator, and two copies of that tail would have drifted the first time
  one of them changed. The final is in the state before any of them runs, so each failure still
  costs a note rather than the film.
- **A resumed animatic lands on the rough cut without the storyboard pinning pass**, because that
  pass needs the crew's plan (`state.plan.finalAnchors`) and the plan is not retained. The note says
  so and says what it costs: the final begins from the script rather than from the stills.
- **The renderer offers the wait** above whichever step the resume landed on.

## Acceptance criteria

1. **A project whose final is still being made resumes to the rough cut with the film named as
   pending**, and the note says it is already paid for and what the finishing will be missing.
2. **Waiting for it lands on the finished film, finished the way an approval finishes one**: the
   creator gets the *scored* cut, not the raw final, because the passes replace the cut as they go —
   and what could not be done is a note, not a failure.
3. **A project whose rough cut is still being made waits for that instead of pitching again**, and
   the note names the pinning pass that will not run and what that costs.
4. **A finished film still wins over anything in flight**: a done final with a second one queued
   behind it resumes to the film, and there is nothing to wait for.
5. **A render that stops is a refusal, not a film**: the server's own reason is raised, and the
   creator is left where they were with the job still named as pending.
6. Reverting `studio.js` fails criteria 1–5, because `flow.waitForPending` does not exist.

- Test: `packages/frontend/test/studio-pending.test.js`. Its fake refuses to answer more than sixty
  requests, because a fake that answers a retry loop with the same unhelpful answer hangs instead of
  failing — and a hang says nothing. That guard caught a real mistake in this file's own fixture
  while it was being written.

## Known gaps this leaves

- **The pinning pass is not recovered, only reported.** Retaining the crew's plan alongside the
  script would let a resumed animatic pin its stills like a live one, and that is a storage change.
- **The wait is not resumable in its turn.** Close the tab again during `waitForPending` and the next
  resume starts the same wait over, which is correct but means the thirty-minute ceiling starts
  again rather than counting the whole time the render has been stuck.
- **Only the two picture stages are watched.** A voice, score, title or graphic job in flight when
  the tab closed is not resumed; those are steps inside a finishing pass rather than steps of the
  studio, and the pass re-runs from the beginning, which is free because each of their keys is fixed
  by content.
- **`index.html` is still untested**, the same gap HV-016-09 named: this repo has no DOM harness, so
  the button the renderer now draws is exercised only through the flow beneath it.
