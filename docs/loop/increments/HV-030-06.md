# HV-030-06 — The shot editor knows the longest shot this studio can render

epic: HV-030
release: 2 "Voice and crew depth"
gate: none new
spend_usd: 0

## Why

A shot direction is provider-agnostic on purpose: it outlives the pool that renders it, and the
contract bounds a shot at 1 to 30 seconds. The shot editor offers exactly that range.

The configured pool often renders less. `kling-v2.5-turbo-pro` — the default paid model — renders at
most 10 seconds. So a creator can type 12, save it cleanly, watch the direction version increment,
and then be told at admission:

> No configured provider supports these render requirements: duration.

That names no shot, no number, and no way forward, on a screen that offered the length in the first
place. It is the same shape of defect as HV-030-05: a bound declared in one file, enforced in
another, and reported in neither's words.

Nothing here loosens the contract. The contract's 30 seconds stands — a direction saved today may be
rendered next year by a pool that can hold it. What changes is that the editor is told the *current*
pool's narrower limit, and admission says what that limit is when it refuses.

## What this increment does

- **`DIRECTION_MIN_DURATION_SEC` / `DIRECTION_MAX_DURATION_SEC`** are declared once in the direction
  contract, and the validator's own message is built from them rather than repeating the numbers.
- **`GET /api/projects/:id/direction` carries `durationLimitSec`**: the longest duration any
  configured final provider declares, capped by the contract, defaulting to the contract's limit
  when no pool is configured or the pool declares nothing.
- **The shot editor binds to it** — the field's max as built, the max once a view loads, the note
  under the fieldset ("This project's providers render at most 10 seconds a shot; a longer shot has
  to be split into coverage"), and a refusal on save before the request is sent.
- **Admission names the shot and the number** when duration is why nothing matched: "No configured
  provider can render shot s1-2 at 30.0 s; the longest they render is 10 s. Shorten it or split it
  into coverage." The limit reported is the pool actually in play, because this same admission runs
  for a rough cut, whose pool is not the final one.

## Acceptance criteria

1. **The served view states the pool's limit**: 30 s for a free pool, 10 s for Kling's turbo model,
   15 s for its keyframe model — read from the pool, not a constant.
   - Test: `packages/api/test/direction.test.ts`.
2. **The contract still bounds a save** independently of the pool: 900 frames saves, 901 and 29 are
   refused, with the range named in the message.
   - Same test, and `packages/planner/test/direction.test.ts` unchanged.
3. **Admission names the shot, its duration and the pool's limit**, reached without rendering or
   reserving anything, and reports the pool in play rather than the final pool.
   - Same test, through a take quote.
4. **The editor's ceiling comes from the served view**, in all three places it is applied, with no
   second copy of the number in the module.
   - Test: `packages/api/test/frontend-modules.test.ts`. This is a source check: the editor is a DOM
     module with a dozen collaborators and is not driven in a shim, so what is pinned is the
     binding, not the rendered behaviour.
5. **Nothing else changes.** The direction, direction-application, crew and API route suites pass
   unchanged.
