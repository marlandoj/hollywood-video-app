# HV-022-08 — A voice vendor's own budget line

epic: HV-022 (performance: voice, dialogue, lip-sync, ADR)
release: 2 "Voice and crew depth"
gate: G14-202609210000 (the operator approved ElevenLabs with a $25 ceiling and alerts at $5 and $15)
spend_usd: 0

## Why

The operator approved the second voice vendor with a line of its own: **$25, alerts at $5 and $15**.
Before this, a vendor's takes were bounded only by the film's $40 cap and the program's $500 cap —
so a handful of films could spend the whole voice budget without anything saying so, and nothing
distinguished one vendor's commitment from another's.

The vendor sells a prepaid character allowance rather than per-call billing, so what the studio has
*committed* to it is the honest measure: the takes it has paid for plus the holds its queued takes
carry, until the operator allocates that vendor's invoice.

## What this increment does

- **`packages/operator/src/voice-vendor-budget.ts`**: `HV_VOICE_VENDOR_CAP_USD` (default $25, never
  above the monthly cap), the alert thresholds, `assertVoiceVendorBudget` and `voiceVendorAlerts`
  (each threshold crossed once, by the take that crosses it).
- **`PostgresAudioLedger.voiceVendorSpend(provider)`**: what one vendor's audio takes have spent and
  hold, read from the takes' own policies, so one vendor's line never counts another's takes.
- **`admitAudio`** checks the line inside the same transaction that reserves the hold, after the
  film's limit and before the reservation: a refused take holds nothing and enqueues nothing.
- **The API** passes the operator's line to every audio admission.

It never raises anything. The monthly cap, the per-film cap and the per-shot cap are unchanged, and
a take that costs nothing is never refused by a budget.

## Acceptance criteria

1. **The line is the operator's.** $25 by default, settable, never above the monthly cap, and a
   nonsense value is refused.
2. **Spent plus held plus this take may not pass it**, with the vendor named in the refusal, and a
   free take always passes.
3. **Each alert is crossed once**, by the take that crosses it.
   - Tests for 1–3: `packages/operator/test/voice-vendor-budget.test.ts`.
4. **Only that vendor's takes count against it**, and a take past the line is refused inside
   admission: nothing is reserved and no job is enqueued; with room on the line the same take is
   admitted.
   - Test: `packages/storage/test/audio-jobs.test.ts` (PostgreSQL).
5. **Nothing else changes.** The audio admission, dialogue and audio-studio suites pass unchanged.
