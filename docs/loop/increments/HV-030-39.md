# HV-030-39 — `--resume` redoes a sequence's finishing step that died

epic: HV-030
release: 3 "Features"
gate: none new
spend_usd: 0

## Why

Release 3's live run (G23) was carried on by `studio-run.ts --resume` (HV-030-37). It reused sequence
1's 8 rendered shots, and sequence 1's final completed. Then the final's voices pass
(`dialogue-replacement`, "Laying the cast's voices into the final") hung in the worker's object-store
upload. HV-030-38 (#383) fixes the upload.

The studio waits on a render for 30 minutes without progress (`STALL_LIMIT_MS`). Then `pollJob` throws
"This render has not moved for 30 minutes. It is still running on the server as job …". `finishFinal`
keeps the film and notes: "Casting: the cast's production voices could not be recorded (…); the film
keeps its temporary voices." That note is in the sequence's `finishes` and in the run report's
`feature.sequences[].finished` (`voiced: false`). But the report is written only when the run ends.

The flow gave up at 15:17 and carried on to the music, the ambience and the score, all on the unvoiced
final. The score's sound mix is queued behind the hung voices job, which is still `running`. To deploy
the fix, the operator cancels the hung job, stops `studio-run`, deploys and resumes. But on a resume:

- every finishing step asks with its fixed key, and the studio answers a repeated key with the job it
  has. The cancelled voices pass is answered with itself for ever, so the sequence stays unvoiced;
- a score that failed or was cancelled stays unscored the same way, and the contract needs each
  sequence's film to be a score (HV-030-33);
- the music cue was keyed by the cut. A score on the voiced cut would buy a second cue;
- a run stopped with a signal wrote no record, so where it stopped and why was lost.

## What this increment does

- **Finishing steps that died are asked for again** (`askFinishing` in
  `packages/frontend/src/studio.js`). This covers the cast's takes, the voices pass, the score, the
  titles and the join. A resumed flow has read the project's jobs, and for each step it looks at the
  jobs of that key and its retries:
  - a job done or running is kept or waited on, under its own key;
  - if every one ended failed or cancelled, the step is asked for under `<key>-retry-<n>`, where n
    counts them;
  - with none, the key is the same as before.

  The flow's normal order holds: the voices on the final, then the ambience and the score on the voiced
  cut. Nothing renders a picture. A flow that hasn't resumed asks exactly as before.
- **The flow logs what each step did** (`flow.finishLog`): its sequence, step, key and job, and whether
  it was `kept`, `waited`, `made` or `retried`. A retried step names the job it replaces, that job's
  status and its failure or cancel reason. Takes are logged only when retried.
- **The generated music cue is keyed by the final it scores** (`crew-music-<final id>`), not the cut. A
  final scored again on its voiced cut gets the cue already paid for.
- **The resume record says what was redone** (`scripts/studio-run.ts`). Each `resumes[]` entry gains
  `finishing` (the flow's log), `finishing: "kept" | "redone"` on each sequence, and `why`. `why` holds
  the earlier run's Casting and Composer notes for each sequence it redid.
- **A run stopped by SIGTERM or SIGINT writes its record**: `outcome: stopped`, the signal, and
  `interrupted`, with the step, the sequence, each sequence's finishing and the notes so far. A resume
  keeps it under `replaced`.
- The runbook (HV-030-31, step 2) says how to stop a hung finishing step and resume.

## Acceptance criteria

1. **A died finishing step is redone; one that succeeded is kept.**
   - The voices pass the operator cancelled is asked for again on the final under
     `crew-voices-<final>-retry-1`. The score is then asked for on the voiced cut, with the take and the
     music cue already made.
   - A retry still running is waited on.
   - A finished voices pass is kept.
   - Run again on a finished feature, every finishing step is kept and no job is admitted.
2. **The record says what was retried and why.** A run stopped by SIGTERM writes `outcome: stopped`,
   the signal and where it was. The resumed record lists each step with how it was asked. A retried
   step names the job it replaces, its status and its reason. The sequence is marked `redone`, and the
   stop is kept under `replaced`.
3. **The record is still the feature's.** After the resume, sequence 1's film is the retried score
   (`sound-mix`). The joined, shared feature holds both sequences as scores, as HV-030-33's contract
   needs, and `feature.unscored` is empty.
4. **No picture for the redone sequence.** The real API and worker run on mock with sequence 1's score
   held by a hung worker. The run is stopped and the hung job cancelled. `--resume` then asks for the
   score under `<key>-retry-1` on the same final, and sequence 1 gains no rough cut or final.
5. **Reverting the change fails the new tests** (checked).
   - Restoring `packages/frontend/src/studio.js` and `scripts/studio-run.ts` from `main` fails all three
     tests in `test/release-3-resume-finishing.test.ts` (main's script writes no record when stopped),
     all three in `packages/frontend/test/studio-resume-finishing.test.js`, and the key test in
     `packages/frontend/test/studio-retry.test.js`.
   - Restoring only `studio.js` fails the resume test: its score is the cancelled job, and sequence 1
     stays unscored.

- Tests: `packages/frontend/test/studio-resume-finishing.test.js` (1, 2; 3 pass),
  `test/release-3-resume-finishing.test.ts` (1 to 4; 3 pass),
  `packages/frontend/test/studio-retry.test.js` (its key test, updated; 5 pass). Unchanged and passing:
  `test/release-3-resume.test.ts` (4 pass), `test/release-3-driver.test.ts` (17 pass), the frontend
  suite.

## Known gaps

- **The cast's voices are not run through the real API here.** Takes need the operator's PostgreSQL
  audio service. The voices retry is tested against a fake of the studio's routes, and the score retry
  through the real API and worker.
- **A finishing job still running is waited on**, with the studio's 30-minute clock. A hung job must be
  cancelled before the resume, as the runbook says.
- **A stale score is not withdrawn.** The score already queued on the unvoiced final still runs (at $0,
  on the studio's machine) before the voices retry, and is not used.
- **Music keyed by the final.** A project scored before this change has its cue under the cut's key. On
  the live run the cut was the final itself, so the key is the same. If the voiced cut's length differed
  from the final's, the studio would refuse the repeated key, and the Composer's own score would be used,
  with a note.
- **The ambience isn't a job.** It is asked for again on every pass, and the route reuses its beds.
- **A record lost before this change.** A run stopped before this change wrote no record. The resume
  then starts from the last record written.
- Nothing has run on staging.
