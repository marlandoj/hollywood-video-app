# HV-024-13 — Every budget line reads its dollars as plain decimals and refuses a cap it can't compare

epic: HV-024
release: 2 "Voice and crew depth"
gate: none new
spend_usd: 0

## Why

A review of HV-024-10 found two faults in how the studio reads its spending limits.

- **The music hold rounded up a cent too far.** `musicCueHoldUsd(444)` returned $1.12, but 7.4
  minutes at $0.15 is exactly $1.11. In floating point, `444 / 60 * 0.15 * 100` is
  `111.00000000000001`, and its ceiling is 112. It was the only wrong length from 1 to 600 s, and it
  over-held.
- **A monthly cap that isn't a number was never refused.** Every line read its setting with
  `Number()`. With `HV_MONTHLY_BUDGET_USD=abc` the monthly cap was `NaN`. "Is this line above the
  monthly cap?" is never true against `NaN`, so a $1,000,000 music line was accepted. The film's
  limit and the voice line had the same shape. `Number()` also reads `"0x10"` as 16, `"1e3"` as
  1000, `" 5 "` as 5 and `"Infinity"` as Infinity.
- HV-024-10's record said a nonsense value fails at startup. Nothing tested the server's own read.

## What this increment does

- **One parser for dollar settings** (`packages/operator/src/dollar-setting.ts`).
  - A dollar setting is plain decimal dollars: digits, then optionally a point and one or two more
    (`500`, `40`, `12.5`, `12.50`).
  - It refuses hex, binary and octal, exponents, signs, surrounding whitespace, `Infinity`, `NaN`,
    separators, more than two decimals, and a number too long to be finite.
- **The monthly cap** (`monthlyBudgetCap`) is $5,000 when unset, as before. Otherwise it must be
  plain dollars above zero. A blank value is refused: it used to read as $0, which stopped the API
  anyway, behind a message about the film's limit.
- **The film's limit, the voice line and the music line** (`capUnderMonthly`) keep their defaults
  ($40, $25, $10) when unset or blank. Otherwise each is plain dollars above zero and not above the
  monthly cap. Each refuses to compare against a monthly cap that is not a finite amount above zero.
  The crew's line has no dollar setting to read.
- **The API reads all four first**, before it opens anything, so a bad value stops it with the
  setting's name. **The worker reads the monthly cap first** too, and each job reads it through the
  same parser.
- The PostgreSQL ledgers' default monthly cap and the snapshot and archive import scripts use the same
  parser.
- **The music hold is worked in whole cents.** The rate is 15 cents a minute, derived from
  `MUSIC_PRICE_USD_PER_MINUTE`; a rate that is not a whole number of cents stops the module. A cue
  now holds `ceil(whole seconds × 15 / 60)` cents, so 444 s holds $1.11.
- **No default moves.** Every value that was valid and documented reads as it did.
- **Docs:** `README.md` (the monthly cap and the other three limits), `docs/CREW.md`,
  `docs/PROVIDER-ROUTING.md`, `docs/SOUND-SESSIONS.md`.

## Acceptance criteria

1. **The parser.** Plain decimals read as written. Each of hex, binary, octal, exponent, sign,
   whitespace, `Infinity`, `NaN`, separator, a third decimal and a 400-digit number is refused.
2. **The monthly cap** is $5,000 unset and reads `5000`, `500`, `150` and `1250.50` as before. Each
   form in 1, blank, `0` and `0.00` is refused, naming `HV_MONTHLY_BUDGET_USD`.
3. **Each line** (film, voice, music):
   - keeps its default unset or blank, and reads `4`, `0.03`, `12.50` and `500` as before;
   - refuses each form in 1, naming its own setting, and `0` or more than the monthly cap as before;
   - refuses to compare against a monthly cap of `NaN`, `Infinity`, `-Infinity`, `0` or `-500`, and
     against `HV_MONTHLY_BUDGET_USD=abc` or `0x10` when it reads the monthly cap itself.
4. **The review's case**: a $1,000,000 music line under a monthly cap of `NaN` is refused.
5. **The API at startup**:
   - A monthly cap of `abc`, `0x10`, `1e3`, ` 500`, blank, `0`, `-500` or `Infinity` stops it, naming
     the setting.
   - A $1,000,000 music line under a monthly cap of `abc` does not start.
   - A film, voice or music line written as `0x10`, `1e1` or ` 5 ` stops it, naming the setting, and
     so does `501` under a $500 month.
   - Unset values, `500`, and `500` with `40`, `25` and `10` still start.
6. **The worker at startup**: a monthly cap of `abc`, `0x10`, `1e3`, `500 `, blank or `0` stops it
   before it opens its queue.
7. **The cents hold**: every whole second from 1 to 600 holds exactly its cost in cents, rounded up,
   and never below the exact amount. 444 s holds $1.11.
8. Reverting `film-budget.ts`, `voice-vendor-budget.ts`, `music-vendor-budget.ts`, `server.ts` and
   `worker.ts` fails criteria 3 (the refusals), 4, 5 (the refusals), 6 and 7.

- Tests: `packages/operator/test/dollar-setting.test.ts` (1–4),
  `packages/api/test/budget-settings-startup.test.ts` (5),
  `packages/queue/test/monthly-cap-startup.test.ts` (6), `packages/operator/test/music-vendor-budget.test.ts`
  (7). `film-budget.test.ts` and `voice-vendor-budget.test.ts` pass unchanged.

## Known gaps this leaves

- **The per-render caps are still read with `Number()`.** `HV_COST_CAP_PER_SHOT_USD`,
  `HV_ANIMATIC_COST_CAP_USD` and `HV_CHARACTER_SHEET_COST_CAP_USD` are read at each request, not at
  startup. A non-number there gives a `NaN` render cap. The ledgers refuse a hold that is not
  finite, but nothing refuses the setting at startup. Moving them onto this parser is a separate
  increment.
- **The snapshot and archive scripts name `HV_MONTHLY_BUDGET_USD` in their refusal** even when the
  value came from `--monthly-cap`.
