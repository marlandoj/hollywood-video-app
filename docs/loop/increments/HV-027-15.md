# HV-027-15 — A finished film could not be delivered with its captions in the picture

epic: HV-027
release: 2 "Voice and crew depth"
gate: none new
spend_usd: 0 — ffmpeg and libass only. No provider is called and nothing is generated.

## Why

Release 2's delivery slice is "9:16 and 1:1 reframes, burned subtitles, SDH, mezzanine". The reframes
and the mezzanine are built. Burned subtitles are not, so a film could not be delivered to:

- a feed that autoplays without sound;
- a platform that drops sidecar files;
- a screen in a lobby.

Every picture edit and assembly already seals the captions it was completed with, as
`conform/captions.vtt`. HV-027-01 left one decision open: a burned picture can never be un-burned, so
a burned deliverable must be terminal. That was to be decided deliberately rather than discovered.

## What this increment does

- **Three new kinds**, each with its own idempotency key, file name and offer:
  - `open-captions`, in the master's own frame;
  - `open-captions-9:16`, in the 9:16 reframe;
  - `open-captions-1:1`, in the 1:1 reframe.
- **The words are the film's own.**
  - The binding names the sealed `captions.vtt` only when it is byte for byte the track the cut
    derives (`editVtt` / `editAssemblyVtt`), with its cue count.
  - The renderer checks the file's digest, size and cue count again before anything is encoded.
  - Nothing is re-worded or added. Times round outward to centiseconds (start floored, end ceiled).
- **Cropped first, captioned after**, through the reframe's own encode. The encode is refactored out
  as `encodeDeliveryCut`, so a burned cut is held to every check a plain cut is.
- **Dialogue cannot restyle the burn.** Braces and backslashes in a caption are made inert before
  libass sees them.
- **The burn checks itself** (HV-027-06 style).
  - Each sampled cue is drawn alone on a black frame of the delivered size and its ink box recorded.
  - A cue with no ink, or with ink touching the frame's edge, refuses the render and quotes the
    caption.
  - The check is retained as `deliveryOutput.captions` and re-validated with the output.
  - The creator's view shows cues, cues checked, and cues too short to land on a frame.
- **Nothing else changes.** A binding without a track keeps its old revision, so existing
  deliverables re-derive unchanged. Permission (HV-027-14), retention, `requestedUsd: 0`, the
  artifact link and the listing are the existing kind-agnostic paths.
- **`docs/DELIVERY-CAPTIONS.md`**, plus the "not built" sections of the reframe, mezzanine and job
  docs.

## Acceptance criteria

1. **Offers.** A captioned 640×360 film answers every burned kind:
   - `open-captions` and `open-captions-1:1` are offered, with their frame and 2 cues;
   - `open-captions-9:16` is refused with the 256-pixel reason.

   A film whose cut carries no spoken line, or whose track cannot be tied to its cut, is refused by
   name, and the reframes and the mezzanine are unaffected. Old bindings and plans keep their
   revisions.
2. **Delivery.** `open-captions-1:1` is admitted at zero cost, and a second key returns the same job.
   It renders 360×360 H.264/AAC with both cues checked inside the frame, and is listed and served as
   `open-captions-1x1.mp4`.

   The captions are in the picture. Against the plain 1:1 reframe of the same film, the caption band
   differs by more than 10 levels, the rows above it by less than 2, and a frame after the last cue
   by less than 2. The master-frame kind renders at 640×360.
3. **Inert text.** A caption reading `{\an8}TOP \N OF IT` is burned as one line at the bottom, like
   its neighbour. A cue too short for any 30 fps frame is counted in `betweenFrames`, not sampled.
4. **Refusals.** Each of these is refused:
   - a caption wider than the frame, quoting it;
   - a track whose digest is not the plan's, before anything is encoded;
   - a track with the wrong cue count;
   - a retained check that was dropped, names another track, has a box on the edge, has no ink or
     under-samples;
   - a caption check on a plain reframe.
5. **Terminal.** A burned deliverable is refused as an editorial source and as a delivery source.
6. Reverting the source files fails criteria 1 to 4. Separately:
   - reverting the escaping fails 3;
   - dropping the edge check, the track digest check or the output's check validation fails 2 and 4;
   - untying the track from the cut fails 1.

## Known gaps this leaves

- **SDH is not built.** Speaker identification and non-speech cues are the next increment.
- **The font is not pinned.** libass asks fontconfig for DejaVu Sans. The edge check is what stands
  between a different host font and a cut-off caption.
- **Placement is fixed at the bottom.** Nothing moves a caption off a face or on-screen text.
- **The PostgreSQL worker path is not exercised here**, as in HV-027-14. It calls the same
  `renderDeliveryJob`.
