# HV-027-01 — A vertical and a square cut of a finished film

epic: HV-027
release: 2 "Voice and crew depth"
gate: none new
spend_usd: 0 — ffmpeg only. No provider is called and nothing is generated.

## Why

HV-027 has no code, no docs and no increments. Its Release 2 slice is "9:16 and 1:1 reframes, burned
subtitles, SDH, mezzanine", and the reframe is the part that can be built first without deciding
anything irreversible: a cut of a finished master is a new file beside it, and the master is not
touched.

The epic's real shape is a **new job stage** — a finished job's artifact set is sealed three ways
(`editorial.revision` over its file list, the closed inventory in `validateEditOutput`, and
reproduction on restore), so adding a deliverable to a completed job is refused by design, exactly as
it should be. That stage is a large increment: a plan field, an output kind, a validator, a worker, a
checkpoint, an artifact-permission entry and signed URLs. This increment builds the two pieces that
stage will need and that can be proved on their own, the way HV-026-01 built the quality check before
anything called it.

## What this increment does

- **`packages/planner/src/delivery-reframe.ts`** — `DELIVERY_REFRAME_RECIPE` and
  `deliveryReframePlan`: the crop geometry, the refusals, and a plan that re-derives from its own
  source. Two decisions are declared in the recipe because they are the design:
  - **Nothing is scaled.** A cut is a crop, so it is never sharper than the master and never pretends
    to be. A 9:16 cut of a 720-line film is 720 lines tall. A platform wanting 1080×1920 needs a
    1920-line master, which this studio does not make — the editorial timeline is capped at 1080 — so
    the honest answer is a 608×1080 cut, not an upscale dressed up as a delivery.
  - **The sound is the master's own**, copied rather than re-encoded, so a reframe cannot change what
    the film sounds like.
- **`packages/generator/src/delivery-reframe.ts`** — one decode into one cut, on the bounded
  subprocess runner the sound path already uses. It probes the master first and **refuses a plan made
  for different dimensions before encoding anything**, then probes the result and refuses a cut whose
  size, length or soundtrack does not match the plan.
- **Even geometry throughout.** 4:2:0 chroma is shared between pairs of pixels, so the crop width,
  height and both offsets are even; the plan records how far the rounding lands from the exact ratio
  rather than claiming the ratio is exact.
- **`docs/DELIVERY-REFRAMES.md`**, ending in what is not built.

## Acceptance criteria

1. **A cut keeps the master's lines and takes the format's width**, to the nearest even pixel:
   1920×1080 gives 608×1080 and 1080×1080; 1280×720 gives 406×720 and 720×720; a master already
   narrower than the format keeps its own columns; and no cut is ever taller than its master.
2. **The frame can be placed across the master** from 0 to 10000, clamped inside it, and its offsets
   are always even. A plan re-derives from its own source, so an edited plan is refused.
3. **A master that cannot carry the format is refused, and says what it would have been**: an
   animatic's 9:16 cut would be 202 pixels wide, under the 256-pixel minimum. An unknown format, a
   master with no duration, fractional dimensions and an out-of-range placement are each refused.
4. **A real master is cut to both formats**, and the delivered cut has the planned dimensions,
   yuv420p at 30 fps, the master's length to within a fifth of a second, and **the master's own
   soundtrack** — same codec, rate, channels, and mean level within 0.1 dB. The cut carries no black
   picture: a crop is not a letterbox.
5. **A plan made for another master is refused before anything is encoded.**
6. **Nothing else changes.** No render path, recipe or frame hash is touched; the planner and
   generator suites pass unchanged.

- Tests: `packages/generator/test/delivery-reframe.test.ts`, which measures both cuts with the
  quality check HV-026-01 built — the first thing to use it.

## Known gaps this leaves, stated rather than implied

- **Nothing calls this yet**, for the sealing reason above. The job stage that carries a deliverable
  is the next increment.
- **The frame is not subject-aware.** FULL-SCOPE asks for shot-aware reframing; this is a placed
  crop, which is the part that can be built without a vision model. A crew persona choosing a
  placement per shot is later work, and the plan already carries the placement it would set.
- **Burned-in subtitles, SDH and audio description are not built**, and there is a decision waiting
  in them: the assembler's existing burn-in marks its output as carrying captions, and editorial,
  dialogue replacement and source admission all refuse such a picture. A burned deliverable must
  therefore be terminal, never admissible as a source, and that should be decided deliberately rather
  than discovered.
- **No mezzanine master, no other ratios, no ladders, no packages.** Those are Release 4's slice of
  this epic, except the mezzanine, which is Release 2's and is not started.
