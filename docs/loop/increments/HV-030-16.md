# HV-030-16 — The stand-in crew wrote a time of day its own continuity report flagged

epic: HV-030
release: 2 "Voice and crew depth"
gate: none new
spend_usd: 0

## Why

`standInPlan` decided a shot was at night by testing `/\bNIGHT\b/i` against the whole heading. The
continuity report reads a heading's time with `continuityHeadingTime`, which looks only at the
segments after " - " and treats MIDNIGHT and NIGHTTIME as night. The two disagreed:

- `EXT. HARBOR - MIDNIGHT` and `INT. APARTMENT - NIGHTTIME` were planned as "day", in soft
  daylight.
- `EXT. NIGHT MARKET - DAY` names a place called the night market, by day, and was planned at night.

As soon as the creator pressed "Plan the film", every shot in those scenes carried a
`time-contradicts-heading` warning. It asked them to fix directions the crew had just written, and
the shots rendered in the wrong light.

## What this increment does

- **The stand-in reads the heading's time the way the continuity report does** (`continuityHeadingTime`).
  Night light and "night" go to headings the report reads as night. Everything else is day, as
  before.

## Acceptance criteria

1. **MIDNIGHT and NIGHTTIME** are planned at night, in night light. With the crew's directions
   applied, the report has no `time-contradicts-heading` finding.
2. **`EXT. NIGHT MARKET - DAY`** is planned by day, in daylight, with no finding.
3. **Plain DAY and NIGHT headings** are planned as before.
4. Reverting `production-plan.ts` fails criteria 1 and 2.

- Tests: `packages/planner/test/crew-standin-heading-time.test.ts`.

## Known gaps this leaves

- **Headings with no time the report can read** (`- CONTINUOUS`, `- LATER`) are planned by day. The
  report makes no finding for them, so nothing contradicts, but a later scene's night is not carried
  forward.
- **The model crew's plan is not corrected.** It is checked by `validateCrewPlan`, not rewritten. If
  the model writes a time against the heading, the report shows the finding, as it should.
