# HV-019-05 — Live storyboards and live finals on private staging

epic: HV-019 (with HV-018 storyboards and HV-017 character consistency)
release: 1 "Studio" (build order step 5 in docs/ROADMAP.md)
gate: G13-202609192200; ADR-0021 (fal is the first paid provider); G1 within the $500 program cap
spend_usd: 15
providers: fal (`image:fal:flux-schnell` for storyboards, `fal:kling-v2.5-turbo-pro` for finals)

## Goal

The studio makes real pictures. The operator switches private staging from mock to fal: first storyboards, then finals. The switch is one command, and it cannot move a cap.

## What this increment does

- **The switch** (`scripts/staging-providers.py`):
  - Three profiles from a fixed table: `mock`, `live-storyboards` and `live-film`.
  - Only the provider lines of the runtime configuration change. A pool override is removed.
  - A live profile is refused without the operator's `FAL_KEY`, and the key's value is never read into configuration or printed.
  - The choice is recorded in `provider-profile.json`.
  - The API and workers are restarted. A worker finishes its job before it stops.
  - Every deploy (provision, cutover, rollback) still writes `mock`.
- **The studio driver** (`scripts/studio-run.ts`): one film through the studio's front door, exactly as the page does it (`createStudioFlow`). Every crew proposal is accepted, the crew's original cast is attested and the rough cut is approved. It reports ids, timings and the film's spend at each approval, and it never touches a provider key.
- **Docs:** `docs/STAGING-LOCAL.md`, "Provider profiles".
- **The live proof, on staging.** One studio film (a reel) goes from pitch to final with `live-film`, through the studio's three approvals, then staging returns to `mock`.
  - It is recorded in `docs/evidence/release-1/live-reel.json`: job ids, the providers used, the cost-ledger totals, and the character-consistency notes from the stills and shots.
  - Budget: at most $15, inside the film limit ($40, HV-019-04) and the monthly $500.

## What this increment does not do

- **It adds no provider, key or account.** fal and `FAL_KEY` exist since ADR-0021 and HV-018.
- **It keeps live generation off for end users (ADR-0020).** Staging is private.
- **It does not choose models for the creator.** The profile is the operator's, and the crew never picks a provider.

## Acceptance criteria

1. **Deploys stay mock.**
   - Every deploy writes the `mock` profile.
   - Test: `scripts/test_storage_deployment.py` `ProviderProfileTests.test_every_deploy_writes_the_mock_profile`.
2. **A live profile needs the key.**
   - It is refused without the key, or with a truncated one.
   - Nothing is written when it is refused.
   - Test: `test_a_live_profile_needs_the_operators_fal_key_and_changes_nothing_without_it`.
3. **Only the provider lines change.**
   - Every other line, including every cap, is unchanged.
   - Pool overrides are removed.
   - The file stays mode 600.
   - The key never appears in the configuration or the record.
   - Test: `test_only_provider_lines_change_and_every_cap_is_kept`.
4. **Back to mock, and nothing outside the table.**
   - Returning to mock needs no key.
   - A profile outside the table is refused.
   - Test: `test_back_to_mock_needs_no_key_and_unknown_profiles_are_refused`.
5. **The live reel.**
   - It is recorded in `docs/evidence/release-1/live-reel.json`, which is pushed only after it is checked against the staging cost ledger.

## Gates

- **G1:** within the program cap. `spend_usd: 15` is declared here, and the ledger is checked before and after.
- **G4:** not triggered. No migration, no frozen path, and no budget default raised.

## Result (2026-09-19)

- **The run.** The live reel went from pitch to final through the studio's three approvals with `live-film`. It cost $0.706 against the $15 declared: two FLUX stills at $0.003 each, and two Kling clips at $0.35 each.
- **The ledger.** It read $0 before and $0.706 after, with nothing left held. Staging is back on `mock`.
- **Character consistency failed.** NORA changed sex between the storyboard and the final. TEO, a child, was cast as an adult. The final did not start from the approved still.
- **Length.** The final is 3.5 s from 10 billed seconds.
- The evidence is in `docs/evidence/release-1/live-reel.json`. The fixes are the next increment, HV-017-05.
