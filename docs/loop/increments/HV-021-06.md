# HV-021-06 — The continuity repair spread a day/night contradiction while its note said nothing was proposed

epic: HV-021
release: 2 "Voice and crew depth"
gate: none new
spend_usd: 0

## Why

The Supervisor's one-click repair holds each look field to the first shot in the scene that states
it. It also writes a note for a scene directed against its own heading's time: *"Either the heading
or the direction is wrong and only you can say which, so nothing is proposed for it."*

The edit and the note disagreed. Take `INT. KITCHEN - DAY`, with shot 1 directed "night" and shot 2
directed "day":

- The repair proposed turning shot 2 to `night`, because that was the first shot's look.
- The note beneath it said nothing was proposed.
- After the repair was accepted, the shots contradicting the heading went from `["s1"]` to
  `["s1","s2"]`. The one-click repair made the continuity report worse, while telling the creator
  it hadn't touched that scene.

## What this increment does

- **A scene with a `time-contradicts-heading` finding gets no time-of-day edit.** Its note already
  said so, and the choice between heading and direction stays with the creator.
- **The scene's other look fields are still held**: key, fill and back light, and motivated sources.
- **A scene whose times all agree with the heading is still repaired as before.**

## Acceptance criteria

1. **A scene directed against its heading's time gets no time-of-day edit.** Its note is present and
   now true. Applying the repair leaves the same shots contradicting the heading as before.
2. **That scene's key light is still held to its first shot.**
3. **A scene with "morning" and "afternoon" under a DAY heading is repaired as before**, to "morning".
4. Reverting `continuity-repair.ts` fails criteria 1 and 2.

- Tests: `packages/planner/test/continuity-repair-heading.test.ts`. The planner and API continuity
  suites pass (23 / 0).

## Known gaps this leaves

- **Proposing the heading's time isn't done here.** When the first shot agrees with the heading and a
  later one doesn't, an edit to the heading's time would fix the contradiction. It would also be the
  Supervisor choosing the heading over the direction, which the note says it doesn't do.
