# HV-017-10 — The two paths a locked look had not been taught about

epic: HV-017
release: 2 "Voice and crew depth"
gate: none new
spend_usd: 0

## Why

HV-017-09 put the locked look on the cast record so it would inherit the cast's versioning, history,
restore and validation. That was the right home, and it means every path that writes a cast record
now meets the lock's validator. A critic pass found two that had not been taught what to do when they
met it.

**An actor share of a locked character could never be imported.** The share carries the character
record, and `copiedActorReferences` gives every copy a fresh identity in the destination project — so
a lock naming the *source* project's asset ids can never be satisfied there. The import threw
`"Lock this character's look to images the character already retains."` A share is immutable and
revision-hashed for its whole life, so **every recipient failed identically for as long as the share
lived**, and the owner got no signal at the mint.

This is the defect class `actor-library.ts` already documents as having happened twice, in its own
comments: *"A share that mints is importable by construction, rather than by two pieces of code
agreeing."* HV-017-09 reintroduced it one field over.

**Adopting sheet views over a locked look failed anonymously.** `addCharacterReferences` with
`replaceExisting` dropped the character's images and let the snapshot validator throw — the same
message, which names no character and says nothing about unlocking. HV-017-09's doc claims a removal
"is refused, naming the character and saying what to do"; that was true of one path and not this one.

## What this increment does

- **An imported actor gets no lock.** `importedActor` drops `referenceLock` beside the voice and the
  scene-bound intent it already drops, with the reason in the comment: the copies are new images with
  new identities, so a carried lock could never be satisfied. The destination locks its own look from
  its own images.
- **Replacing the images a lock names is refused by name**, in `addCharacterReferences`, with the
  same shape of message the removal path uses: *"Replacing these images would break SPUD's locked
  look. Unlock the look, or lock it to other images, before replacing them."*

## Acceptance criteria

1. **A share of a locked character imports.** The imported record carries the copies and no lock, it
   round-trips through the snapshot validator, and the destination can lock its own look from its own
   images.
2. **Adopting over a locked look is refused by name**, before anything is written.
3. **Adding beside the locked images still works**, and the lock still names exactly what it named.
4. **Unlocking allows the replacement**, so the creator's decision is the thing that unblocks it.
5. **Nothing else changes.** The reference-lock, casting and actor-library suites pass otherwise
   unchanged.

- Tests: `packages/planner/test/reference-lock.test.ts` (1),
  `packages/api/test/reference-lock-route.test.ts` (2–4).

## Known gaps this leaves

- **Nothing warns at the mint** that a shared character's lock will not travel. The share is valid
  and importable now, which is what matters, but an owner who locked a look may expect it to carry.
  *Closed by HV-017-15: the lock now travels, rebuilt over the copies, so there is nothing to warn about.*
- The remaining write paths were traced and are sound: a record stored before HV-017-09 has no lock
  and none is invented for it; restoring a cast version restores the lock and the images together;
  and the archive import path goes through the same snapshot validator as everything else.
