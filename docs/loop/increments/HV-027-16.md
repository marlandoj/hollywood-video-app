# HV-027-16 — A finished film could not be delivered with captions for the deaf and hard of hearing

epic: HV-027
release: 2 "Voice and crew depth"
gate: none new
spend_usd: 0 — ffmpeg only. No provider is called, nothing is transcribed or generated.

## Why

SDH is the last open part of Release 2's delivery slice ("…burned subtitles, SDH, mezzanine").
HV-027-15 delivers the film's captions burned into the picture. SDH also needs two other things:

- **who is speaking;**
- **the sounds that carry the story**, which a hearing viewer gets from the soundtrack and a caption
  reader does not.

The pipeline already knows both, and nothing delivered them. The film's own captions open each
speech with the speaker's name. A reviewed sound session names every recording it places, with its
role, its level and its time.

## What this increment does

- **A new kind, `sdh`,** delivered as `sdh.mp4`. It is the master's own picture and sound,
  stream-copied, with one `mov_text` track marked hearing-impaired and captions, handler `SDH`.
- **Dialogue** is the film's tied sealed caption track, word for word, with speakers as the captions
  carry them.
- **Sounds** come from `editSoundCues` / `editAssemblySoundCues`. These list the sound-mix cues the
  cut plays:
  - through a mix clip or the cue's own stem;
  - at a heard level (clip gain plus cue gain) above the -60 dB floor;
  - only for the stretch the cut keeps, on the captions' own clock (plain, retimed and assembly
    ranges).

  Each is labelled `[music: label]`, `[ambience: label]` or `[label]` from the recording's own label.
  Every label passes `checkPrompt`, each alone and all together, and a refused one is named.
  They are read when the deliverable is bound (`binding.sounds`), re-checked on validation, and absent
  from older bindings. That refuses only `sdh` for those.
- **Overlap.** Overlapping cues are cut into segments that show every active cue, one per line. MP4
  timed text shows one sample at a time; measured, a music cue under dialogue was cut off.
- **Frame edges.** Sound starts and ends are both floored to the millisecond, and cue edges under
  2 ms apart are snapped to the earlier one. No 30 fps frame then shows a sound or line that has
  ended beside the one that starts on it.
- **Proof** (HV-027-06 style), retained as `deliveryOutput.sdh` and re-validated with the output:
  - the track is read back out of the delivered file and must equal what was written;
  - picture and sound packets must hash as the master's;
  - text holding a brace or a backslash is refused by name before anything is written, because
    ffmpeg's ASS path alters it.
- **Offer and view.** The offer carries the dialogue and sound counts, and the view carries
  `{dialogue, sounds, segments}`. Idempotency, permission, retention and zero cost are the existing
  kind-agnostic paths.
- **Docs.** An SDH section is added to `docs/DELIVERY-CAPTIONS.md`, and the reframe, mezzanine and
  job docs are updated.

## Acceptance criteria

1. **Where a sound is described.** A placed sound is described where the cut plays it, by its own
   label:
   - after a 1.5 s trim, the rain starts the film and the door moves with the picture;
   - a cue at the floor, or a mix clip at the floor, is not described, and neither is a cue whose
     clip and cue levels sum to the floor or below (-40 dB + -40 dB);
   - through stems, a sound is heard only on its own lane;
   - an assembly keeps only what its ranges keep.
2. **What the plan accepts.** An SDH plan is offered from the tied captions and the read sounds. It
   is refused by name:
   - with nothing to caption;
   - without a tied track;
   - from a binding whose sounds were never read, and there only `sdh` is refused.

   Malformed sound lists are refused. A retained check that does not read back, or whose streams are
   not the master's, is refused. A sound label the prompt check refuses (`Pokemon theme`) refuses
   `sdh` by name, and only `sdh`.
3. **End to end.** A dubbed film with a reviewed "Garden birdsong" cue, cut in a picture edit, is
   offered `sdh` with 2 lines and 1 sound. It is made at zero cost, and a second key returns the same
   job.

   Read back out of the delivered file independently, the track is five segments: both lines with
   `SPUD:`, and `[ambience: Garden birdsong]` from 1.000 s to 3.000 s under them. The picture and
   sound packets are the master's. It is listed, served as `sdh.mp4`, and terminal.
4. **Refusals before writing.** Text MP4 timed text would alter is refused by name before anything is
   written, and so is a track that is not the plan's.
5. **Frame edges** (review). At every frame boundary from 1 to 29, a sound ending there and the next
   starting there share one millisecond edge. A line ending on frame 1 and a sound starting on it are
   never shown together on frame 1.
6. Reverting the source files fails criteria 1 to 5. Separately:
   - dropping segmentation fails 3;
   - dropping the floor or the lane rule fails 1, and comparing the two gains separately again fails
     the summed-level case of 1;
   - dropping the brace refusal fails 4;
   - dropping the stream proof fails 2 and 3;
   - dropping the label check fails 2;
   - ceiling sound ends again, or snapping only edges under 1 ms, fails 5.

- Tests: `packages/planner/test/delivery-sdh.test.ts`, `packages/generator/test/delivery-sdh.test.ts`,
  `packages/api/test/delivery-sdh.test.ts`. The HV-027-15 planner tests and
  `delivery-jobs.test.ts` / `delivery-read-files.test.ts` are extended for the new kind.

## Known gaps this leaves

- **No language tag.** The track is `und`: the studio's codes are two-letter, MP4 takes three, and no
  mapping is carried.
- **Some sounds are not described.** A sound that was never a sound-mix cue (a picture's own native
  audio) is not described. A continuation cue of a long speech does not repeat its speaker.
- **Text with a brace or a backslash is refused**, not carried.
- **The PostgreSQL worker path is not exercised here**, as in HV-027-14.
