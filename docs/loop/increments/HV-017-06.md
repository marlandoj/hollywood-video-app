## Why

In the live reel, NORA changed between the storyboard and the final, because the final was independent text-to-video.

## What this increment does

- **The plan route** answers `finalAnchors` when the configured final pool has a native first-frame provider.
- **The studio flow** (`packages/frontend/src/studio.js`, `approveLook`), when `finalAnchors` is set:
  - Pins each still of the rough cut as its shot's first frame, through the existing anchor upload and direction save.
  - Pins only shots the crew directed, that nobody anchored, and whose source is unchanged.
  - Re-cuts the rough cut from the pinned stills (the free `anchor-storyboard` presenter). That is what the creator approves.
- **The page and the studio driver** read stills through the signed artifact URL.
- **Docs:** `docs/CREW.md`.

## Acceptance criteria

1. **The client sequence.**
   - Direction view, then upload, then save, then a second rough cut.
   - Only the crew-directed, unanchored shot of this rough cut is pinned.
   - The final is made from the second rough cut.
   - Test: `packages/frontend/test/studio.test.js`.
2. **End to end over HTTP**, with a real server, the worker and a closed fal fixture:
   - The pinned still is a `shot-anchor` image, and the direction carries it at frame 0 with `fallback: "stop"`.
   - The second rough cut uses the anchor presenter with no provider traffic.
   - The one final submission's `start_image_url` is exactly the pinned still.
   - The shot is paced to the keyframes model's 3 s floor.
   - Test: `packages/api/test/studio-anchors.test.ts`.
3. **A text-to-video final pool** answers `finalAnchors: false`, and nothing is pinned.
   - Test: `packages/api/test/crew-plan-route.test.ts`.
