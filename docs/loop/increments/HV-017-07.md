## Goal

Re-run the live reel that failed consistency (`docs/evidence/release-1/live-reel.json`), now with HV-017-05 casting and pacing and HV-017-06 anchoring, and record whether the storyboard and the final agree.

## What this increment does

- **A new profile**, `live-film-anchored`, in `scripts/staging-providers.py`:
  - the rough cut on FLUX stills;
  - the final on Kling O3 keyframes, starting from the pinned still;
  - Kling 2.5 for any shot without a pinned still.
- **The studio driver** reads stills through the signed artifact URL, as the page does, and reports whether stills were pinned.
- **The live run** of the same lighthouse script through the studio flow, recorded in `docs/evidence/release-1/live-reel-anchored.json`. Staging then returns to `mock`.

## Acceptance criteria

1. **The profile.**
   - Keyframes are primary, Kling 2.5 is secondary and the stills are FLUX.
   - The caps are unchanged.
   - Test: `scripts/test_storage_deployment.py` `test_the_anchored_profile_starts_finals_from_a_frame_and_keeps_a_text_fallback`.
2. **The live run.**
   - It completes through the three approvals.
   - Its cost is checked against the staging ledger, and must be under $5.
   - It is recorded with the stills and final frames compared.

## Result (2026-09-20)

- **The run.** The lighthouse reel completed through the three approvals with `live-film-anchored`.
  - Both stills were pinned, and the rough cut was re-cut from them at $0.
  - The final ran on two Kling O3 keyframe clips at $0.42 each.
  - The film's spend was $0.846.
- **A first attempt stopped** ($0.006) because of an edge defect that pooled connections, which HV-032-05 fixes. The run used that fix beside the live edge.
- **The ledger** read $0.706 before and $1.558 after, with nothing held. Staging is back on `mock`.
- **Storyboard to final is now consistent.** NORA is the same older woman in both, and the cliff shot keeps its framing and light. The final is 9.5 s, where it was 3.5 s.
- **Still open.** Identity is not locked across shots. TEO's age, which the script never states, was the image model's choice.
- The evidence is in `docs/evidence/release-1/live-reel-anchored.json`.
