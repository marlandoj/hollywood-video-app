# HV-030-14 — The crew's cast passed its gate alone and was refused beside the scene it was cast for

epic: HV-030
release: 2 "Voice and crew depth"
gate: none new
spend_usd: 0

## Why

The stand-in crew checks what it writes beside the script's action. The render prompt puts the cast
and the action together, and `standInCast` exists so that "the cast can never be what turns a scene
into a refusal". The model's plan got no such check: each field was gated on its own.

Take a scene that reads "The crowd is close to violence." with this cast from the model:

1. The appearance was "A councillor who can incite a room with a look."
2. It passed `validateCrewPlan` and `crewChanges`, and was saved to the cast.
3. The render prompt that `applyCast` builds from the action and the cast was refused as violent
   incitement, for every shot in the scene. The crew's plan had already been paid for.

The stand-in's own test rejects the same text. Shot direction has the same gap: a performance note
is rendered beside its shot's action, but was gated without it.

## What this increment does

- **Each cast member is gated beside the script's action.** The check covers appearance, age range
  and wardrobe, and follows the stand-in's rule: it applies when the action passes on its own.
- **Each planned shot's direction is gated beside that shot's action.**
- **A plan that fails either check is refused in `validateCrewPlan`**, so `runPlan` falls back to
  the stand-in plan, with the model call recorded once.

## Acceptance criteria

1. **What the defect did, measured.** The script and the appearance each pass alone, and the scene's
   render prompt with that cast is refused as `violent_incitement`.
2. **A cast that would be refused beside its scene is refused at the gate**, and the refusal names
   the character.
3. **A shot's direction that would be refused beside its action is refused at the gate.**
4. **An ordinary plan for the same scene is still used.** A paid plan with the refused cast falls
   back to the stand-in plan with `model_unusable`, and its cost is recorded once.
5. Reverting `production-plan.ts` fails criteria 2–4.

- Tests: `packages/planner/test/crew-plan-beside-script.test.ts`. The crew planner suites and the
  plan route pass.

## Known gaps this leaves

- **The cast is checked against the whole film's action, not only the scenes it appears in.** This
  is stricter than the render requires and the same as the stand-in's rule. A cast refused because of
  another scene falls back to the stand-in and costs no render.
- **The look note** reaches the crew's notes, not a render prompt, so it is still gated alone.
