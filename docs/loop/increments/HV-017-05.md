# HV-017-05 — The crew casts from the script and paces shots to what is billed

epic: HV-017 (character identity), with HV-030 (the crew)
release: 1 "Studio" (build order step 5, follow-up to HV-019-05's live reel)
gate: G13-202609192200
spend_usd: 0 (mock only; the live re-run comes with HV-017-06)

## Why

The live reel (`docs/evidence/release-1/live-reel.json`) had three problems:
- NORA was drawn as a bearded man in the storyboard and as a woman in the final.
- TEO, the grandson, was cast as an adult.
- The final was 3.5 s from 10 billed seconds.

The stand-in cast every character as "As the script describes NAME." with age "adult", and the crew left shots at 2 s against Kling's 5 s minimum clip.

## What this increment does

- **Casting from the script** (`packages/planner/src/crew/introductions.ts`, `standInCast`): see `docs/CREW.md`. It uses the introducing sentence, a fixed table of nearby sex and age words, and kinship vocatives. An unstated age is left unstated.
  - The text must pass the content gate on its own and beside the script's action, and must not name a public figure.
  - Otherwise it falls back to the lead, then to the placeholder.
- **Pacing:**
  - `billedShotTiming` reads the configured final pool.
  - The crew holds each directed shot to the shortest billed clip, or longer when its lines need it (`dialogueSeconds`).
  - With a free pool, timing is unchanged.
  - The plan route passes the shots and timing to `crewChanges`.

## What this increment does not do

- **The final still does not start from the approved storyboard still.** That is HV-017-06: pin the stills as first-frame anchors and route finals to `kling-o3-standard-keyframes`. It needs its own live proof.
- **Identity between shots is not locked.** That needs character references (sheets) and is later HV-017 work.
- **The live model's casting prompt is unchanged.**

## Acceptance criteria

1. **The lighthouse script.**
   - NORA is cast as "An older woman." with the script's sentence and "TEO calls her Grandma.", age "older adult".
   - TEO is "A male character." with an unstated age.
   - Test: `packages/planner/test/crew-introductions.test.ts`.
2. **Cues are read only beside the name.**
   - A possessive or a pronoun before the name belongs to someone else.
   - Conflicting cues leave the attribute unknown.
   - Capitals win over a lookalike word.
   - A kinship word counts only between exactly two speakers.
   - Test: `packages/planner/test/crew-introductions.test.ts`.
3. **The cast passes the validators and reaches the shot prompt.**
   - A public figure is never quoted.
   - The cast is never what makes a scene refused.
   - Test: `packages/planner/test/crew-introductions.test.ts`.
4. **Pacing.**
   - Kling gives 5 s shots, and long lines give 10 s.
   - A free pool keeps today's timing.
   - Tests: `packages/planner/test/crew-introductions.test.ts`, and over HTTP `packages/api/test/crew-plan-route.test.ts`.
