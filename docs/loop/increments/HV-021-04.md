# HV-021-04 — The repair's refusal took the report down with it

epic: HV-021
release: 2 "Voice and crew depth"
gate: none new
spend_usd: 0

## Why

A critic pass over the Continuity Supervisor found two defects, both in what a creator is told.

**A film too drifted to repair lost its report.** `continuityState` computed the proposal eagerly, so
`continuityRepair`'s limit refusal propagated out of the review route as a 400 — and the creator lost
the **continuity report**, which is the diagnostic that would have told them which scenes to fix. The
ceiling is five look fields times one fewer than a scene's packets, and a direction snapshot carries
up to sixty entries, so a sixty-shot film drifting in every field reaches **295 edits against a limit
of 240**: reachable on the plan the route itself offers, not a contrived case.

The refusal's own text was wrong too. *"Fix a scene at a time"* is not something a creator can do —
review is all-or-nothing across the film and there is no per-scene repair route.

**The summary said nothing contradicted itself while the report held a warning.** A film whose only
defect is a scene directed against its own heading yields no edits, because this repair deliberately
proposes nothing for it, and `continuityRepairSummary` answered *"Nothing in this film's declared
look contradicts itself."* The note was in the payload; the headline denied it.

## What this increment does

- **The repair is computed beside the report, not instead of it.** `reviewContinuityRepair` answers
  with `report`, `proposal: null` and `unavailable: "<the reason>"`, so the report still reads.
  Accepting refuses with the same reason rather than a message about an empty repair.
- **The refusal says the number and what to do**: how many places the look drifts, the limit, and
  that the report beside it names every scene.
- **The proposal carries `refused`** — the finding codes it saw and would not repair — so the
  summary can tell a scene that contradicts itself from one that merely has not been described.
- **The summary says "nothing contradicts itself" only when nothing does.** A wardrobe nobody stated
  is not a contradiction, and treating it as one would make the check one a creator learns to
  dismiss; a heading contradicted by its own direction is one, and it is named.

## Acceptance criteria

1. **A film too drifted to repair still gets its report.** Over the real route, with a sixty-shot
   plan directed to drift in all five fields: `proposal` is null, `summary` is null, `unavailable`
   names the limit and the report, and the report's warnings and `look-changed` finding are intact.
2. **Accepting refuses with the same reason**, not with a message about an empty repair.
3. **The refusal names the number and the limit**, and does not tell the creator to fix a scene at a
   time.
4. **`refused` names what was seen and not repaired**, and a clean film's is empty of contradictions.
5. **The summary is true of the proposal**: a film with unknowns but no contradiction still reads
   "nothing contradicts itself"; a film directed against its own heading does not.
6. **Nothing else moves**: the continuity, repair and route suites pass otherwise unchanged.

- Tests: `packages/api/test/continuity-repair-route.test.ts` (1–2),
  `packages/planner/test/continuity-repair.test.ts` (3–5), and the existing suites (6).

## Known gaps this leaves

- **240 is still an arbitrary limit.** It was chosen so one accepted repair cannot rewrite a film's
  whole direction in a single call, and nothing has measured what a creator would actually accept in
  one go. A film above it now reads its report and fixes the look by hand; there is no partial or
  per-scene repair, which is the thing that would make the limit not matter.
- **`CONTINUITY_REPAIR_CONTRADICTIONS` has one entry.** `look-changed` is the other warning, and it
  is always repairable, so it never reaches `refused`. If a future finding is a contradiction the
  repair will not resolve, it has to be added here or the summary will understate it again.
- **The summary names finding codes, not sentences.** "time-contradicts-heading" is accurate and not
  something a creator reads comfortably. The notes beside it are in words; the headline is not.
