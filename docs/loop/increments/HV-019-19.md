# HV-019-19 — Every prompt fits its provider's limit before anything is paid (Release 3's live run, G23)

epic: HV-019
release: 3 "Features"
gate: none new
spend_usd: 0

## Why

Release 3's live run (G23, `live-film-referenced`) stopped on "The Tide Clock". Sequence 1's storyboard
rendered (23 stills) and 8 of its 21 finals. Then a final for scene 2 failed:

```
fal GET /fal-ai/kling-video/requests/<id> failed (422): {"detail":[{"type":"string_too_long","loc":["body","prompt"],
"msg":"String should have at most 2500 characters","input":"INT. CLOCK TOWER WORKSHOP - DAWN - CONTINUOUS. ...
```

- A shot's prompt is its heading and action, then the cast direction, the reference map, the shot direction
  and the feature's style bible. Kling O3 reference also appends its own image note ("@Image1 is reference
  image 1." for each image). Nothing bounded the whole.
- fal accepted the submit and refused at result time. The job retried and failed. About $6.50 was spent.
- The mock took any length, so four $0 rehearsals never saw it.

Measured on the rehearsal's own setup (stand-in crew, WREN and OSWIN locked, continuity repair), over all
202 shots on the referenced profile: the longest final prompt is 2,531 characters, and 2,651 with O3's
note. Two prompts are over 2,500 alone and nine once the note is counted. In the longest, the cast direction
of four characters is 1,112 characters, the style bible 737, the shot direction 319, the reference map 210
and the heading and action 153. The live crew's text is longer: there, scene 2 was over.

## What this increment does

- **The limit is declared in the catalogue.** `FalModelSpec.maxPromptChars` is 2,500 on all three Kling
  entries. `falPromptLimit` takes off the image note, so O3 reference takes 2,380 with four images.
  `promptCharLimit(stage, spec, images)` reads it for a pool entry. FLUX Schnell and FLUX.2 edit declare
  none: no limit for them is recorded in this repository, so none is invented and stills are never cut.
- **It is not in the capability snapshot.** No capability or provider-plan revision moves. The limit's effect
  is the shot's fitted prompt, which its input hash already covers.
- **The mock honours it.** The mock video adapter takes what the strictest live fal video model takes,
  note included (`mockVideoPromptLimit`). A rehearsal on the mock fits and refuses the same prompts.
- **Each film shot is fitted before admission** (`packages/planner/src/prompt-fit.ts`). `fitShotPrompts` runs
  last in the film chain, in the same way at admission, in the worker and in shot reuse. A shot's limit is
  the smallest among the pool's providers that take its image count. Cuts go only as far as needed, in this
  order:
  1. the style bible's location line;
  2. the style bible's sound line;
  3. the shot direction's sound and transition intent;
  4. the style bible's tone, lens, palette, lighting and look;
  5. the shot direction's other lines, last first;
  6. the picture performance direction;
  7. each character's scene performance intent;
  8. unlocked characters' cast direction, least prominent first, down to the name;
  9. locked characters' relationships and character arc;
  10. the action, down to its first 240 characters.
- A part is dropped whole or shortened at a word boundary with "…", never mid-word. A block whose lines are
  all gone loses its header.
- **Never cut:** the heading, the cast direction's header, the sentences of a locked character's look
  (appearance through what to preserve), the reference map, and any text the planner can't match exactly to
  a part it built.
- **A shot within its limit is unchanged, byte for byte, with no record.**
- **A fitted shot carries `promptFit`** (`hv-prompt-fit/1`) into its provenance. It holds the limit, the
  original and fitted lengths and sha256s, and each part cut with its length before and after. Both the
  whole prompt and the fitted prompt pass the safety gate.
- **A shot that still doesn't fit is refused at admission** (HTTP 400, `PromptFitError`). The message names
  the locked characters and says what to shorten. Nothing is queued or sent.
- **Adapters refuse locally.** The fal video adapter and the mock throw `PromptLengthError` before any
  request. The router does not fail it over.
- **Reuse.** Capability and provider-plan revisions are unchanged, and every shot within its limit keeps its
  prompt. So sequence 1's 23 stills and its 8 rendered finals keep their input hashes. fal accepted those
  8 finals, so their prompts were within the limit.
- docs/PROVIDER-ROUTING.md has a "Prompt limits" section.

## Acceptance criteria

1. **Declared limits.** All three Kling entries declare 2,500. O3 reference's limit counts its image note
   (2,380 with four). FLUX Schnell, FLUX.2 edit, the stills mock and the anchor storyboard declare none. The
   mock video adapter declares the strictest live video model's limit.
2. **No revision moves.** Every live-film-referenced and mock capability revision equals main's at 8132909.
3. **Local refusal.** The fal adapter refuses a prompt one character over its limit, note counted, with zero
   requests. At the limit exactly, one request goes out. The mock refuses the same prompts.
4. **The fit.** A prompt within its limit is the same object with no record. An over-limit prompt is cut in
   the documented order, at word boundaries. The record accounts for every character removed. The heading,
   the cast header, a locked character's look and the reference map are never cut. A prompt whose parts
   can't be matched is not split by guesswork.
5. **Refusal at admission.** On the referenced profile, a final whose locked character's look is too long
   is refused with HTTP 400 and a message naming her. No job is queued, no Kling request is made and
   nothing is spent.
6. **Live profile end to end.** On the referenced profile, a final over Kling's limit is fitted. Every
   prompt fal receives is within 2,500. The unlocked character's direction is what was cut. The short shot
   is sent exactly as the rough cut was. The stills are sent whole. The provenance records the fit.
7. **The whole feature.** All 202 shots of "The Tide Clock", WREN and OSWIN locked, continuity repair
   applied, are planned per sequence as the worker plans them. Before the fit, at least one prompt is over
   2,500. After it, every final goes through the real fal adapter and fal receives at most 2,500. Every
   shot within its limit is unchanged. The stills are unchanged. The mock rehearsal's finals are fitted too.
8. **Reverting the change fails the new tests.** Restoring the changed sources from main fails
   `prompt-limits.test.ts`, `prompt-fit.test.ts`, `referenced-prompt-limit.test.ts` and
   `feature-prompt-limits.test.ts`.

- Tests: `packages/generator/test/prompt-limits.test.ts` (1, 2, 3; 4 pass),
  `packages/planner/test/prompt-fit.test.ts` (4; 6 pass), `packages/api/test/referenced-prompt-limit.test.ts`
  (5, 6; 2 pass), `packages/api/test/feature-prompt-limits.test.ts` (7; 1 pass), all four for 8.
  Unchanged and passing: the generator suite (90 files), the planner suite (139 files), and
  `mock-profile-locks`, `referenced-profile`, `feature-identity-locks`, `shot-reuse`, `style-bible-route`,
  `casting`, `direction`, `takes`, `sheets`, `feature-finishing-bounded`, `studio-feature-sequences`,

  `c2pa-export`, `generation-revocation` in `packages/queue/test`.

## Known gaps

- **The live crew's text isn't reproduced here.** The tests use the stand-in crew, whose sequence 1 has no
  over-limit shot. The fit doesn't depend on the text. But if the live locked characters' look sentences
  alone don't fit, that shot is refused at admission, at $0, and the operator shortens those notes.
- **The FLUX limits are unknown.** None is invented, so stills are never cut. The 23 live stills, scene 2's
  included, were accepted with the same planner prompts as their finals.
- **2,500 comes from one 422**, on O3 reference. It is applied to all three Kling entries as one family.
- **Characters are counted as JavaScript string length** (UTF-16 units). That is never fewer than the
  characters fal counts, so it can only be stricter.
- **Paths that aren't fitted.** Takes, character sheets, motion studies, living-script and current-film
  renders are not fitted. Their adapters still refuse an over-limit prompt before any request.
- **Resuming the run.** Asking for sequence 1's final again with the studio's default request key returns
  the failed job. A fresh request is needed. That path is not changed here.
- **The full suite was not run locally.** The current-film queue suites run past the local time bound. CI's
  full suite is the merge gate.
