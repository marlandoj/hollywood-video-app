# HV-021-01 — The continuity packet, and the drift a declared film can actually show

epic: HV-021
release: 2 "Voice and crew depth"
gate: none new
spend_usd: 0 — no provider is called. The report is computed from the project's own saved
screenplay, cast and direction.

## Why

HV-021 has no increments and no code. The epic's own name is the risk: FULL-SCOPE P6 asks for
"face/subject similarity to locks, wardrobe and palette match, location match, composition and
screen-direction checks, OCR for accidental text, logo detection" — none of which this studio can do
today. There is no image analysis anywhere in the product, and HV-017's identity **locks** do not
exist yet (`docs/CASTING.md`: reference guidance "does not establish a visual identity lock"). The
only continuity number in the codebase, `continuityScore` in `packages/generator/src/index.ts`,
compares two synthetic clip fingerprints and is honest about it in `docs/SELECTIVE-RENDERING.md`.

So the first increment builds the part that is real today and that everything else in the epic hangs
off: **the packet**. FULL-SCOPE lists it first for that reason —

> "Continuity packet per render: characters present (identity locks, wardrobe state, injuries),
> location state, time, weather, lighting, screen direction, eyelines, props in hand, last approved
> frame." (`docs/FULL-SCOPE.md` P6)

Every field in that sentence that the project already declares is assembled into one record per
shot, and the report states the contradictions **between declarations** — which is a real class of
continuity defect, catchable before a frame is generated, and cheap. A scene headed `- NIGHT` with a
shot directed "morning" is drift the film will show, and nobody has to look at a picture to find it.

`docs/COVERAGE.md` already does this for the 180-degree axis and eyelines, and is the pattern this
follows down to the finding shape. The two do not overlap: coverage checks camera geometry, this
checks time, light, wardrobe and what the render has to keep a face consistent with.

## What this increment does

- **`packages/planner/src/continuity.ts`** — `continuityPacket` (one per shot) and
  `continuityReport` (per scene and per film), in the shape `coverageReport` established:
  `hv-continuity/1`, a `rulesVersion`, the casting and direction revisions it was computed against,
  the source plan hash, the stale shot ids, and per-finding `{code, severity, shotIds, message}`.
- **Six findings**, each derivable from saved declarations alone and nothing else:

  | Code | Severity | What it says |
  |---|---|---|
  | `source-stale` | unknown | Saved direction whose shot source changed. Reviewed before it is trusted. |
  | `look-changed` | warning | Two shots in one scene declare different time of day, key/fill/back light or motivated sources. |
  | `time-contradicts-heading` | warning | A shot's time of day is the opposite family to the scene heading's own (`- DAY` against "night"). |
  | `wardrobe-unstated` | unknown | A character the scene names has no wardrobe for it and no default. Nothing is held constant. |
  | `identity-unanchored` | unknown | A character has no retained reference image, so consistency rests on the written description. |
  | `handoff-absent` | note | Consecutive shots where the later one does not start from a frame anchor (FULL-SCOPE's last-frame handoff). |

- **Comparison counters** (`lookComparisons`, `wardrobeComparisons`, `handoffComparisons`) on every
  scene and on the totals, so an empty finding list can be read for what it is. `docs/COVERAGE.md`
  states the rule this inherits: "Zero warnings is never reported as proof of coverage or
  continuity." A film with nothing declared produces no findings and no comparisons, and the report
  says so in the same breath.
- **The Director's desk carries it.** `GET /api/projects/:id/direction` returns `continuity`
  alongside `coverage`, computed from the same `shots`, cast and direction it already has.
- **`docs/CONTINUITY.md`** — the creator-facing description, ending in the paragraph that states
  what this does not establish.

Nothing else moves. `continuityScore`, `checkContinuity` and `repairLoop` are untouched, so
`packages/benchmarks/baseline.json`'s pinned `continuityAvg` cannot drift.

## Acceptance criteria

1. **The packet carries what the project declares.** For each shot: the characters the scene names,
   each with the wardrobe that resolves for that scene (its own entry, else the default, else
   unstated), what the cast forbids changing, and how many reference images are retained; the look
   fields; the scene heading and number; and the frame anchor the shot starts from, if any.
2. **A contradiction between two declarations is a warning, and a missing declaration is an
   unknown** — never the other way round. `look-changed` and `time-contradicts-heading` are
   warnings; `wardrobe-unstated` and `identity-unanchored` are unknowns; `handoff-absent` is a note.
3. **Every finding names its shots**, and each fires only on its own cause: six focused cases, one
   per code, and a case where a fully declared, self-consistent scene produces no warnings.
4. **No finding is inferred from a picture.** The report is a pure function of the screenplay, the
   casting snapshot and the direction snapshot: the same three inputs produce the same report,
   including its revision, and no provider, clock or file is read.
5. **An empty report is not a pass.** A film with no declared look and no cast reports zero warnings
   *and* zero look and wardrobe comparisons, and the totals carry both, so the absence of warnings
   can be read as the absence of declarations rather than as continuity.
6. **A changed source is not silently reported.** A shot whose source moved under a saved direction
   appears in `staleShotIds` and gets `source-stale`, and its stale settings are not compared.
7. **The desk serves it** through the real route, owner-only, with the casting and direction
   revisions it was computed against.
8. **Nothing else changes.** The coverage, direction, casting and benchmark suites pass unchanged.

- Tests: `packages/planner/test/continuity.test.ts` (1–6), `packages/api/test/continuity-route.test.ts` (7).

## Known gaps this leaves, stated rather than implied

- **No visual continuity of any kind.** Nothing here compares pixels, faces, palettes or locations.
  The drift FULL-SCOPE P6 asks for — similarity to identity locks, wardrobe and palette match, OCR,
  logo detection — needs identity locks (HV-017) and an image model, and stays out of scope until
  both exist. `docs/CONTINUITY.md` says so where a creator will read it.
- **No repair yet.** FULL-SCOPE's one-click corrected re-render spec, and the Continuity Supervisor
  persona that would offer it, are the next increment; this one only reports.
- **Weather, props in hand and injuries are not modelled anywhere in the product**, so the packet
  cannot carry them. They are absent from the record rather than defaulted to something untrue.
- The heading's time family is read from the heading's own trailing token, and only a direct
  day-against-night opposition is reported. "LATER", "CONTINUOUS" and an unparsed heading produce no
  comparison, which is counted as such.
