# HV-027-02 — The mezzanine master, and the proof that it is this film

epic: HV-027
release: 2 "Voice and crew depth"
gate: none new
spend_usd: 0 — ffmpeg only, on files a rendered job already holds. No stream is even encoded.

## Why

HV-027-01 built the reframes and named the mezzanine as the part of this epic's Release 2 slice that
was not started. It is the deliverable that matters to anyone who will take the film somewhere else
and work on it.

The obvious way to build it is wrong, and worth saying out loud because it is what most tools do:
take the delivered `export.mp4` and encode it to ProRes. That file is H.264 at CRF 18, so a ProRes
copy of it is larger, slower to make, and carries exactly as much of the film as the MP4 did —
nothing can put back what the encode threw away. It is a big file wearing a master's clothes, and
this epic already refused that trade once, for scaling: *"an honest 608×1080 cut rather than an
upscale dressed up as a delivery."*

The conform, it turns out, already makes the right thing on its way to the MP4. `EDIT_CONFORM_RECIPE`
has carried `pictureMaster: "ffv1-yuv420p-sequential-parts"` since it was written: a **lossless**
picture master in parts under an ffconcat list, beside a canonical 48 kHz 24-bit mix. `sealEditJob`
inventories the whole conform directory, so both are already retained artifacts of every rendered
job. The mezzanine was sitting there.

## What this increment does

- **The mezzanine is the conform's own two streams, copied.** `-c:v copy -c:a copy` into Matroska,
  metadata stripped. Nothing is encoded, transcoded or re-compressed; the bytes of the picture are
  the bytes the conform made.
- **It proves it.** The conform records a hash of every frame of its picture master as it builds
  them (`EditConformReport.pictureFrames`). The plan carries `contentHash` of that list — sixty-four
  bytes, not a hash per frame — and the render decodes the file it has just written, hashes its
  frames and compares. A single differing frame is refused. The cost of the proof is one decode of
  the file that was just written.
- **The size is known before anything is written**, because both streams are on disk with recorded
  sizes: it is their bytes plus an allowance for the container. A mezzanine may take at most half of
  `EDIT_STORAGE_LIMITS.outputBytes`, because it is retained beside the output it came from and is the
  largest thing in it. A film too long for that is refused with the number.
- **The mix's size is checked, not believed.** A 44-byte header and six bytes per sample of stereo
  48 kHz 24-bit sound for exactly the film's frames is arithmetic; a mix of another length means the
  plan is not describing this conform.
- **`mezzanineSource` reads the plan's inputs off `conform.json` and `timeline.json`**, and refuses
  unless the parts' frames, the timeline's frames and the recorded hash count all agree — three
  numbers that should be the same and were never compared.
- **`scripts/delivery-mezzanine.ts`** is the operator's way to run it today, the same answer
  HV-026-03 gave for the quality check. Exit 0 when the mezzanine was written and proved, 1
  otherwise. There is no middle verdict, because an unproved mezzanine is not one.

### Why it is not ProRes, measured

| | Bytes | Frames identical to the conform's picture |
|---|---|---|
| FFV1 rewrap (this) | 1,539,214 | 90 of 90 |
| ProRes 422 HQ | 4,217,081 | 0 of 90 |
| The delivered H.264 master | 255,786 | 0 of 90 |

ProRes 422 HQ is 4:2:2 and ten-bit; the conform's picture master is 4:2:0 and eight-bit. A wider
codec around a narrower picture costs 2.7× the size and changes every pixel to carry no more
information. If a recipient's suite needs ProRes, that conversion is theirs to make — from a master
that has lost nothing, which is what this increment delivers.

## Acceptance criteria

1. **The mezzanine is the conform's two streams and nothing else**: FFV1 yuv420p 30 fps and
   `pcm_s24le` 48 kHz stereo, the film's dimensions and frame count, its duration within one frame.
2. **Its picture is provably the conform's own.** The frame hashes the render measures equal the
   hashes the conform recorded, and the result carries them. The test also asserts the conform's
   per-part hash lists concatenate to the whole picture master's, because the plan comes from the
   former and the check measures the latter.
3. **A lossy master of the same picture is a different picture**, and smaller: the test encodes one
   and asserts all ninety frames differ. That is the increment's thesis, measured rather than
   claimed.
4. **A conform that is not the one the plan names is refused** — a different picture of the same size
   and length reaches the frame-hash check and fails it; a missing picture master or mix is refused
   by name before anything runs; a plan for more frames than the conform holds is refused at the
   probe.
5. **The plan refuses what a mezzanine cannot honestly be**: odd dimensions, dimensions or a frame
   count outside the timeline's own limits, a malformed frame-hash reference, a mix of the wrong
   length, and a film whose master would not fit beside it.
6. **`mezzanineSource` refuses a conform whose three counts disagree**, naming all three.
7. **Nothing is left behind.** After a refusal the caller's directory holds no probe file and no
   frame list, and no mezzanine.
8. **The operator can run it** and is told, on every run, what the mezzanine does not carry.

- Tests: `packages/generator/test/delivery-mezzanine.test.ts` (1–8).

## Known gaps this leaves, stated rather than implied

- **Nothing calls this**, for the same reason HV-027-01 and HV-026-03 record: the job stage that
  carries a deliverable does not exist. An operator runs it on a rendered job's conform directory.
  That stage is now blocking three increments across two epics and is the thing to build next.
- **A feature-length mezzanine does not fit.** A lossless master of a long film exceeds the budget it
  is allowed and is refused. That is a real limit of this studio's storage, not a policy, and the
  refusal says the number rather than filling a disk.
- **No colour of any kind.** The picture carries exactly the colour the conform gave it. Nothing is
  graded, tagged or transformed, and the illegal luma range HV-026-02 measured in the delivered
  master is carried into the mezzanine unchanged — faithfully, which is the point, but it is still
  there.
- **No timecode, no subtitle track, no stems, no alpha.** Each is listed in the recipe's
  `notCarried` and printed on every run.
- **This is not a package.** No IMF, no DCP, no festival deliverable, no ladder, no other ratio.
  Those are Release 4's slice of this epic.
- **The ProRes comparison was measured on one three-second synthetic fixture.** The ratio will differ
  with content; what will not differ is that the ProRes frames are not the conform's frames.
