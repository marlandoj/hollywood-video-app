# HV-029-15 — The review page could not pin a comment, and the owner's export showed no review

epic: HV-029
release: 2 "Voice and crew depth"
gate: none new
spend_usd: 0

## Why

HV-029-14 added the API for timecoded comments and per-stage approvals. Neither screen used it:

- The review page had two decision buttons and one status line.
- The owner's export showed nothing a reviewer had said or decided. A comment's timecode was only
  a number.

## What this increment does

- **A comment box on the review page** (`#review-comment`, from `review-notes.js`):
  - It stays hidden, and its button stays off, until the cut is on screen and the view says the
    link may decide (`permission: "approve"`).
  - Focusing the text box pauses the cut.
  - The button names the frame on screen ("Comment at 00:00:01:15") and follows the player. The
    frame is read at the click and sent with the trimmed text and this tab's viewer id.
  - One comment is in flight at a time.
  - A pinned comment is listed under the box.
  - A refused comment keeps its words and shows the server's reason. An empty or over-long one is
    not sent.
- **Commenting continues after a decision.** A recorded decision now disables only the two
  decision buttons, not every button on the panel.
- **The owner's reviews** (`#reviews` on the export), loaded whenever an export is shown, after a
  review link is created, and on **Refresh reviews**:
  - One line per stage: rough cut, final, picture edit, sound mix, deliverable. Each gives its
    counts and latest decision, or "no decision yet".
  - Each comment, sorted by frame within its link, has its timecode, "Viewer N:", its text and a
    **Resolve**/**Reopen** button.
  - The timecode moves the player to the middle of that frame. The player then shows exactly that
    frame, as `frameAt(secondsAt(f)) === f` for every frame.
  - The timecode works only when the comment is on the cut the player is showing. On another cut
    it is disabled, and the comment says "(on another cut)".
  - The panel stays hidden while the project has no review links.
- **Text, never markup.** A reviewer's words are set with `textContent`.
- **Frame arithmetic.** The page's frame arithmetic matches the server's timecode. A small
  tolerance keeps 2.3 s on frame 69, where floating point gives 68.999….
- **Served and pinned.** The API serves `/api/review-notes.js`. `frontend-modules.test.ts` pins it
  as a page entry. `contrast.test.ts` counts the two new operable rules, and both clear the
  contrast thresholds.

## Acceptance criteria

1. **Frame-exact.** For every frame in ten minutes at 30 fps:
   - seeking to `secondsAt(f)` shows frame `f`;
   - `timecode(f)` equals the server's `reviewTimecode(f)`.
   `frameAt(2.3)` is 69 and `frameAt(1.5)` is 45.
2. **Only when it can be used.** The comment box is hidden, and its button off, while the view is
   outstanding. Once an approve view answers, both are shown and live. A read link and a refused
   view leave the box hidden, and send nothing.
3. **Pinned where the reviewer stopped.** Focusing the text pauses the player. At 1.5 s the button
   reads "Comment at 00:00:01:15".
   - One click sends `{frame: 45, text}`, trimmed, with the viewer id.
   - A second click in flight sends nothing.
   - On the reply, the comment is listed, the text is cleared and the status names the timecode.
4. **Refusals.** Blank and 501-character comments are not sent. A 422 shows the server's reason,
   keeps the text and enables the button again.
5. **After a decision.** After Approve cut, the decision buttons are off, and a comment can still
   be pinned and sent.
6. **The owner's list.** Resuming a project with an export on screen requests
   `GET /api/projects/:id/reviews` with the bearer token.
   - The five stage lines are shown.
   - Comments are sorted by frame, with the viewer and "(on another cut)" where it applies.
   - Markup in a comment is shown as text.
7. **Jump.** A comment's timecode sets `player.currentTime` to 45.5/30, which is frame 45, and
   focuses the player. A comment on another cut has its timecode disabled and labelled so, and
   does not move the player.
8. **Resolve.** Resolve sends `POST …/review-comments/:id {resolved: true}` with the bearer token,
   and the row shows Reopen and is marked resolved. Reopen sends `{resolved: false}`.
9. **Empty, then refreshed.** A project with no links keeps the panel hidden. Refresh reviews
   fetches again and shows the new comments.
10. **Served.** The page's new module is served. Without the server route,
    `frontend-modules.test.ts` reports `/api/review-notes.js -> 404`.
11. Reverting `index.html` fails criteria 2–9. Removing `review-notes.js` fails them all.
    Reverting the route in `server.ts` fails criterion 10.

- Tests:
  - `packages/frontend/test/review-comments.test.js` runs `index.html`'s own module script through
    `desk-page.js`, which now loads the real `review-notes.js` and accepts module overrides.
  - `packages/api/test/frontend-modules.test.ts` and `packages/frontend/test/contrast.test.ts`
    have updated pins.

## Known gaps this leaves

- **A reviewer sees only this page's comments.** The list shows what this page load pinned. After
  a reload it is empty, because the review view does not return the link's comments (HV-029-14).
- **Jumping to a comment on another cut is disabled, not offered.** The owner has to choose that
  cut in its own panel first.
- **The owner's list is not live.** It loads with the export and on Refresh. It does not poll.
- **No stage filter or unresolved-only view.** The comments list is one flat list per project.
