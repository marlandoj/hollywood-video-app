# HV-016-16 — After waiting for a render, the note still said it was being made

epic: HV-016
release: 2 "Voice and crew depth"
gate: none new
spend_usd: 0

## Why

HV-016-11 let a resumed project wait for the render it left running. `resume` said so above the
step: *"Your final film is still being made — it is running on the server… Wait for it here, or come
back later"*, and the same of the rough cut. `waitForPending` polled the job and moved the creator to
the finished film or to the rough cut, carrying the rest of the state forward, including that note.
So the finished film sat under a line saying it was still being made and telling the creator to
wait. The rough cut sat under a line telling them to wait rather than pitch again.

## What this increment does

- **When the wait ends, the note changes.** It becomes the note a project that had already finished
  that render resumes with: what was not retained, and nothing about waiting. `resumed` is set to
  the step the creator is now on.
- Those two notes are now constants, `RESUMED_FINAL` and `RESUMED_ROUGH_CUT`, so `resume` and
  `waitForPending` show the same words.

## Acceptance criteria

1. **A rough cut** that finishes while the studio waits is shown under the rough cut's own resumed
   note, with no "still being made".
2. **A final** that finishes while the studio waits is shown under the film's own resumed note.
3. Reverting `studio.js` fails criteria 1 and 2.

- Tests: `packages/frontend/test/studio-pending-note.test.js`.

## Known gaps this leaves

- **A render that fails while the studio waits** leaves the "still being made" note above the error.
  `pollJob` throws, and the studio shows the error in its status line. The step and the note stay as
  they were until the creator reloads.
