# HV-031-14 — A line break between two words hid them from the safety gate

epic: HV-031
release: 2 "Voice and crew depth"
gate: none new
spend_usd: 0

## Why

Several rules in `packages/safety/src/index.ts` write the gap between two words as a single space
or as `.`: the brand names (`harry potter`, `star wars`, `dc comics`, `mickey mouse`,
`spider.?man`, `coca.?cola`), `(a|an|the) (real|actual|living|famous) (person|…)`,
`(sitting|current|former|real) (president|…)`, `face.?swap` and `non.?consensual`. `.` never
matches a line break, and a space never matches a tab or a second space. So "Theme from
Harry\nPotter", "a famous\nactor", "the sitting\r\npresident" and "dc  comics" all passed
`checkPrompt`. NEL (`\u0085`) was worse: the HV-031-08 fold deletes it, so "Harry\u0085Potter"
became "harrypotter".

Every caller that joins a request's fields with "\n" before gating it could be beaten the same
way, by ending one field on "Harry" and starting the next on "Potter". Found in the review of
HV-031-13.

## What this increment does

- **`spaceForMatching`** (new, in `packages/safety`) turns every run of Unicode `White_Space`
  into one ASCII space: newline, CR, tab, VT, FF, NEL, no-break space, U+1680, U+2000–U+200A,
  U+2028, U+2029, U+202F, U+205F, U+3000, and runs of any of them.
- **`checkPrompt` reads four texts instead of two:** as written, folded, and each of those with
  whitespace spaced (the folded one is spaced before and after the fold). It refuses if any one
  matches. The spaced texts are added, never substituted, so every old refusal stands. That
  matters: a NEL inside a word ("CS\u0085AM") is caught only because the fold deletes it, and a
  NEL between words only because it is read as a space. Identical texts are read once.
- **The crew joins are left as they are.** With the gate fixed, the plan step's answers, a style
  card's fields and the read-through's tone beside a card refuse a split brand. A test per caller
  shows it.
- `foldForMatching` is unchanged. Safety refusals only grow.
- `docs/CASTING.md` says how the gate reads whitespace.

## Acceptance criteria

1. Each separator (newline, CR, CRLF, tab, VT, FF, NEL, NBSP, U+1680, U+2000–U+200A, U+2028,
   U+2029, U+202F, U+205F, U+3000, and runs of two or more) between the words of a two-word rule
   is refused, with the same category as the single-spaced phrase.
2. Every prompt in `PROHIBITED_PROMPT_BATTERY`, with its spaces swapped for any of those
   separators, is refused with its own category.
3. `spaceForMatching` maps every `White_Space` code point in the BMP, and runs of them, to one
   space. It leaves `\ufeff` to the fold.
4. A shot whose action ends on "Harry" and whose dialogue cue is "POTTER" is refused by
   `checkShot`.
5. The plan step (proposal and reply; tone and question), the style card (tone and look) and the
   read-through (tone and card) each refuse a brand split across two fields that pass alone.
6. Screenplay line breaks and indentation, and benign phrases split by a line break, still pass.
7. Refusals do not shrink. Over the safety tests' corpus (every string literal in the other safety
   tests, the battery, all 185 names in context and with each homoglyph, and the battery laced and
   woven with every invisible and control character), every string the old predicate (as written
   or folded) refused is still refused, with the same category: 4,723 of them. Anything newly
   refused is refused by the old predicate once its whitespace is spaced. No rule's pattern names a
   newline, tab, CR or double space. Dropping the as-written and folded texts and reading only the
   spaced ones would shrink 29 strings, and this test catches that (checked).
8. A 200,000-character text of newlines, tabs and NBSPs is judged in under a second. HV-031-05's
   and HV-031-10's timing tests still pass.
9. Reverting `checkPrompt` fails criteria 1–5 (8 tests in
   `packages/safety/test/whitespace-runs.test.ts` and
   `packages/planner/test/crew-split-across-fields.test.ts`).

- Tests: `packages/safety/test/whitespace-runs.test.ts` (new),
  `packages/planner/test/crew-split-across-fields.test.ts` (new), and the whole `packages/safety`
  suite.

## Known gaps this leaves

- **The other "\n" joins aren't tested one by one.** The generator's storyboard, animatic and
  image calls, motion studies, scene cuts, audio performances, dialogue replacement, SDH cues and
  graphics all go through the same `checkPrompt`, so they are covered by the gate's tests, not
  their own.
- **A word split by a visible character** ("Harry-Potter", "Harry_Potter", "Harry/Potter") is
  still read as written. Only `spider.?man` and `coca.?cola` accept any single character. That is
  a rule-by-rule widening, not a whitespace fix.
- **Still a keyword filter**, as FR-054 says: not a semantic or likeness detector.
