# HV-016-21 — A parenthetical wrapped across two lines was spoken aloud and captioned

epic: HV-016
release: 2 "Voice and crew depth"
gate: none new
spend_usd: 0

## Why

`lineSources` (`packages/planner/src/performances.ts`) turns a speech into the lines the voice engine
says and the speech captions show. It took a line to be a direction only if the whole line was one,
`/^\([^\r\n]*\)$/`. A parenthetical too long for the dialogue column is written in Fountain over two
lines, as it is printed — `(quietly, looking at` / `the door)` — and neither half matches. Both became
spoken lines: the temporary voice read "quietly, looking at" and "the door" aloud, the captions burned
them in, and each took a directable line index and one of the shot's 128 line slots.

## What this increment does

- **A wrapped parenthetical is one direction.** An opener with no other bracket, lines with no bracket,
  and a closer whose only bracket ends it are joined into one cue, held by the lines after it like any
  other parenthetical, and none of them is spoken.
- **The join is in the planner, not the parser.** The parser keeps one entry per physical line, and
  the living script and the edit index bind each entry to its line; joining there would move those
  bindings. Here, only the wrapped lines stop being sources.
- **Anything without that exact shape is read as before,** so every speech that was already read
  correctly keeps its lines, indices and hashes.

## Acceptance criteria

1. **Two lines.** `(quietly, looking at` / `the door)` before `I'm leaving.` leaves one source, line 2,
   cued `(quietly, looking at the door)`.
2. **Three lines, mid-speech.** A three-line wrap between two spoken lines is one cue on the second, and
   no performance's spoken text contains it.
3. **Nothing correct changes.** One-line parentheticals, brackets inside a spoken line, an opener that
   never closes, and an opener followed by another bracket give the same hashes as the old rule.
4. Reverting the source file fails criteria 1–2; criterion 3 compares against the old rule, verbatim,
   and holds either way.

- Tests: `packages/planner/test/wrapped-parenthetical.test.ts`.

## Known gaps this leaves

- **The shot captions outside the speech path** (`captionCues`) still join every dialogue line,
  parentheticals included, one-line ones as much as wrapped ones.
- **A living-script line patch** may still introduce an opener such as `(and so`, which could then join
  with a later line ending `)`. The patch guard refuses only a whole-line parenthetical.
- **A spoken line that really does start with `(` and a later one ending `)`,** with no bracket in
  between, is read as a direction. Fountain reads it the same way.
