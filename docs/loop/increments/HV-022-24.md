# HV-022-24 — The creator approves the studio's lip-sync hold, apart from the film

epic: HV-022
release: 3 "Features"
gate: none new
spend_usd: 0

## Why

HV-022-23 let the studio queue a voiced cut's ready on-camera lines as lip-sync passes, behind
`HV_STUDIO_LIPSYNC`, which is off. Four questions went to the owner, and Kevin answered them on 2026-11-11. In
his words:

> "Price: a flat hold per lip sync pass
> First face point: VEO or fal.ai
> Approval: does the creator approve the lip sync hold apart from the film? YES
> Unreviewed passes: keep reusing their face points? YES .. Ones you marked revise or cutaway already aren't reused."

Three of these answers change nothing that was built, and are recorded here. One changes the studio: until now
it queued lip-sync on the creator's earlier approval of the final, as `voiceFinal` does for the cast's takes.
The rough cut's quote never included the lip-sync hold, so the creator approved money they were not shown.
This increment gives that hold its own approval.

## The owner's decisions (2026-11-11)

HV-022-23's open questions now say they are answered and point here. Its history is not rewritten.

## The design

The creator approves the hold in two places, and each enforces it on its own:

1. **On the server**, `POST /api/projects/:id/lip-sync/:voicedCutId/work-list` needs a third field,
   `holdApprovedUsd`. It must be a number equal, to the micro-dollar, to the list's `newHeldUsd` at the moment of
   the request: the hold this list would take now (`assertStudioLipSyncHoldApproved`).
   - A missing, malformed, negative or different amount is refused with 409 before anything is held. The
     message gives the hold as it stands, for example "The lip-sync hold is now $5.00, not the $10.00 approved.
     Review the current hold before queueing. Nothing is queued or held."
   - `generationApproved: true` and the current `worklistRevision` are still required, and are checked first. A
     body with an approved amount but no generation approval is refused too.

   The list revision covers the lines, the face points and the policy. It does not cover which lines already
   hold a pass, so a list can keep its revision while its hold changes, for example when another run takes a
   line. The amount is what makes the approval the creator's own: an amount they were shown, not just a list
   they were shown.

   With no policy there is no hold to approve, so the answer stays the 503 "nothing can be queued". With a policy
   but no PostgreSQL provider service, the approval is checked first and the answer is then the 503. A request
   that would be refused anyway is still refused for a missing approval.
2. **In the studio**, the run stops after the cast's voices are laid in, on a step of its own, "Your approval:
   the lip-sync hold". It follows the studio's other approval steps (the plan and look, the rough cut, the final):
   a heading the page moves focus to, the film, what is asked, the spend line, and a primary and a secondary
   button. The step shows:
   - the voiced cut;
   - the lines the crew would lip-sync, by speaker and text;
   - the number of passes and the flat hold per pass;
   - the exact total ("Approving holds $10.00 against this film's limit; nothing is queued or held unless you
     approve.");
   - how many other on-camera lines still need a face point.

   The creator then chooses:
   - **Approve the $10.00 lip-sync hold** (`flow.approveLipSync`) queues with `holdApprovedUsd` set to that
     amount, and the run goes on to the score and the titles. If the server refuses the amount because the hold
     changed (a 409 that admitted nothing), the studio reads the list again. It shows the step again with the
     new hold and the server's reason, and asks again. Nothing was held in between.
   - **Keep the voiced cut without lip-sync** (`flow.declineLipSync`) asks for nothing and holds nothing. The
     run goes on with the note "Lip-sync: you kept the voiced cut without lip-sync, so nothing was queued or
     held. The 2 on-camera lines ready for it can be lip-synced later under Direct lip-sync."

   A list with no new passes, an unavailable list (no policy, no PostgreSQL) and a list that cannot be read ask
   the creator nothing. Each leaves a note, as in HV-022-23. A refusal after the approval keeps HV-022-23's
   notes: queued and held in part, or could not be queued, and the film keeps its voiced cut.

**Why stop the run, rather than ask at the rough cut.** The hold is only known once the voices are laid in:
which lines carry an applied take, which shots have a face point on this exact picture, and which lines already
hold a pass. A quote at the rough cut would be a guess. Stopping at the point where the amount is exact means
the creator approves the number the server then enforces.

**`finishFinal` is now two halves.** The voices, then (switched on) the lip-sync step; then `finishVoiced`:
the score, the titles, and a feature's join. With the switch off, `finishFinal` runs the second half straight
away, so the run is as before. A reopened feature finishes its earlier sequences in one loop
(`resumeFeature`), which cannot stop part-way. There `finishFinal(final, {askLipSync: false})` does not ask: it
notes how many lines were ready and at what hold, and that nothing was queued or held.

## What this increment does

- **The approval on the server** (`packages/api/src/lipsync-api.ts`):
  - `holdApprovedUsd` is an accepted field;
  - `assertStudioLipSyncHoldApproved` checks it against `newHeldUsd` in integer micro-dollars;
  - the order is: no policy (503), stale or unapproved list (409), hold approval (409), no PostgreSQL (503),
    then HV-022-23's checks and admissions, unchanged.
- **The approval in the studio** (`packages/frontend/src/studio.js`):
  - the `"lip-sync"` step and its title;
  - `lipSyncSummary`, which states what the creator is asked;
  - `renderLipSync`;
  - `flow.approveLipSync` and `flow.declineLipSync`;
  - `finishVoiced` and `afterLipSync`;
  - `lipSyncReview` and `lipSyncQueue`, which replace `lipSyncFinal`. `lipSyncQueue` always sends the amount it
    shows.
- **The owner's decisions**, in `docs/LIP-SYNC.md` ("The owner's decisions (2026-11-11)") and here.

## What this increment does not do

- No face-point provider. Veo or fal.ai is a later increment, with its own capability revision, pin, key and
  evaluation.
- No change to the work list, the face points, the policy schema, the plan schema, the ledger, the worker, the
  holds arithmetic, the $500 monthly cap, the $40 and $150 film limits, or any budget default. The switch stays
  off by default, and nothing is spent.
- No change to how the film itself is approved. The three approvals of a reel or short, and the 1 + 2 × N of a
  feature, keep their titles. The lip-sync step appears only while the operator's switch is on, and only when
  there is a new hold to approve.
- No change to `packages/safety`, `docs/legal/**`, `docs/adr/**`, `.github/workflows/**`, the capability pins or
  any evidence.

## Acceptance criteria

1. **Off is unchanged.** With the switch off, a voiced cut asks nothing about lip-sync, the run never stops on
   the lip-sync step, and `…/work-list` is still the unknown-route 404.
2. **The server needs the approved amount.**
   - On PostgreSQL, with a list whose new hold is $5, a `POST` is refused with 409 in each of these cases:
     - no `holdApprovedUsd`, where the message asks for the approval;
     - `4.99`, `10`, `0`, `"5"`, `-5` or `null`, where the message gives the hold as it stands ("is now $5.00,
       not the $4.99 approved");
     - an amount without `generationApproved`.
   - In every case there is no reservation and no job.
   - With `5`, the line is admitted once, with its $5 reservation.
   - Two racing requests that both approve $5 admit it once. The other request either reuses the pass (200,
     $0 held) or is refused because the hold is now $0 (409). Their reported holds sum to the one $5
     reservation.
   - Asking again with the old $5 is refused. Asking with $0 admits nothing.
   - After the pass fails, `holdApprovedUsd: 0` admits nothing and claims no hold.
   - On the file store, without PostgreSQL:
     - the approval is checked before the 503: missing, stale, `"5"`, `4.999`, `NaN` and `-5` are each refused
       with 409;
     - `5` and `5.0000001` (the same to the micro-dollar) reach the 503;
     - an unknown field is still 409.
     - With no policy the answer is the 503, with or without an amount.
3. **The studio asks first.** Switched on, the run stops on "Your approval: the lip-sync hold":
   - It has read the list and nothing else: no queue request and no score yet. The step shows two lines, two
     passes at $5.00 each and the exact $10.00, and that one other line needs a face point.
   - A line that already holds a pass is not shown, and a list with no per-pass price shows no `NaN`.
4. **Approve.** One queue request, carrying `holdApprovedUsd: 10`. Then the score, and the note that two lines
   are queued with $10.00 held. Approving again is refused, because the step is gone.
5. **Decline.** No queue request. The run goes on to the score, and the note says the voiced cut was kept
   without lip-sync and nothing was queued or held.
6. **A stale amount.** The server refuses the approved $10 (409, nothing held). The step is shown again with
   the hold as it stands ($5.00) and the server's reason. The next approval sends `holdApprovedUsd: 5`.
7. **Nothing to approve.** A list with no new passes, or no policy, never shows the step.
8. **Refusals after approval** keep HV-022-23's notes:
   - a budget refusal part-way, a 207, a 500 after an admission, a refusal after passes that were only reused;
   - "could not be queued" when nothing was admitted;
   - "nothing new was queued" when everything was reused.

- Tests:
  - `packages/api/test/studio-lipsync.test.ts` (1, 2): the file store, updated;
  - `packages/storage/test/studio-lipsync.test.ts` (2): PostgreSQL. One new case covers the missing,

## Known gaps

- **A reopened feature's earlier sequences are not asked.** `resumeFeature` finishes them in one loop, so the
  hold is noted, not asked for, and nothing is queued or held. The creator can lip-sync those lines under Direct
  lip-sync.
- **A reopened link while the step is open goes back to the final.** The pause lives in this tab's state, as the
  other finishing passes' state does. A reopened project resumes at its final, as before, without the
  voiced-cut finishing.
- **The step's drawing has no DOM test of its own.** The flow, the summary text and the server's checks are
  tested. `studio-focus.test.js`'s guard checks the step's title and its heading, and its place in the render
  table. `renderLipSync` follows `renderRoughCut`'s pattern, but no test drives the stubbed DOM as far as the
  voiced cut.
- **The first studio run still queues nothing.** There is no face point yet, so every on-camera line needs one.
  The Veo or fal.ai increment closes this.
