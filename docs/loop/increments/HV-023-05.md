# HV-023-05 — The joined feature's cut, exported as OTIO and as a CMX 3600 EDL

epic: HV-023
release: 3 "Features"
gate: none new (Release 3 build order step 8, agreed at G20-202610031349)
spend_usd: 0

## Why

Release 3's build order, step 8: "OTIO and CMX 3600 EDL of the feature's cut, read back in a test". Its
part, `HV-023.interchange` (surface `desk-api`), is exercised when "the feature's cut is exported as OTIO
and as a CMX 3600 EDL, and each reads back to the same shots and frames". HV-023-04 wrote the two formats
for a saved editorial sequence and left open that "the feature's cut is not yet a thing to export".
HV-030-30 then made it one: a `feature-film` job joins each sequence's finished film, in order, with a
0.4 s dissolve at each join, an opening title over the start and end credits after the last frame — and
left open that the joined feature "has no OTIO or EDL export … step 8 reads the feature's cut and will
need the join's record or its own cut list". This is that cut list.

## What this increment does

- **`packages/planner/src/feature-interchange.ts`** (`featureInterchangeCut`) reads a finished
  `feature-film` job — its admitted plan (each sequence's film and the final it was made from, copied at
  admission) and its validated export — into one 30 fps timeline for HV-023-04's writers:
  - **Every clip is one shot of one sequence**, in order, named by its final's render record
    (`output.shotRenders`: sequence, final job, shot id and record revision in the OTIO clip's
    `metadata.hv.shot`). Its media is the sequence's film as it was joined (`urn:hv:job:<filmJobId>`,
    after any voice, lip sync or sound mix), and its source range is where the shot sits in that film.
  - **Shots are placed as the worker and the assembler placed them.** Inside a final the worker
    dissolves one shot into the next over 15 frames, or cuts when any shot carries recorded speech
    (`assemble`); that 0.5 s is now one constant, `FINAL_SHOT_CROSSFADE_FRAMES` in
    `packages/planner/src/feature-film.ts`, which the worker passes. Those dissolves are in the
    sequence film's picture already, so neighbouring shots of one film are cut at the middle of each
    (7 + 8 frames) and **not written as dissolves again** — a conform would apply them twice.
  - **Each join is a dissolve**, centred, 6 + 6 frames (`FEATURE_FILM_CROSSFADE_FRAMES`), the only
    dissolves written; a film under 48 frames joins with a cut, as the assembler does.
  - **The end credits are a clip** on picture layer 1 after the last frame, with a hard cut, as the join
    appends them. **The opening title is a clip on picture layer 2** over the start, because the join
    overlays it; the EDL carries layer 1 only, as HV-023-04's does, and `notCarried` says so. Gaps or
    markers were not used: a clip reads back in both formats with its own job id and frames, and a
    marker would carry neither.
  - **Refused by name**, never approximated: a job that isn't a feature's film or isn't finished; a
    final with no shot records (a current-film final keeps none); a shot that doesn't end on a whole
    frame; a shot shorter than the dissolves around it; a plan or record changed since admission; and
    a cut whose length differs from the export's measured length by more than one frame per sequence
    film.
- **`packages/planner/src/edit-interchange.ts`**: `editOtio` and `editCmx3600` take the feature's cut
  (`FeatureInterchangeCut`, schema `hv-feature-interchange/1`) as they take a saved sequence's. The OTIO
  timeline's `metadata.hv` names the feature-film job, its plan revision and its output revision in place
  of the sequence and history; a saved sequence's files are byte for byte as before.
- **`packages/api/src/feature-interchange-api.ts`**:
  `GET /api/projects/:projectId/feature-film/:jobId/interchange/{otio,edl}` on the desk API, behind the
  owner token, mirroring HV-023-04's export: no query, at most two at once, each within 30 s, cancelled
  on close, and the same checks before writing and again after — the project is available; the job is
  this feature's finished, retained join; it is the newest finished join, of the current split and
  screenplay (the split's revision and `stalePlanReason`); every film and graphic it joined is still the
  one admitted and unexpired (`assertFeatureFilmSourcesAvailable`); and every film's cast still permits
  it (`assertOutputPermission`, as the feature's review link checks). A stale or unfinished join is
  refused with 409, a bad request with 400. The response is an `attachment` (`<jobId>.otio` / `.edl`),
  `private, no-store`, `nosniff`, `no-referrer`, with the file's SHA-256 in `x-hv-interchange-sha256`.
  It decodes no media, admits no job and spends nothing.
- **`docs/PICTURE-EDITORIAL.md`**'s interchange section describes the feature's cut and its route.

## Acceptance criteria

1. **The feature's cut reads back from OTIO and from the EDL to every shot of every sequence.** With
   HV-030-30's three-sequence fixture (`test/fixtures/feature-film.ts`, now able to give each final shot
   render records), real sequence films made with ffmpeg from flat-colour shots dissolved as the worker
   dissolves them, and the real assembler's join with an opening title and end credits: both files read
   back, by HV-023-04's independent readers, to the eight shots in order with the source and record
   frames worked by hand (the joins' 6 + 6 dissolves included), the credits after the last frame and the
   title on layer 2; the EDL's events equal the OTIO's hard cuts in CMX terms, and two event lines are
   asserted column for column.
2. **At the joined film's timing.** The cut's length matches the joined export's ffprobe duration and
   decoded frame count within one frame, and the middle frame of every clip in the export shows that
   clip's shot (the title over the start only).
3. **Through the studio.** The owner downloads both files of a mock three-sequence feature made through
   the real front door, API and worker, and every shot of every final's render records reads back in
   order at the frames worked from those records, with two 12-frame dissolves, its length within one
   frame of the joined export's ffprobe duration.
4. **A spoken sequence is placed at its cuts**: a final whose shot carries speech was cut, not
   dissolved, and its shots are placed so.
5. **What cannot be written exactly is refused by name** (see above); and films too short to lend a
   join its dissolve, which the assembler joins with a cut, are written with cuts.
6. **Nobody else can**: no token, another project's token, and this job asked for through another
   project's route are refused; another project's own route is refused; a job that isn't a joined film,
   an unknown format, a query and an extra path segment are refused. No refusal carries the file.
7. **Stale or unfinished is refused**: a join still queued; an older join once a newer one finishes
   (the newer one exports the same shots); and a join whose screenplay changed since.
8. **No token or URL is in either file**: not the owner's token, not the artifact root or a storage
   path, no `://`, no media file name, no expiry. The download changes no job, no artifact and no spend.
9. Two exports at once is the bound; cancellation and close release stalled reads.

## Known gaps

- **The shots are read from records, not measured.** Each shot's frames come from its final's render
  record and the worker's assembly rule; a sequence film's own length is checked only through the
  joined export's recorded length (within one frame per film). A voice, lip-sync or mix version is
  assumed to keep its final's picture timing, as those stages do today.
- **Picture only.** The joined film's sound (each film's mix, crossfaded at the joins) and captions are
  not written; they stay in the export. The credits' silence and the title overlay's alpha are not
  described beyond the clips.
- **The title is in the OTIO only.** The EDL is layer 1, as HV-023-04's; one EDL per layer is not done.
- **Shots are sub-ranges of their sequence's film, not their own originals.** A finishing editor
  relinks each sequence film by job id and gets the joined picture exactly; the shots' own render media
  (with handles) and their 15-frame dissolves as editable transitions are not offered.
- **No button.** The route is the desk API's; the front door does not offer the download yet.
- **Not opened in OpenTimelineIO this time.** HV-023-04's one-off check against OpenTimelineIO 0.18.1
  and its CMX adapter covered the same writers; the feature's files were read back only by the
  repository's own readers.
- **The checks are a copy of the review link's and HV-023-04's**, not shared code with either.
