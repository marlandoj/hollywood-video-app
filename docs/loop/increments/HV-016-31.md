# HV-016-31 — Approving a mixed preview end to end, and what a finished mixed film cannot be used for

release: 2 "Voice and crew depth"

## Why

HV-016-30 turned mixed (V3) films on. Two questions were still open.

- **Approval.** A mixed final runs only on its preview's exact approval. The API's review type
  already accepted a V3 review, but nothing ran the whole local path: the owner's decision is
  recorded through the API's project service, and a final consumes it.
- **Deliveries.** Main's deliveries bind the conform of a picture edit or an assembly. A finished
  mixed film is neither. Asking to deliver from one got "The film this deliverable is made from is
  no longer available." That is not true, and it tells the creator nothing.

This is the last increment of the PR #83 rebuild (HV-016-23 to HV-016-31).

## What this increment does

- **The approval path is exercised, not changed.** `ProjectService.recordCurrentFilmDecision` takes a
  V2 or V3 review; `AnimaticApproval.currentFilmReview` has allowed both since HV-016-28.
  `validateCurrentFilmRuntimePreviewReview` picks the version from the preview's own plan. A new
  local test runs the whole path with real media:
  - the worker renders a mixed preview that keeps one take;
  - the owner's decision is recorded through the API's project service;
  - a final on a changes-requested decision is refused before proof, original copies or dispatch;
  - a final on the approval completes, and its proof names the preview and the approval time.
- **Decision: a finished mixed film is refused by name as a delivery source.**
  - `assertDeliverySourceNotMixed` (`packages/planner/src/delivery-jobs.ts`) runs first in
    `assertDeliverySourcePermission` and `deliveryBindingForJob`, so the delivery route answers 409:
    "A mixed film, one that keeps takes from earlier films, cannot be delivered from yet. A
    deliverable is cut from a picture edit or an assembly, and a mixed film cannot be edited yet."
  - It reads property descriptors only, so a hostile body runs no getter.
  - It refuses the source before the kind is looked at, so HV-027-15's burned-caption kinds
    (`open-captions`, `open-captions-9:16`, `open-captions-1:1`) are refused the same way.
  - Supporting it means making a mixed film an editorial source first. A deliverable's master is
    always a conform, never a raw film.
- **A finished mixed film is refused by name as an editorial source** (`editOriginalJob`).
  The refusal comes before the V2 check, so inspection and binding report "cannot be used as an
  editorial source yet" instead of a bare version mismatch. `provenanceMatches` still accepts only
  `hv-provenance/1.0` (HV-016-26), so dialogue and sound reuse cannot pass a mixed export either.
- **`docs/CURRENT-FILM-MIXED-ASSEMBLY.md`**: the PR's design note, rebuilt for main. It adds how a
  mixed preview is approved, what a mixed film cannot be used for, the Bun result-shape fix and
  measured timings.
- **`docs/CURRENT-FILM-REUSE.md`** now says version three is active: admission, claim, the worker,
  output, origin inventory custody and archive restoration are in place, and a finished mixed film
  is not yet an editorial or delivery source.

## Acceptance criteria

1. **Decision through the API.** An exact V3 review is recorded, and the same decision again replays
   the saved one without appending. A review opened on other output, a V2 review of a mixed preview
   and a wrong token are refused, and nothing is saved.
2. **Refused final.** A final on a changes-requested decision fails with "Retain the exact mixed
   current-film preview decision". No provider is called, and no proof or originals are saved.
3. **Approved final.** A final on the approval completes with every slot generated and costs
   nothing. Its prepared proof names the preview and the approval time and keeps the preview, and
   its media verify independently.
4. **Delivery and editing.** Through the running API server:
   - the job view of the finished mixed final has no plan, checkpoint, originals or proof;
   - `GET`, and `POST /api/projects/:id/deliveries/:jobId` for every delivery kind (including
     HV-027-15's `open-captions` kinds), answer 409 with the mixed-film message;
   - `editOriginalJob` and `inspectEditSource` refuse it by name.

- Tests: `packages/queue/test/current-film-mixed-approval.test.ts` (new),
  `packages/planner/test/current-film-mixed-source-clock.test.ts`,
  `packages/planner/test/delivery-jobs.test.ts`, `packages/api/test/delivery-route.test.ts`,
  `delivery-source-permission.test.ts`, `delivery-admission-source.test.ts`.

## Known gaps this leaves

- **Mixed films as editorial or delivery sources.** This needs a V3 source receipt contract (`/4`),
  inspection and save, and conform and preview integration. Delivery would then follow from the
  edit, as it does for V2.
- **No product surface submits a mixed film or its decision.** The API compiles only V2 plans, and
  no HTTP route records a current-film decision, V2 or V3. The project service is the caller.
- **A mixed final is slow.** Its proof keeps the mixed preview, and every verification of the final
  re-verifies that preview and the preview's own proof. See the timings in
  `docs/CURRENT-FILM-MIXED-ASSEMBLY.md`.
