## Why

PR #393's `graphics-contract` job was cancelled at its 10-minute limit three times while every other
check passed. The time went to installing system packages, not to the tests:

- main, run after #392 merged: the apt download took about 5 s and the test step 4 min 36 s.
- #393, third attempt: the apt download took 4 min 38 s (about 356 kB/s from the runner's mirror), and
  the test step was cut off at 4 min 44 s on its last file, with every test that had run passing.

A slow mirror on the day can push a healthy run past 10 minutes. 15 leaves room for that without
hiding a real hang for long.

## What this increment does

- `.github/workflows/ci.yml`: the `graphics-contract` job's `timeout-minutes` goes from 10 to 15.
  Nothing else in the file changes. `telemetry-contract` keeps its 10.
