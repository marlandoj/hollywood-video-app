# HV-016-28 — Custody of a mixed film's originals and proof in PostgreSQL and S3

epic: HV-016
release: 2 "Voice and crew depth"
gate: none new
spend_usd: 0

## Why

PR #83 ("Run mixed films with owned proof and atomic recovery") is being rebuilt on main as small
HV-016 increments. HV-016-27 gave the local (file-backed) studio a way to hold a mixed (V3) film's
originals and proof, and to verify them from owned bytes.

The hosted studio keeps the same custody in PostgreSQL and S3. Every write has to happen in the
same fenced transaction as the job row. Each read has to lock the exact rows it relies on, and a
large or oddly shaped query result must not be trusted as a plain array. That is what the
`sqlResultRows` normaliser from HV-016-23 is for. Admission, claiming and dispatch still refuse V3.

## What this increment does

Ported from PR #83:

- **`packages/storage/src/current-film-proof-context.ts`** resolves a mixed film's proof context
  inside the caller's transaction. It locks the target's preview, and the exact historical jobs and
  complete artifact indexes the proof closure names. It returns detached metadata and never claims
  bytes. It reads rows through `sqlResultRows`.
- **`current-film-mixed-context.ts`** holds the V3 transaction checks.
  `assertCurrentFilmMixedTransaction` locks the carriers of unprepared originals, or else the saved
  preparation and its index. `assertCurrentFilmMixedLockedTransaction` is the held-row check for
  artifact writes. It also reads through `sqlResultRows`.
- **`current-film-context.ts`** sends V3 bodies to the mixed check.
- **`artifacts.ts`:**
  - `held` sends a saved V3 job to the locked mixed check.
  - Only the exact measured current-film MP4, or a declared current-film preview copy, may exceed
    8 GiB, up to 128 GiB. That applies to upload, `fileInfo` and restore alike.
  - New V3 paths: `prepareCurrentFilmProof`, `checkpointCurrentFilmProof`,
    `checkpointCurrentFilmOrigins`, `checkpointCurrentFilmMixed` and
    `completeCurrentFilmMixedExport`. Each re-verifies the owned media inside the held transaction
    and publishes the index and job row atomically.
  - The ordinary `checkpoint` and `publishExport` refuse V3.
  - Importing verifies V3 proof, originals and delivery.
- **`jobs.ts`** gains PostgreSQL `checkpointCurrentFilmOrigins` and `checkpointCurrentFilmProof`, and
  switches idempotency and recorded files to the runtime switch. `enqueueWithin` still runs the
  domain's `enqueue` first, so V3 is still refused.
- **`projects.ts` and the API** use the runtime preview review and recorded files, and
  `AnimaticApproval.currentFilmReview` may be a V3 review.
- **The public job view** (`packages/api/src/server.ts`) strips `currentFilmOrigins` and
  `currentFilmProof`, and the redaction test covers both.
- **The local store** (`packages/queue/src/index.ts`) supports held V3 jobs.
  - `checkpoint` takes a `hv-current-film-checkpoint/3` for a V3 job and refuses a mismatched
    version.
  - `complete` validates output through the runtime switch.
  - `recordRouteDecision` accepts a V3 route only for a prepared, selected fresh slot.
  - `enqueue` and `claimNext` still refuse V3.

Adapted to main:

- **`server.ts`** keeps main's permission-aware `publicJob` and only adds the two stripped fields.
- **`artifacts.ts` import** keeps main's delivery check and its pre-parsed clip manifests (HV-031-09).
  The V3 exemption from the legacy clip manifest is layered on top.
- **The mixed source-clock test** (HV-016-26) now completes its held V3 fixture through the store's
  `complete`, as in the PR, instead of hand-applying completion fields.

Held back, and why:

- **`ledger.ts`.** The PR's hunks switch PostgreSQL admission and dispatch to the runtime switch, so
  they would admit V3. They move to the admission increment (HV-016-30) with
  `current-film-proof-admission.test.ts`.
- **`current-screenplay-snapshots.ts` and `archives.ts`** are snapshot and archive recovery. They
  move to HV-016-29 with `hv-state/16`.

## Acceptance criteria

1. **Proof context.** A real preview and exact complete retained indexes resolve with deterministic
   locks and detached historical context, and with no byte claim. A shared target and source preview
   is retained and locked once, and survives JSON reconstruction. Target evidence is validated before
   any SQL.
2. **Proof context refusals.** A missing original falls back to a complete carrier, and expiry never
   grants permission. A hash cannot stand in for missing HLS, a missing clip manifest, measured native
   roles or exact byte counts. Changes after discovery fail without switching carriers. Getters,
   conflicting same-ID rows and truncated queries are refused before they are read.
3. **Mixed transaction** (PostgreSQL). Admission and unprepared execution lock the real carrier. Only
   complete saved, indexed preparation survives missing old carriers. Locked-row checks use the saved
   preparation and the complete current index. Both modes are validated before custody. The index
   refuses accessors, sparse rows and over-capacity arrays. Withdrawal, expiry and approval order stay
   mandatory. V2 and V3 preview decisions work, and stale or changed ones are refused.
4. **Public view.** An owner's or reviewer's job view never contains `currentFilmOrigins`,
   `currentFilmProof` or their contents.
5. **Held V3 completion.** A held V3 job completes through the local store with a validated mixed
   output (the mixed source-clock test).
6. **V3 is still refused at admission and claim.** The HV-016-24 admission test and the HV-016-27
   held-V3 fixture still pass.

  hosted worker test (`current-film-mixed-worker.test.ts`) needs real PostgreSQL and S3 and lands
  with the worker in HV-016-30, where it runs only in CI.
