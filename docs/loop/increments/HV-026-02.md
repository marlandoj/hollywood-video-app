# HV-026-02 — The first quality check on a film this studio delivered

epic: HV-026
release: 2 "Voice and crew depth"
gate: none new
spend_usd: 0 — nothing is generated. The check read a film that already existed on staging out of
the object store and wrote nothing back.

## Why

HV-026-01 built the check and said plainly that nothing called it yet. A check nobody has run against
a real film is a contract, not a measurement, and this project has twice learned the difference: the
ElevenLabs vendor was proved against fixtures six increments running, and the first live take found a
defect in ninety seconds because a fixture builds its request the way the contract says to.

So this runs it, once, against the titled cut of the first ElevenLabs-voiced film — the same film
`docs/evidence/release-2/elevenlabs-voiced.json` records — and keeps what it found.

## What this increment does

No behaviour changes. This is a measurement and its record:

- **`docs/evidence/hv026-finishing/first-check.json`** holds the whole report, the film it was taken
  from, the release and ffmpeg it was taken with, what each finding was read to mean, what was
  decided about it, and the gaps it leaves.
- **`test/picture-qc-check.test.ts`** holds that record to the code: the recipe revision is recomputed
  from `PICTURE_QC_RECIPE`, and the findings and verdict are **re-derived from the recorded
  measurement**, so the record cannot drift from the check that produced it. It also cross-checks the
  film's own facts against the hand-measured record of the same film.

## What it found

The measurement took 2.3 seconds for a 14-second film.

**It agrees, number for number, with the hand measurement of the same film.** Duration, codecs,
sample rate, channels, byte count, `-29.7 dB` mean and `-3.4 dB` peak — every figure typed into the
Release 2 evidence by hand comes back out of the code. The hand measurement was right; it simply had
nothing behind it. That is the gap HV-026-01 was written to close, and this closes it.

Three warnings, and the third is the one that matters:

1. **Black picture** for 0.87 s between the film and the credits, and for the last 1.5 s of the
   credits card — 2.4 s of a 14 s film with nothing on screen. Expected, and worth stating.
2. **Frozen picture** for four seconds in the middle. Expected on this run: the picture pool was
   mock, and a mock shot is a held frame. On a live render it would mean something else entirely.
3. **Illegal levels.** The delivered master carries luma from **11 to 253**, outside the 16–235 its
   limited-range H.264 signalling promises, so a conforming player will clip both ends. Nothing in
   the picture path converts range: the graphics are generated as full-range RGBA and the conform
   encodes `yuv420p` without an explicit range conversion.

The third is a real delivery defect, found by the first check ever run. **It is recorded, not
fixed**: the fix is a range conversion inside the render path, which is a deterministic filter change
and has to be specified to the same bit-exact standard as every other stage of the conform. That is
its own increment, and it belongs with the grade work rather than smuggled into an evidence pass.

## Acceptance criteria

1. **The record is a report this recipe could have produced.** Its recipe revision is
   `contentHash(PICTURE_QC_RECIPE)`, its `notChecked` list is the recipe's own, and rebuilding the
   report from its recorded measurement reproduces it exactly, findings, verdict and revision.
2. **The record is bound to the film it came from**: the object key carries the source SHA-256, and
   the project and job ids are the ones the Release 2 record names for that film's picture edit.
3. **The check agrees with the hand measurement**, field for field, including both sound levels.
4. **Every finding is read and answered.** The record carries a reading and a decided action per
   finding, in the same order, and the one real defect is named in the known gaps as unfixed rather
   than absorbed into the two expected ones.
5. **Findings carry their evidence**: every black and freeze span has a positive length, the level
   finding names the measured luma, and the frame count sampled is the film's own frames.
6. **The check spent nothing**, and the month's ledger is unchanged in the record it corroborates.

- Tests: `test/picture-qc-check.test.ts`.

## Known gaps this leaves

- **Nothing calls the check in the product yet.** It was run here by hand against a delivered master.
  Where it runs, who sees it, and whether a `review` verdict blocks anything is still the next
  increment, unchanged by this one.
- **The illegal luma range is not fixed**, for the reason above.
- **One film, one host, picture pool on mock.** This is the first check, not a corpus, and it says so.
