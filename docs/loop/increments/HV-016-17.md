# HV-016-17 — An `INT/EXT.` heading was read as action, and its scene folded into the one before

epic: HV-016
release: 2 "Voice and crew depth"
gate: none new
spend_usd: 0

## Why

Fountain lists six scene-heading prefixes: INT, EXT, EST, INT./EXT, INT/EXT and I/E. The parser's
pattern was `/^(INT|EXT|EST|INT\.\/EXT|I\/E)[.\s]/i`. It has `INT./EXT` but not `INT/EXT`, which is
the spelling most screenwriting programs print for a car or a doorway. So `INT/EXT. CAR - NIGHT`
matched nothing and became an action line of the previous scene, with no warning:

- the car scene's action and dialogue were planned and shot under the previous scene's heading;
- the scene count dropped by one;
- a script that opened with such a heading had no first scene at all.

The PDF importer and the edit-script and living-script heading finders carry copies of the same
pattern and lost the same scene. The creative bible's location list strips the prefix with its own
pattern: it filed the car as "INT/EXT. CAR", and "INT./EXT. CAR" as "/EXT. CAR".

## What this increment does

- **Every copy of the heading pattern accepts every prefix Fountain lists,** `INT/EXT` included:
  `packages/parser/src/index.ts`, `pdf.ts`, `final-draft.ts`,
  `packages/planner/src/edit-script-source.ts` and `living-script-document.ts`.
- **The bible's location list strips `INT/EXT` and `INT./EXT` whole.**

## Acceptance criteria

1. **The screenplay parser.** An `INT/EXT.` heading starts its own scene, with its own action and
   dialogue. Every listed prefix starts a scene in either case. `INTERIOR` does not.
2. **The PDF importer.** A PDF whose scene heading is `INT/EXT.` imports that scene.
3. **The creative bible** files `INT/EXT. CAR` and `INT./EXT. BARN` under `CAR` and `BARN`.
4. **Every copy agrees.** Each copy of the heading pattern accepts all six prefixes.
5. Reverting the source files fails criteria 1–4.

- Tests: `packages/parser/test/int-ext-heading.test.ts`.

## Known gaps this leaves

- **The pattern is still copied five times.** The copies sit in two packages, and the planner's
  heading finders run over already-parsed source text. A shared export would be a larger change than
  this fix. The last test holds the copies together in the meantime.
- **`EXT/INT` is not a Fountain prefix** and is still read as action. A writer can force it with a
  leading `.`.
