# HV-025-03 — The Editor titles the film

epic: HV-025 (graphics), with HV-030 (the crew)
release: 1 "Studio" (build order step 7, second part)
gate: G13-202609192200
spend_usd: 0

## Why

The studio's films start on the first shot and stop on the last frame. They have no title and no credits. The credits are where the film says who made it, and that includes an AI crew, synthetic voices and a generated score. The graphics renderer (HV-025-01) and picture editorial can already make both. Nothing in the studio used them, and a creator should not have to fill in a titles form to get them (G13).

## What this increment does

- **`packages/frontend/src/titles.js`** (pure):
  - `filmTitle` takes the Fountain `Title:`, else the logline shortened at a word, else "Untitled".
  - `creditRows` lists the writer from the title page (else "The creator") and every crew persona marked "(AI crew)". It adds the Azure voices row only when they were used, and the Composer row only when the score was mixed.
  - `titlePlans` builds a 4 s title and a 6 s scrolling credits plan. Both are valid for `motionGraphic`. Type is sized from the short side, and text lengths are capped.
  - `titleOperation` builds one timeline insert. The title goes on the layer above the film. The credits ripple in after the film. A stretch of the music stem plays under the credits when the cut is a scored `sound-mix`, and otherwise the credits are silent.
  - `PERSONA_TITLES` now lives here. `studio.js` re-exports it unchanged.
- **The studio flow.** `approveRoughCut` → `titleFinal`, after `scoreFinal`:
  1. `GET /graphics` for `rendering.available`. If the renderer is unavailable, the film is shared untitled with an "Editor:" note.
  2. The two graphics are saved under the fixed ids `crew-title` and `crew-credits`, and saved again only when their plan changes. They render with keys fixed by the saved revision.
  3. The cut and both graphics are inspected as editorial sources. The sequence `crew-titles-<cut id>` is created, or completed if a previous attempt left it.
  4. One edit is applied, and the `picture-edit` is rendered with the fixed key `crew-titles-<cut id>`.

  The titled cut is `state.final` and is what is shared. Any failure keeps the scored cut, with a note.
- **The API.**
  - `GET /api/projects/:id/graphics` reports `rendering: {available, chromeVersion}`, where `available` means `HV_GRAPHICS_CHROME_PATH` is set.
  - `titles.js` is served beside the studio at `/api/studio/titles.js`.
- **`scripts/staging-providers.py --titles chrome --chrome-path PATH | off`.**
  - It checks that the path is an absolute, executable file whose `--version` output contains 152.0.7977.75.
  - It writes only `HV_GRAPHICS_CHROME_PATH` into `runtime-config.sh`, and records the choice in `provider-profile.json`.
  - `--profile` and `--voice` behave as before.
- **Docs:** "Titles" in `docs/CREW.md`, and "Titles runtime" in `docs/STAGING-LOCAL.md`.

## What this increment does not do

- **It does not install the browser.** The staging host already has Chrome Headless Shell 152.0.7977.75 and its libraries. The operator turns titles on with `--titles chrome`.
- **It does not let the creator edit the title or the credits.** The Director's desk (graphics studio and editorial) can still change and re-export them by hand.
- **It does not continue the score seamlessly.** The music under the credits is a stretch of the film's own music stem, taken after the score's opening fade when the film is long enough, and faded out. It is not a new cue.
- **It does not credit the cast by name.** The cast are original characters played by generated pictures and voices, and the voices row says so.

## Acceptance criteria

1. **The title, credits, plans and edit.**
   - The title page, the logline and "Untitled", in that order.
   - The writer, every crew persona, and the voices and score rows only when they were used.
   - Both plans pass `motionGraphic` in landscape, vertical, small and 4K frames, with conservative type.
   - The edit applies to a real timeline: the title over the start, the credits after the film, and the music under them only from a music stem.
   - Test: `packages/frontend/test/titles.test.js`.
2. **The flow.**
   - Without a renderer, the scored cut is shared with the Editor's note.
   - With one, the full route runs in order: graphics saved and rendered, sources inspected, the sequence created, one edit, and the export with its fixed key. The titled cut is shared.
   - A second pass saves and edits nothing new.
   - A failed title render keeps the scored cut and says why.
   - Test: `packages/frontend/test/studio.test.js`.
3. **Availability.** `rendering.available` is false without `HV_GRAPHICS_CHROME_PATH` and true with it. This test always runs.
   - Test: `packages/api/test/graphics.test.ts`.
4. **End to end, with the pinned browser.** Setup: a real server and the worker. Gated on `HV_GRAPHICS_CHROME_PATH`, like the file's other render tests, so CI's graphics job runs it.
   - The studio makes and scores a film while the API has no renderer. It shares the scored cut with the Editor's note.
   - The Editor's real 4 s title and 6 s credits render in the pinned browser, so their text fits.
   - Short copies of them, laid in with the studio's own `titleOperation`, turn the scored `sound-mix` into a `picture-edit`: the title over the start, and the credits after the end with music under them.
   - The export runs the credits' length longer.
   - The review link opens the titled cut.
   - Test: `packages/api/test/graphics.test.ts`, about 2 minutes on a 2-CPU machine.
5. **The whole studio, opt-in.**
   - Setup: the fal fixture and full-length graphics.
   - The studio's own `titleFinal` turns the scored cut into the titled `picture-edit`, and the review link opens it.
   - Its export takes about 15 minutes on the JSON test store, beyond the graphics CI job's 10-minute budget. It therefore also needs `HV_STUDIO_E2E=1`. It passed locally on 2026-09-20.
   - Test: `packages/api/test/graphics.test.ts`.
6. **The staging switch.**
   - The titles setting needs the pinned browser.
   - It touches only its own line and survives profile and voice changes.
   - `off` removes it.
   - Test: `scripts/test_storage_deployment.py` (`test_titles_need_the_pinned_browser_and_touch_only_their_line`).
7. **The module is served beside the studio.**
   - Test: `packages/api/test/frontend-modules.test.ts`.

## Risks

- **A title the bundled Inter font cannot draw** (for example, a script titled in a script Inter does not cover) fails the render. The film is then shared untitled with the Editor's note.
- **The titled export is slow.** Preparing and verifying the edit checks every retained graphic frame, and the checks read the project and the queue each time. The whole-studio test's 6 s film took about 15 minutes on a 2-CPU machine with the JSON store. On the staging host (PostgreSQL), the time has not been measured yet. The creator waits on the last approval for it. If it is too slow there, a lighter path is the next step: for example, shorter graphics or credits rendered straight into the film.
