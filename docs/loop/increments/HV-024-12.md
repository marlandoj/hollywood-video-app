# HV-024-12 — The studio generates ambience beds itself, at no cost

epic: HV-024
release: 2 "Voice and crew depth"
gate: none new
spend_usd: 0

## Why

Release 2's sound slice promises generated music, sound effects and ambience. Music is in review
(HV-024-11). Sound effects from a vendor would need a new gate, because G3 covered music only.

Ambience doesn't need a vendor. Room tone, wind, rain, traffic, surf and crowd murmur are shaped
noise, and FFmpeg can make shaped noise from a fixed seed, as the Composer's score is made from
fixed tables. Until now a film's ambience stem was always silent unless the creator uploaded a
recording.

## What this increment does

- **A catalogue of seven beds.** `packages/planner/src/sound-ambience.ts` defines them: room tone,
  wind, rain, traffic hum, surf, crowd murmur and night air. Each is a fixed seed and FFmpeg filter
  chain under recipe `hv-ambience/1`.
- **A bed for each scene, from its heading.** INT is room tone. The location's words pick surf,
  crowd, traffic, rain or wind. An EXT … NIGHT with no telling word is night air. The creator can
  override any scene with another bed or with none. A scene or preset that doesn't exist is
  refused.
- **The cut's scenes.** They come from the base film's shots (`shot-<scene>-<n>`) and its
  screenplay. Neighbouring scenes with the same bed share one looping cue.
- **The cues sit under the voices.** Each is −6 dB, ducked 6 dB under speech. It fades over one
  second at the film's ends, and over a quarter-second where the bed changes.
- **The renderer.** `packages/generator/src/sound-ambience.ts` renders a seamless 20-second loop
  of 48 kHz stereo 24-bit audio:
  - two seeded noise sources, so the bed is wide;
  - an equal-power crossfade of the tail over the head, so the loop has no click;
  - a sample peak of −18 dBFS, less the preset's trim.
- **The API.** `GET` and `POST /api/projects/<id>/ambience/<cutId>`:
  - `GET` shows each scene's bed.
  - `POST` renders the beds the library doesn't hold yet, saves them with the studio's rights
    record, and answers cues that go straight into the sound-mix session.
  - Nothing is queued, reserved or spent.
- **Honest labelling.** Each bed's credit is "Ambience generated by the studio". Its source names
  the preset, recipe and seed. Every cue sheet that uses a bed carries that credit.
- **Docs.**
  - New: `docs/SOUND-AMBIENCE.md`.
  - `docs/SOUND-SESSIONS.md` links to it.

## Acceptance criteria

1. **The catalogue is fixed and named.** It has seven presets with distinct seeds. Every periodic
   part completes whole cycles in the loop.
2. **A scene's bed comes from its heading.** INT is room tone, EXT … BEACH is surf, a busy place
   is a crowd, a road is traffic, weather is rain, open country is wind, and EXT … NIGHT is night
   air. Words match whole words only.
3. **The creator's choice wins.** An override replaces one scene's bed and "none" silences it. An
   unknown scene or preset is refused.
4. **The cues fit the cut.** Neighbouring scenes with one bed share a cue. The fades fall at the
   film's ends and where the bed changes. The cues are a valid sound session as they stand.
5. **The same seed renders the same bytes.** Rendering twice gives identical files, and a
   different seed gives a different file.
6. **The length is exact.** Each bed is exactly 960,000 frames of canonical 48 kHz stereo 24-bit
   audio, with the canonical header.
7. **Loudness is bounded.** Every bed peaks at −18 dBFS less its trim. Its true peak is at or below
   −17 dBTP, and it measures between −34 and −28 LUFS on FFmpeg's `ebur128`. The loop has no click
   at its seam.
8. **A session with ambience renders.** With a real server, worker and FFmpeg:
   - a two-scene cut gets room tone and surf;
   - the beds are saved once, then reused;
   - the mixed ambience stem carries both beds and dips at the scene boundary;
   - the cue sheet credits "Ambience generated by the studio";
   - the cost is $0.
9. Reverting the ambience modules and the route fails `sound-ambience.test.ts` in planner,
   generator and API.

- Tests: `packages/planner/test/sound-ambience.test.ts` (12),
  `packages/generator/test/sound-ambience.test.ts` (6), `packages/api/test/sound-ambience.test.ts`
  (2). The existing sound suites still pass unchanged.

## Known gaps

- **The crew flow doesn't ask for ambience yet.** `studio.js` mixes the Composer's score in
  `scoreFinal`, which open PR #335 (HV-024-11) is changing. The ambience cues join that session in
  a follow-up after #335 merges.
- **The closing credits don't name the ambience yet.** `titles.js` is also changed by #335. The
  credit is in each bed's rights record and in every cue sheet today.
- **Generated sound effects are not here.** A vendor for them needs its own gate.
- **No one has listened to the beds in a finished film.** They are procedural and modest, like the
  Composer's score. Their loudness is measured, not judged by ear, and it doesn't qualify a film
  against EBU R128 or ATSC A/85.
- **Byte-identity holds for one FFmpeg build.** Another build may differ in the last bits of its
  floating-point filters. The library keeps the rendered bytes, so a delivered version never needs
  them rendered again.
