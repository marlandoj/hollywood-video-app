# HV-017-16 — A creator can lock a character's look from the cast desk

epic: HV-017
release: 2 "Voice and crew depth"
gate: none new
spend_usd: 0

## Why

A creator can lock a character's look to one to four of its own images, in the order the render
numbers them (HV-017-09). Until now that was API-only. HV-017-15 let the cast desk show a locked
look and unlock it, so a creator who unlocked an imported look could not lock it again from the page.
Its Known gaps said so.

Two smaller faults sat beside it:

- **The lock's name and note were never read by the content policy.** `referenceLockRecord` checks
  their length and characters, and nothing else. Every other piece of creator text on the cast
  passes the policy before it is kept. These two did not, and they travel inside an actor share to
  whoever imports it.
- **A locked image still offered "Remove reference N".** Pressing it always failed with a 409.
  A sheet still offered "Replace the current reference set" over a locked look, which the server
  also refuses. The refusal did reach the status line, so this was not silent. But the page offered
  a choice whose only outcome was a refusal.

## What this increment does

- **"Choose a locked look" on the card.** A character with images and no lock gets a disclosure. It
  holds one checkbox per image, each inside its own label with a thumbnail and the words
  "Reference N". The order the creator ticks them in is the render order. Each ticked image says
  where it falls ("Reference 3 · 1st in the look"), and one line gives the whole order ("Render
  order: Reference 3, then Reference 2."). Unticking one closes the gap.
- **A name and an optional note, checked as the server checks them.** The name is required, up to
  120 characters. The note is up to 400. Neither may hold control characters other than tab and line
  breaks. A missing image or name is said in the live status line, and focus goes to the control
  that needs changing. Nothing is sent.
- **"Lock look for <name>" sends the route's own body:** `{expectedVersion, lock: {assetIds, label,
  note}}`, with the ids in the creator's order and the name and note trimmed. On success the card
  shows HV-017-15's *Locked look* line and **Unlock look** button, and focus goes to the character's
  heading.
- **The server reads the name and note against the content policy.** `assertLockTextAllowed` runs
  `checkPrompt` over each field on its own when a creator locks a look. If the two were joined,
  words from both could pair into a refusal that neither earns alone ("Tutorial look" with "bomb
  shelter set"). A refusal is a 400 that names the field, for example *"This look's name names a real
  person or a public figure, who can't be cast. Change the name, then lock the look again. Nothing
  was saved."* A stored lock is not re-judged when it is read, so a policy that grows never makes a
  saved cast unreadable.
- **A lock read again at the border.** A look locked before this increment was stored unread, and
  it could still be shared and imported unread. Following HV-031-05's rule for import text,
  `createActorShare` refuses to share an actor whose locked look's name or note fails the policy.
  The refusal tells the owner to lock the look again with another one. `carriedReferenceLock` reads
  both fields on import, so a share minted before the check brings the actor in unlocked, with a
  `lookNote` that names the field without quoting it: *"The locked look was not carried over: its
  name falls outside the content policy. The actor was imported unlocked; lock its look again from
  its images here."*
- **Refusals reach the status line.** A refusal leaves the form as it was, with the creator's
  choices, and focus returns to **Lock look**. A 409, meaning the cast changed in another session,
  reloads the cast and says so in one sentence: *"The cast changed in another session, so it was
  reloaded and nothing was saved. Review it and try again."* The lock is not sent again.
- **Busy and no double submit.** While the lock is saved every desk control is disabled. A second
  press sends nothing. `aria-busy` marks the desk around its status line through `busy.js`, never
  over it. This applies to every cast save, not only locking.
- **Locked images explain themselves.** An image in the locked look shows "In the locked look.
  Unlock the look first to remove it." instead of a remove button. A sheet over a locked look does
  not offer "Replace the current reference set". It says *"<Name>'s look is locked, so these views
  are added beside its images. Unlock the look first to replace the reference set."*
- All text is set with `textContent`.

## Acceptance criteria

1. **A creator locks a look from the card, in the order they chose.** Ticking references 1, 3 and 2,
   then unticking 1, numbers them 3 then 2. **Lock look** sends exactly
   `{expectedVersion: 3, lock: {assetIds: ["a3","a2"], label, note}}` with the name and note trimmed.
   The card then shows the locked look line and **Unlock look**, and focus is on the character's
   heading.
2. **The form checks before it sends.** No image, a blank name, a name with a control character,
   or a note over 400 characters is said in the live status line. Focus moves to the control at
   fault and nothing is sent. Each checkbox sits inside its own label. A character with no images
   has no lock form.
3. **Server refusals and a stale cast.** A 400 refusal appears in the status line and keeps the form
   and its choices, with focus back on **Lock look**. A 409 reloads the cast (version 5 shown), says
   so in one fixed sentence, does not resend, and the next lock goes out on version 5.
4. **No double submit, and busy around the status line.** While a lock is pending its button is
   disabled and a second press sends nothing. `aria-busy` is set in the desk but not on its status
   line, and is cleared afterwards.
5. **Locked images say to unlock first.** A locked image has no remove button and says why. An image
   outside the look keeps its button, and a locked character has no second lock form.
6. **A sheet does not offer replacement over a locked look**, and says why.
7. **The server refuses a name or note outside the content policy, field by field.** "Taylor Swift on
   tour" as a name and a note naming a brand each get a 400 that names the field. The cast version
   does not move and no lock is saved. "Tutorial look" with "bomb shelter set", each clean alone,
   locks. So does the same image under a clean name.
8. **A look locked before the check does not cross projects unread.** With such a look stored in the
   cast, sharing the actor is refused and the refusal names the field. A share minted before the
   check imports the actor unlocked with a note naming the field. A clean name with a refused note is
   the note's fault.
9. Reverting `casting.js`, `character-sheets.js`, `reference-lock.ts`, `actor-library.ts` and the lock
   route's service method in `packages/api/src/index.ts` fails "a creator locks a character's look from its card, in
   the order they chose the images", "the lock form asks for images and a name before anything is
   sent", "a server refusal reaches the status line, and a stale cast is reloaded without sending
   the lock again", "a lock being saved cannot be sent twice, and the desk is busy around its status
   line", "a locked image says to unlock the look first instead of offering a remove button", "a
   sheet's replace choice is not offered over a locked look, and the sheet says to unlock first" and
   "a locked look whose name or note falls outside the content policy is refused, and nothing is
   saved" and "a look locked before its text was checked is refused at the share and left behind at
   the import".

- Tests: `packages/frontend/test/lock-look.test.js` (1–6), `packages/api/test/reference-lock-route.test.ts`
  (7), `packages/planner/test/actor-library.test.ts` (8). `packages/frontend/test/busy.test.js` now lists `casting.js` among the modules that import
  `busy.js`. `packages/frontend/test/focus-target.test.ts` now lists the locked-look checkbox among the

## Known gaps this leaves

- **The chosen order can only be changed by unticking and ticking again.** There are no move-earlier
  or move-later buttons. With at most four images, that is at most a few clicks.
- **A conflict reload clears the form.** After a 409 the cast is redrawn, so the creator ticks the
  images and types the name again. That is deliberate (the cast may have changed), but it costs
  retyping.
- **A look locked before this increment stays in its own project unread.** It is read when it
  crosses projects (share and import), not when the cast is read. Restoring an older cast version
  brings it back the same way. Re-judging stored records on read would make a saved cast unreadable
  whenever the policy grows, which `characterRecord` deliberately avoids.
- **Thumbnails load when the disclosure opens.** One that fails to load stays blank. Its label still
  names the image.
