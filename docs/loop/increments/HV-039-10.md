# HV-039-10 — Reviewing a character sheet while another generated closed it every five seconds

epic: HV-039
release: 2 "Voice and crew depth"
gate: none new
spend_usd: 0

## Why

**Generate character sheets** in the cast desk lists every sheet for a character. While any of them
is queued or running, `load` runs again every five seconds, and it began by rebuilding every row:

```js
history.replaceChildren();
… details.open=index===0&&job.status==="done"; …
```

Take a creator reviewing an earlier, finished sheet: they have opened it, are ticking "I reviewed
this view" on the views to use, and are heading for **Use selected views as references**. Every five
seconds, for as long as the new sheet takes, three things happened:

- the sheet they were reviewing closed;
- keyboard focus dropped to the page body;
- its images were requested again.

The ticks themselves survived, because they are stored beside the list, but the creator couldn't
see them.

## What this increment does

- **Rows are kept across status checks.** Each row is compared by what it shows: status, reason,
  whether it has output, its views, and its cast version. Signed image links are left out, since
  they change on every request. A row is rebuilt only when that changes.
- **A generating sheet's progress is written into its own row** ("2 of 4 views complete."), and so
  is its summary's status.
- **When one row does change**, it is swapped in place and the others are not touched. A sheet that
  was open stays open.
- **Refresh sheets** still draws every row again, keeping what was open. That is how an expired
  image link gets replaced.

## Acceptance criteria

1. **An earlier sheet being reviewed stays open across a status check.** Its row, its "I reviewed
   this view" checkbox and its "Use selected views as references" button are the same elements, the
   tick stays ticked, and the new sheet's progress reads "2 of 4 views complete."
2. **A status check that changes nothing leaves every row where it is.**
3. **When the new sheet finishes**, it is drawn with its four views, the sheet under review is left
   alone, and nothing is left polling.
4. **Refresh sheets draws every row again** and keeps the open sheet open.
5. Reverting `character-sheets.js` fails criteria 1–3.

- Tests: `packages/frontend/test/character-sheets-poll.test.js`. The five-second check is run by the
  test, and the test DOM gains `replaceWith`.

## Known gaps this leaves

- **A new sheet appearing rebuilds the list**, because the order changed. That happens once, when
  the creator presses Generate.
- **Adopting views** still rebuilds the whole cast list, through the cast desk's `mutate`.
  HV-039-05 moves focus to the character's card after it.
