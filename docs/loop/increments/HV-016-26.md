# HV-016-26 — Assembling a mixed film, with its own provenance and a real assembly time

epic: HV-016
release: 2 "Voice and crew depth"
gate: none new
spend_usd: 0

## Why

PR #83 ("Run mixed films with owned proof and atomic recovery") is being rebuilt on main as small
HV-016 increments. HV-016-25 gave a mixed (V3) film owned copies of the takes it keeps. The next
step is to turn a complete mixed checkpoint into an export. The export has to take the owned bytes
only, keep the originals' native speech, measure its own clock, and write a public provenance record
that says which shots were kept, from which film, and which were fresh.

The PR's assembler dates that record 1970, as every export did before HV-031-02. Main now requires
the caller's assembly time and refuses a placeholder, and the mixed record must do the same.

## What this increment does

Ported from PR #83:

- **Exclusive export directories** (`packages/assembler/src/current-film-output-directory.ts`). An
  export goes into its job directory or a fresh UUID delivery directory under it, so an assembler
  that finishes late cannot overwrite a replacement.
- **Mixed assembly** (`current-film-mixed.ts`, and `assembleCurrentFilmMixedAsync` in `index.ts`).
  Before FFmpeg runs, every owned role is checksummed and the originals' native PCM is checked. The
  kernel gets no cost or render record from the caller, and its measured clock is
  `hv-current-film-clock/3`. `assemble` and `assembleAsync` refuse a `mixed` option, whether it is an
  own or inherited property, so V3 can only use its explicit entry point. The caller's access check
  runs at every yielded step and every streamed hash chunk.
- **Mixed provenance** (`hv-provenance/3.0`). A whitelist of target and original identities, frames,
  and picture and speech revisions. It carries no captures, routes, paths, costs or casting.
- **The mixed source clock** (`packages/planner/src/current-film-mixed-source-clock.ts`), held back
  from HV-016-24. Its test needed the generator's originals copying (HV-016-25) and this
  increment's mixed assembly, and both are now here.

Adapted to main:

- **The caller supplies the assembly time.** `CurrentFilmMixedAssembleOptions.assembledAt` is
  required and goes through `provenanceAssembledAt` before any access check or media read. So a
  placeholder, an impossible date or a missing value is refused before FFmpeg. The same time goes
  into the kernel's `AssembleOptions.assembledAt`, which main requires.
- **Main's credential block.** Credentials come from `provenanceCredentials`, not a hand-written
  claim.
- **Restore is given the time.** `createCurrentFilmMixedProvenance` takes the time the export
  recorded. `validateCurrentFilmMixedProvenance` reads it from the manifest, requires a real
  instant, and rebuilds everything else exactly. The manifest's bytes are pinned by the artifact
  index when a later increment publishes them, not by this reconstruction.
- **Decision: `hv-provenance/3.0` is not accepted where main validates provenance.**
  `provenanceMatches` still accepts only `hv-provenance/1.0`. It guards reusing an export as an
  editorial, dialogue or sound source (`edit-source-media`, `dialogue-replacement`, `sound-media`),
  and those paths verify a V2 or ordinary job's media and shot records. A mixed export has a
  different shot shape and its own reconstruction check, so it cannot pass as a source until an
  increment verifies it as one. A test holds this.
- **The mixed source-clock test** used to finish its fixture through the store's `complete`, which
  still refuses V3. It now checks that refusal, validates the output with
  `validateCurrentFilmMixedOutput`, and applies the same completion fields to its held copy.
- **Test fixtures** pass one fixed real `assembledAt`.
- **Main's provenance census** (`packages/planner/test/provenance.test.ts`) lists
  `current-film-mixed.ts` as an importer of the provenance module. The census still finds the 1.0
  spec, issuer and claim literals only in `provenance.ts`, and no date literal in the assembler.

## Acceptance criteria

1. **Export directories.** An exclusive delivery directory keeps a replacement export when an earlier
   writer finishes late. Foreign owners, nested paths and linked parents are refused.
2. **Boundary.** The legacy entry points refuse an own or inherited `mixed` option. An
   already-cancelled V3 assembly stops before reading caller metadata or files.
3. **Mixed assembly.** Original native speech is kept. Captions match an independently generated
   target, and the clock matches the measured spans. An all-kept final measures each odd 15-frame
   dissolve without restoring the original directories.
4. **Refusals.** Missing or changed owned roles, or an altered original record, are refused before
   assembly. Access is checked before media and after asynchronous steps, and a withdrawn target or
   an abort leaves no export or manifest.
5. **Provenance.** The `hv-provenance/3.0` manifest is a reproducible whitelist with no private keys.
   It carries the caller's `assembledAt` and main's credential block. A placeholder, an impossible
   date or a missing time fails validation, and assembling with the placeholder is refused before any
   access check. `provenanceMatches` rejects the manifest.
6. **Mixed source clock.** Target intent, original records and captures, and owned roles stay
   distinct. Repeated text keeps separate voices and exact native positions. An all-kept silent final
   keeps its 15-frame overlaps. Incomplete output, a changed journal, origin or clock, a changed
   lifetime, and resealed or hostile envelopes are refused.
7. **V3 is still refused at admission and completion.** The admission test from HV-016-24 passes, and
   the store's `complete` refuses a V3 job.

## Known gaps this leaves

- **Nothing calls `assembleCurrentFilmMixedAsync` yet.** The queue worker increment will.
- **A mixed export cannot be used as an editorial source,** because `provenanceMatches` refuses
  3.0. That needs its own verifier.
- **HV-016-27 to HV-016-30:** the queue worker (mixed clips and media, proof and proof media), the
  store's origins and proof checkpoints and V3 admission and completion; storage custody and
  recovery, with the SQL call sites of `sqlResultRows`; the archive package and its verifier; and
  the mixed-assembly doc.
