# HV-019-04 — Quality presets and the per-film spend guard

epic: HV-019
release: 1 "Studio" (build order step 5 in docs/ROADMAP.md)
gate: G13-202609192200 (the studio pivot); G1 unchanged
spend_usd: 0 (no provider is called; every paid pool in the tests is admitted and never run)

## Goal

Before any live generation, one film cannot use the whole month. The creator sees what their film has spent, and what it may still spend, at each approval.

## What this increment does

- **The film limit** (`packages/operator/src/film-budget.ts`):
  - A film's paid generation, spent plus held by its queued jobs, may not pass `HV_FILM_SPEND_CAP_USD`. The default is $40.
  - It is a new restriction. It never raises the monthly cap ($500) or the per-shot cap, and a value above the monthly cap is refused at startup.
- **Admission:**
  - In PostgreSQL, `PostgresCostLedger.admit(projectId, input, monthlyCap, filmCap)` checks the limit inside the same transaction and lock as the monthly cap, before each of its three paid reservations.
  - In the JSON store, the API checks before reserving. `CostLedger.filmSpend` counts only the film's own jobs' holds.
  - A refused render is a 429 `budget_exhausted` with the limit in the message, and it holds nothing.
- **The creator's view:** `GET /api/projects/:projectId/spend` returns `{spentUsd, heldUsd, capUsd}`, owner only and `no-store`. The studio shows it at each of the three approvals.
- **Quality presets:** draft and final are the studio's two render stages. The rough cut uses the `animatic` pool, and the final uses the `final` pool (docs/PROVIDER-ROUTING.md). The creator never picks a provider.

## What this increment does not do

- **No live provider.** Staging stays pinned to mock. Live storyboards and finals are the next step, with their own `spend_usd`.
- **No migration.** `hv_reservations.project_id` and `hv_cost_events.project_id` already exist.
- **Audio and lip-sync ledgers are not counted.** They are free on staging (eSpeak); production voice (step 6) adds them to the film's spend.

## Acceptance criteria

1. **The limit.**
   - Default $40.
   - Refused above the monthly cap, and refused when not positive.
   - Spent plus held plus the request may not pass it.
   - Free requests always pass.
   - Tests: `packages/operator/test/film-budget.test.ts`.
2. **Over HTTP, JSON store.**
   - With a paid storyboard pool, a $5 hold and an $8 limit, the first render is admitted and the second is a 429 `budget_exhausted`. Nothing is held for the refused render.
   - A second film has its own limit.
   - `/spend` reports the hold and is owner only.
   - Tests: `packages/api/test/film-budget-route.test.ts`.
3. **In PostgreSQL.**
   - The same limit is enforced inside admission.
   - The refused job is not stored and holds nothing.
   - Admission without a film limit is unchanged.
   - Another film is unaffected.
   - Tests: `packages/storage/test/film-budget.test.ts` (CI's PostgreSQL job).
4. **The studio.**
   - The spend line is fetched and shown at the plan, look and rough-cut approvals.
   - Tests: `packages/frontend/test/studio.test.js`.

## Gates

- **G1:** not triggered. No spend, and the monthly cap is unchanged. This adds a lower limit.
- **G4:** not triggered. No migration, no frozen path, and no budget default raised.
