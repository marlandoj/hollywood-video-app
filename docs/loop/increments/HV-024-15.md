# HV-024-15 — A feature's score fits in its sound library

epic: HV-024
release: 3 "Features"
gate: none new
spend_usd: 0

## Why

Release 3's live run (G23) made all ten sequences, joined them and shared the feature. But sequence 10
was joined without its score. Its finishing notes say:

- "Composer: generated music was not used (/api/projects/:id/music-cues -> 502 The cue was made and
  charged $0.12, but could not be kept in the sound library (The sound library exceeds its retained
  media limit.)) …"
- "Composer: the score could not be mixed (/api/projects/:id/sounds -> 400 The sound library exceeds its
  retained media limit.); the film is shared without music."

The project's library held 13 sounds totalling 507 MiB:
- nine Composer cues of 96–120 s, each a 16-bit original plus its 24-bit normalized copy, about 50 MiB
  each;
- four 20-second ambience beds.

`MAX_SOUND_LIBRARY_BYTES` was 512 MiB, set before features existed. A 20-minute feature's cues alone
come to about 550 MiB, so the limit cannot hold one feature's score. The mock rehearsals never hit it,
because their music was tiny.

## What this increment does

- **`MAX_SOUND_LIBRARY_BYTES` is 2 GiB.** That holds a feature's ten cues and its beds, with room for
  re-scored sequences. It stays a bound on stored media:
  - a library past it is still refused, with the same words;
  - `MAX_SOUND_ASSETS` (64) and `MAX_SOUND_UPLOAD_BYTES` (128 MiB a sound) are unchanged.
- **`packages/planner/test/sound-library-feature.test.ts`:**
  - a library shaped like the run's (ten 120 s cues and four 20 s beds, over 512 MiB) is admitted and
    validates;
  - seven 600 s cues fit, and the eighth crosses 2 GiB and is refused.

## Acceptance criteria

1. A feature's score (ten 120-second cues and four ambience beds) is kept in one project's sound library.
2. The library still refuses media past its bound: "The sound library exceeds its retained media limit."

- Tests: `packages/planner/test/sound-library-feature.test.ts`.

## Known gaps

- Sequence 10 of the live run is re-scored by `studio-run --resume`. HV-024-16 asks again for its lost cue.
