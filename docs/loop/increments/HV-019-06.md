# HV-019-06 — A render holds what it can spend, not its whole cap

epic: HV-019
release: 1 "Studio"
gate: none new (no cap or default is raised; less money is held)
spend_usd: 0

## Why

In the Release 1 run, the ten-shot short "Paper Boats" was refused at its final with this message: "This film has reached its spending limit of $40.00 ($0.03 spent or held)".

A paid render held its whole cap, `HV_COST_CAP_PER_SHOT_USD` ($5) times the number of shots. That is $50 for ten shots, although the film costs about $4 (ten 5 s Kling clips at $0.42). The per-film limit (HV-019-04) counted that hold, so any film of more than eight shots could never be finished.

## What this increment does

- **The hold** is now the dearest eligible provider's estimate for every new shot, times the three attempts the retry policy allows. It is rounded up to the cent and never more than the cap.
- **The render still cannot spend past its hold.** The ledger's `assertCanSpend` refuses any charge beyond the remaining hold, and the render stops. So the hold stays a hard ceiling, just a truthful one.
- **The per-shot cap, the monthly cap and the film limit are unchanged.**

## Acceptance criteria

1. **The hold is the provider estimate.** A paid storyboard render of two stills holds $0.02 (two stills at $0.003, three attempts each), not its $5 cap.
2. **The film limit still refuses a render past it,** and holds nothing for the refused render.

Test for both: `packages/api/test/film-budget-route.test.ts`.
