# HV-029-14 — A reviewer could not say where in the cut, and the owner never saw a decision or its stage

epic: HV-029
release: 2 "Voice and crew depth"
gate: none new
spend_usd: 0

## Why

Release 2's HV-029 slice is "timecoded comments and per-stage approvals". A review link had three
routes: create, open and decide. It had nothing else:

- **No place in the cut.** A reviewer could approve or request changes with one free-text note.
  "The door opens a beat early" had to be described in words, and the owner then had to find the
  moment by scrubbing.
- **The owner never saw the answer.** `submitReviewDecision` stored the decision in the link
  record, and no route returned it. The owner's project view lists the owner's own animatic
  approvals, not a reviewer's.
- **No stage.** A decision on an animatic and a decision on a sound mix were the same record, so
  "the rough cut is approved, the final is not" could not be read from them.

## What this increment does

- **Timecoded comments.** `POST /api/reviews/:token/comments` with `{frame, text}` pins a comment
  to one frame, at 30 fps, of the cut the link is bound to. The gates are the same as for a
  decision:
  - the link has the `approve` capability (`mayComment`); a `read` link only watches;
  - it has been opened, so it names its cut (409 otherwise);
  - on a link that counts viewers, the caller is a viewer it counted (409 otherwise);
  - it is not revoked, expired or past its views (403);
  - the bound cut is still retained and permitted (`assertSelectedOutput`; PostgreSQL re-reads it
    under a share lock, as it does for a decision).
- **Bounded.** The frame is an integer from 0 to 431,999 (four hours). The text is trimmed and
  holds 1 to 500 characters. A link holds at most 50 comments; a further one is refused with 409.
- **Through the content-policy gate.** The text goes through `checkPrompt`, the gate every prompt
  passes. A refusal answers 422 `{reason: "content_policy", category}` and nothing is kept. The
  refusal set is the safety package's own, so it can only grow with it.
- **No identity.** A stored comment has six fields: `id`, `frame`, `text`, `viewer`, `at` and
  `resolvedAt`. `viewer` is the link's own viewer hash on a link that counts viewers, and `null`
  otherwise. No cookie, header value or address is kept (ADR-0018).
- **The owner's view.** `GET /api/projects/:id/reviews` lists every review link of the project:
  - a link is named by its digest, never by its token;
  - each link carries its bound job, its decision, and its comments with `HH:MM:SS:FF` timecodes;
  - a comment's author is shown only as their place in the link's own count ("viewer 2"), never as
    the hash.
- **Resolving.** `POST /api/projects/:id/review-comments/:commentId` with `{resolved}` marks a
  comment resolved (and records when) or open again. Only the owner of the project can do this.
- **Per-stage approvals.** A decision now records `decisionStage` and `decidedAt`. The stage comes
  from the bound cut's job stage, not from the caller:

  | Job stage | Review stage |
  |---|---|
  | animatic | rough cut |
  | final | final |
  | picture edit, assembly edit | picture edit |
  | sound mix, dialogue replacement, lip sync | sound mix |
  | delivery | deliverable |

  A job stage with no review stage refuses the decision. The owner's view has one row per stage,
  in that order, with approved and changes-requested counts and the latest decision.
- **Kept and purged with the link.** Comments and the stage live inside the review link record.
  The JSON store keeps that record as it was read, and PostgreSQL keeps it whole in
  `hv_reviews.body`, so they are exported, archived and restored with the link. They are removed
  with it when the project is swept or taken down.
- **No state schema bump.** Every reader, including an older one, carries the record through
  unchanged. `validateSnapshot` bounds the new fields on the way in. main stays at `hv-state/16`,
  so this does not collide with HV-031-12's `hv-state/17`.

## Acceptance criteria

1. **A comment reaches the owner.** A counted viewer of an opened approve link comments at frame
   45. The owner sees it with timecode `00:00:01:15`, the bound job, and "viewer 2". The owner's
   list does not contain the link token, and no cookie is set.
2. **It stays on the bound cut.** After a newer cut finishes, the comment is still on the job the
   link was fixed to.
3. **Only a reviewer who may decide may comment.** These are refused, and nothing is kept:
   - a read link (403);
   - an unopened link (409);
   - an uncounted or missing viewer on a counting link (409);
   - a revoked link (403).
4. **Bounded.** These answer 400:
   - frames −1, 1.5, 432,000, `"12"` and `null`;
   - empty, blank, non-string and 501-character text.
   500 characters at the last frame is accepted. The 51st comment answers 409, and 50 are kept.
5. **Content policy.** Every prompt in `PROHIBITED_PROMPT_BATTERY` is refused as a comment with
   422, `content_policy` and its category, and nothing is kept.
6. **No identity.** A stored comment has exactly the six fields, and its `viewer` is the link's hash
   of the viewer id. The raw id is nowhere in the state. On a link without viewer ids, `viewer` is
   `null`.
7. **Resolving.** The owner resolves a comment, which records the time, and opens it again. These
   are refused:
   - another project's owner (404);
   - no bearer token (401);
   - a non-boolean `resolved` (400).
8. **Per-stage approvals.** A request for changes on an animatic link records `rough-cut`. After
   the owner approves the animatic, an approval on a link bound to the final records `final`. The
   owner's view lists the five stages in order: rough cut has 1 change request, final has 1
   approval, and the other three have none. Every reviewable job stage maps as in the table above;
   other stages map to none.
9. **Timecode.** At 30 fps, frame 0 is `00:00:00:00`, 29 is `00:00:00:29`, 30 is `00:00:01:00`,
   1837 is `00:01:01:07`, and 431,999 is `03:59:59:29`.
10. **Snapshots keep them with no bump.** An `hv-state/1` snapshot holding a comment and a
    decision stage validates. It round-trips through `writeStateSnapshot`/`readStateSnapshot`, and
    `ProjectService.fromState(...).snapshot()` returns the link unchanged.
11. **Snapshots bound them.** `validateSnapshot` refuses:
    - 51 comments;
    - 501-character or blank text;
    - a negative or fractional frame;
    - a duplicate id;
    - a viewer the link did not count;
    - an extra field, or a malformed time;
    - comments on an unbound link;
    - an unknown stage, or a malformed decision time.
12. **Purged with the project (JSON).** A sweep and a takedown each remove the link and every
    comment.
13. **PostgreSQL (gated).**
    - A comment is refused while an artifact row of the bound cut is missing, and kept once it is back.
    - A second connection sees it and resolves it.
    - A decision records `rough-cut`.
    - A read link cannot comment.
    - `purgeProject` leaves no `hv_reviews` row, and the owner's view is gone.
14. Reverting `packages/api/src/index.ts`, `server.ts`, `review-capability.ts` and
    `packages/storage/src/projects.ts` and `snapshots.ts` fails criteria 1–8 and 10–13. Removing
    `packages/api/src/review-comments.ts` fails them all.

- Tests: `packages/api/test/review-comments.test.ts` (routes, through the real server on the JSON
  store) and `packages/storage/test/review-comments.test.ts` (snapshots, JSON purge, and the
  PostgreSQL path when `HV_PG_*` is set).

## Known gaps this leaves

- **No screens yet.** The review page and the owner's project view do not show comments or stages.
  That is HV-029-15.
- **A reviewer cannot list earlier comments.** The review view does not return the link's
  comments, so after a reload a reviewer sees only what the page kept.
- **Frames are not bounded by the cut's own length.** Jobs carry no common duration field, so the
  bound is four hours rather than the cut's last frame.
- **The deliverable stage cannot be reached yet.** `assertSelectedOutput` refuses a delivery job,
  so no review link can be bound to one. The mapping is in place for when that changes.
- **Decisions recorded before this increment have no stage.** They are listed with
  `decisionStage: null` and counted under no stage.
- **The new routes are labelled `unmatched` in metrics.** Like the existing create and revoke
  routes, they are not added to `ROUTE_TEMPLATES`, which would change its cardinality bound.
