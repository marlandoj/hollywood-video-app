# HV-021-10 — The desk says a CONTINUOUS scene's contradictions in words

epic: HV-021
release: 2 "Voice and crew depth"
gate: none new
spend_usd: 0

## Why

HV-021-07 put the continuity report and its repair on the Director's desk. HV-021-08 then added two
findings across a `CONTINUOUS` heading and a `continuousComparisons` counter. The two landed
separately, and the panel didn't know about the new parts:

- A wardrobe change across `INT. STAIRWELL - CONTINUOUS` showed as
  `Warning: wardrobe-contradicts-previous`, and the time of day as `Warning: time-contradicts-previous`.
  Every other kind on the panel reads as a sentence.
- The "nothing to fix across N comparisons" line added up only the look, wardrobe and handoff
  counters. A film whose only comparisons were across a `CONTINUOUS` heading was told that nothing
  was declared yet, which isn't true.
- The repair's one-line summary named every contradiction it leaves by its code: "Nothing here can
  be repaired automatically: time-contradicts-heading. Read the notes." That is the first sentence
  a creator reads before pressing apply, and it is the one line on the panel the creator can't
  decode.

Known gaps in HV-021-07 and HV-021-08 named all three.

## What this increment does

- **Two new kind words on the panel** (`CONTINUITY_KINDS` in `packages/frontend/src/continuity.js`):
  "A CONTINUOUS scene's time of day contradicts the scene before it" and "A character's wardrobe
  changes across a CONTINUOUS heading".
- **The count includes `continuousComparisons`.** "Nothing to fix. The saved declarations agree with
  each other across N comparisons." now adds up all four counters, and "nothing is declared yet"
  appears only when none of them counted anything.
- **The summary names contradictions in words, at its source.** The summary string is built by
  `continuityRepairSummary` in `packages/planner/src/continuity-repair.ts`, and the API serves it
  unchanged. A new `CONTINUITY_REPAIR_CONTRADICTION_WORDS` gives each of the three contradictions a
  phrase. The summary joins them: "a, b and c". The proposal's `refused` field still lists the codes,
  so anything that reads the machine field is unaffected.
- The panel and the summary use the same words. A test holds them equal, so a contradiction can't
  get words in one place and stay a code in the other.
- `docs/CONTINUITY.md` describes the words and the count in its desk and summary sections.

## Acceptance criteria

1. **A CONTINUOUS scene's two contradictions are drawn in words.** On a real report where MARGUERITE
   changes coat across the heading and the continuing shot is directed "night" after a DAY scene,
   the panel shows both kinds as sentences, and no kind on the panel matches a `…-contradicts-…`
   code. Every contradiction the repair knows has panel words equal to its summary words.
2. **The nothing-to-fix count includes comparisons across a CONTINUOUS heading.** A film with the
   same wardrobe on both sides is counted across all four counters. With only the CONTINUOUS
   comparisons left, it reads "across 2 comparisons", not "nothing is declared yet".
3. **The repair's summary names each contradiction it leaves in words, and the proposal still
   carries the codes.** A scene against its own heading reads "…a shot's time of day contradicts the
   scene heading. Read the notes." Both CONTINUOUS kinds read as one sentence joined by "and". The
   panel shows that sentence, no paragraph on it holds a code, and `refused` still lists the codes.
4. Reverting `continuity.js` fails criteria 1 and 2 (2 tests). Reverting only the summary's wording
   in `continuity-repair.ts` (keeping the new export) fails criterion 3 and the four updated planner
   summary tests. Reverting the whole file makes `continuity-desk.test.js` unloadable, since it imports
   `CONTINUITY_REPAIR_CONTRADICTION_WORDS`.

- Tests: `packages/frontend/test/continuity-desk.test.js` (1–3, 3 new tests; 18 / 0). Updated honestly,
  because they pinned the code in the summary: `packages/planner/test/continuity-repair.test.ts`,
  `continuity-repair-contradiction.test.ts`, `continuity-repair-continuous.test.ts` (the four repair
  suites, 17 / 0) and `packages/api/test/continuity-route.test.ts`. That suite, the repair-route suite
  and the frontend-module suite pass against local PostgreSQL (9 / 0).

## Known gaps

- **Closes** the HV-021-07 gap "The repair's headline still names a code" and the HV-021-08 gap
  "The Director's desk panel has no words for the new kinds" (both the words and the count).
- **The words live in two places.** The panel is plain JavaScript served as-is and can't import the
  planner. So `CONTINUITY_KINDS` and `CONTINUITY_REPAIR_CONTRADICTION_WORDS` are separate. The test in
  criterion 1 holds the three contradictions equal. The other four kinds are panel-only and still
  fall back to the raw code if a new one is added without a label, as HV-021-07 noted.
- **The summary's words are English only**, like every other string the repair writes.
- **Checked on the repository's DOM stub, not in a browser or screen reader**, as HV-021-07 was.
