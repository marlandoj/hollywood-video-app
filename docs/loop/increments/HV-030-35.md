# HV-030-35 — The run driver exports the joined feature's cut and makes a composite at the desk

epic: HV-030
release: 3 "Features"
gate: none new
spend_usd: 0

## Why

Release 3's strict-$0 rehearsal 4 finished the whole feature at the front door on staging: 202 shots,
10 sequences, every sequence scored, joined, titled and shared. Then `scripts/release-3-run.ts`
(HV-030-31) ran behind the front door, and `bun test/release-3-contract.ts run-2.json --rehearsal`
reported 4 problems:

- `HV-030.feature-review` was not exercised. That is expected: Kevin reviews on his phone in the live
  run only.
- `HV-019.quality-routing`: "sequence 1's final was not routed on a measured score: its strategy is
  configured". Staging doesn't set `HV_ROUTING_STRATEGY=quality` or `HV_ROUTING_QUALITY_RESULTS_PATH` yet.
- `HV-023.interchange`: "the feature has no saved editorial sequence; the joined feature is not one
  (HV-030-30), and the interchange export reads saved sequences only (HV-023-04)". But HV-023-05 (#368)
  added an OTIO and EDL export of the joined feature itself. The driver predates it and never called it.
- `HV-025.vfx-composite`: the driver read a composite only from `--vfx-job <id>`, saying "a VFX composite
  has no route". HV-025-13 (#366) made one through the editorial desk's own routes, rendered by the
  editorial conform on the studio's machine. Its quote is `costUsd: 0` and its job's cost cap is $0. Its
  Known gaps say the run driver will call it.

**What the quality strategy does on a mock pool.** A final's provider plan pins the results file at
admission, whatever its pool (`createProviderPlan`). Checked here with `HV_PROVIDER_POOL='["mock"]'`,
`HV_ROUTING_STRATEGY=quality` and the committed `docs/evidence/release-3/benchmark/routing-results.json`:
the plan's strategy is `quality`, its `resultsSha256` is the file's, and its `fallback` is null. That is
everything HV-030-31's step checks. So with those two settings on staging, the step would pass in a mock
rehearsal. But every shot's route decision would say `selectedScore: null`, "not measured: the results
file has no record for mock". No shot would have been rendered by a measured provider, and the record
didn't say so.

## What this increment does

- **The joined feature's cut** (`exportFeatureCut`). With a finished joined film, `--interchange` asks
  `GET /api/projects/:id/feature-film/:jobId/interchange/{otio,edl}` for both files (HV-023-05). They are
  read back as a saved cut's are:
  - each file's sha256 against the studio's `x-hv-interchange-sha256` header;
  - both through HV-023-04's independent readers, to the same shots and frames;
  - and the OTIO must name the joined film, and the EDL's media must be the sequences' films in the
    order they were joined, then the end credits.
  A join the studio refuses (stale, unfinished, a newer one) is `unavailable` with the studio's reason. A
  host without the route says so, naming HV-023-05. `--interchange <sequence id>` still exports that saved
  cut, read back by the same helper. The record says which cut it is (`cut`).
- **A composite at the desk** (`makeVfxComposite`, `--vfx [sequence]`). Over a shot of that sequence's
  film as joined (sequence 1 by default), the driver:
  1. inspects the film (`GET /editorial/sources/:jobId`, waiting while it answers 202) and saves it as an
     editorial sequence of its own;
  2. adds HV-025-13's one composite with `vfxCompositeOperation`: the film's middle 30 frames over its
     first shot from frame 15, through a held rectangle over the middle of the frame, at 75%, saved as one
     edit that the desk validates;
  3. renders it only if the desk quotes $0, and waits for it;
  4. reads the composite back from the job view (`pictureEdit.vfx`). It must be one masked composite with
     this film as plate and element, finished at $0.
  Run again, it reuses the sequence, the edit and, by its request key, the render. `--vfx-job <id>` still
  reads a composite made some other way. The two can't be used together.
- **Quality routing, said plainly** (`shotQuality`). Each sequence's record keeps `routedOnScore`: its
  shots, how many a provider with a measured score rendered, and the router's reason for the first that
  wasn't. The step keeps HV-030-31's rule (every final's plan pins a used results file) and its note now
  says, for example, "0 of 202 shots were rendered by a provider with a measured score; the first that
  wasn't: not measured: the results file has no record for mock".
- The runbook's step 4 in `docs/loop/increments/HV-030-31.md` adds `--vfx`, and its step 7 no longer
  expects `HV-023.interchange` or `HV-025.vfx-composite` to be deferred when their steps finish.

## Acceptance criteria

1. **The joined feature's cut is exported and read back.** `--interchange` asks HV-023-05's route for the
   joined film's OTIO and EDL. Both match their sha256 headers and read back to the same shots and frames,
   naming the 10 sequences' films in join order, then the credits, with the title on its own layer.
   `HV-023.interchange` is exercised with the two files' sha256s, and the record meets the contract.
2. **A saved cut is still exported.** `--interchange feature-cut` exports that saved sequence, as before.
3. **A cut that can't be trusted is not the part.** A join the studio refuses, a cut whose films are out
   of join order and a host without the route each leave the part unexercised, and the step says why.
4. **The composite is made at the desk, at $0.** `--vfx` inspects sequence 1's film, saves it as a
   sequence and saves one edit, which the real `applyEditOperation` accepts: one clip of the film's middle
   frames on the layer above its first shot, through a held rectangle, at 75%. It renders once, on the
   edit just saved, with the review accepted. It waits for the job and records the composite.
   `HV-025.vfx-composite` is exercised with the render's job id. Run again, nothing new is saved.
5. **Through the real routes.** Against the real API and worker on mock, the driver's composite is
   accepted, rendered by the worker, read back from the job view with the film as plate and element, and
   costs $0 on the ledger. A second run reuses the sequence and the render. A stranger's token is refused
   before anything is saved, and no token is in the error. The driver's feature-cut request reaches the
   real route: a project that isn't a feature is refused there by name.
6. **Nothing that spends is asked for.** A composite the desk quotes above $0 is never rendered.
7. **Quality routing says what was measured.** With the plan pinned and no shot routed on a score, the
   step says "0 of N shots…" and the router's reason. With scores, it counts each final's measured shots.
8. **The command line** takes `--vfx` with or without a sequence number, refuses a non-number, and
   refuses `--vfx` beside `--vfx-job`.
9. **Reverting the change fails the new tests.** Restoring `scripts/release-3-run.ts` from `main` fails
   the 6 new tests in `test/release-3-driver.test.ts` and its updated first test on their assertions, and
   `test/release-3-desk-routes.test.ts` on the module it imports (checked).

- Tests: `test/release-3-driver.test.ts` (1, 2, 3, 4, 6, 7, 8; 17 pass),
  `test/release-3-desk-routes.test.ts` (5; 2 pass). Unchanged and passing: `test/release-3-run.test.ts`
  and `test/release-3-scripts.test.ts` (24 pass, 1 skipped until the real record).

## Known gaps

- **Quality routing can pass in a mock rehearsal without a measured shot.** That is HV-030-31's rule,
  kept here. Setting `HV_ROUTING_STRATEGY=quality` and `HV_ROUTING_QUALITY_RESULTS_PATH` on staging would
  pass the step on mock, and its note would say 0 shots were measured. In the live run a shot is measured
  only when its provider is in the results file under the same capability revision
  (`fal:kling-o3-standard-reference`, `fal:kling-v2.5-turbo-pro`). The anchored keyframes lane is not
  benchmarked (HV-019-14), so anchored shots count as unmeasured. Whether the part should ask for measured
  shots is Kevin's call, not changed here.
- **The composite is in a shot of a sequence's film, not in the joined film.** A picture edit is not a
  sequence final, so the join does not take it (HV-025-13's gap). The record names the sequence's film it
  was made over.
- **The composite's element is the same film's middle frames, not a generated graphic.** A graphic needs
  the graphics browser, which staging may not have. The matte, window and opacity are fixed.
- **The fake desk's feature cut is written by the real writers, not by the real route.** The real route is
  reached only for a refusal here (no feature is made in these tests). HV-023-05's own test makes a
  feature and exports it. The rehearsal rerun will show it on staging.
- Nothing has run on staging. The rerun of step 4 is the check.
