# HV-019-20 — Prompt limits are counted in UTF-8 bytes, on the body fal is sent (Release 3's resumed run, G23)

epic: HV-019
release: 3 "Features"
gate: none new
spend_usd: 0

## Why

HV-019-19 (#379) was deployed and Release 3's live run was resumed (`studio-run --resume`, HV-030-37).
Sequence 1's final (job 9760e8b8) reused shots 1-8 at $0. Then it failed on shot 9 (`shot-2-1`, scene 2,
"INT. CLOCK TOWER WORKSHOP - DAWN - CONTINUOUS"):

```
fal GET /fal-ai/kling-video/requests/… failed (422): {"detail":[{"loc":["body"],"msg":"prompt: size must be
between 0 and 2500","type":"input_value_error", …
```

Reproduced from the job's own body (the live crew's casting, direction and style bible, the admitted plan),
through the reuse path the worker checks itself against, the worker's recipe and the real fal adapter:

| Shot 9's prompt as fal received it | Size |
|---|---|
| UTF-16 units (what HV-019-19 measured) | 2,495 |
| Code points | 2,495 |
| UTF-8 bytes | 2,503 |

- The string sent is exactly the fitted prompt plus O3's image note. Nothing is added after the fit, and the
  reuse path, the limit and the reference budget are the right ones.
- The live crew's cast direction quotes the screenplay in curly quotes (“ ”), three UTF-8 bytes each. Four
  of them make the 8 bytes over.
- fal counts this limit in bytes. Its first refusal said "at most 2500 characters" and this one said "size".
  The fit and the guard counted UTF-16 units, so both passed it.
- Two more shots of the sequence were over by bytes as well: 2,502 and 2,507.
- The ledger recorded $0.588 for each of 3 attempts on shot 9: $1.764, the job's `costUsd`. The adapter treats
  a failure after `COMPLETED` as billed. Whether fal bills a 422 is not known here.

## What this increment does

- **`promptSize`** (`packages/generator/src/prompt-limits.ts`) is a prompt's UTF-8 bytes. They are never
  fewer than its characters or code points, so a prompt within it fits however fal counts.
- **Every limit and fit uses it.** It is used for the catalogue's limits less the image note, for the mock,
  and for every size and cut in `fitShotPrompts`. The limit stays 2,500.
- **The cut mark is ASCII** ("...", `CUT_MARK`), not "…".
- **The fit record is `hv-prompt-fit/2`**, with `originalBytes`, `fittedBytes` and each cut's `fromBytes`
  and `toBytes`.
- **The fal guard measures the request itself.** It serializes the body it is about to send, reads its
  `prompt` field and refuses it before any request if its bytes are over the limit.
- **Shots within the limit are unchanged.** Shots 1-8 were not fitted, and their prompts are byte for byte
  as rendered.
- docs/PROVIDER-ROUTING.md says so under "Prompt limits".

   between 0 and 2500" on a prompt opening "INT. CLOCK TOWER WORKSHOP - DAWN - CONTINUOUS. A round room
   full of gears".
2. **Shots 1-8 are unchanged.** Each recorded `inputHash` in the job's shot reuse equals the current
   `renderInputHash`, and the worker's `validateReusePlan` passes.
3. **Shot 9 fits by fal's count.** fal receives exactly the dispatched prompt plus the four-image note, and
   its UTF-8 bytes (more than its characters) are at most 2,500. Every shot of the sequence is too. The fit
   record is in bytes.
4. **The guard refuses by bytes.** The fal adapter refuses shot 9's unfitted prompt with zero requests. It
   also refuses a prompt that is 2,495 characters and 2,503 bytes with the note, with zero requests. The mock
   refuses 2,498 characters that are 2,502 bytes.
5. **Reverting the change fails the new test.** With `fal.ts`, `index.ts` and `prompt-fit.ts` restored from
   main (`promptSize` kept), `g23-resumed-final-prompt.test.ts` fails. Shot 9 is sent at 2,503 bytes, as
   on staging, and the guard sends the 2,503-byte prompt (checked).

## Known gaps

- **A 422 at result time is recorded as billed and retried.** Shot 9 was attempted 3 times. Each time the
  ledger recorded $0.588. A vendor input refusal is the same on every try, but the worker still retries it.
  That is not changed here.
- **Bytes may be stricter than fal needs.** If fal counts characters for some models, a prompt with many
  non-ASCII characters is cut a little more than needed.
- **The fixture is one job's body.** It carries the live crew's text as of this run. A later edit to the cast
  or bible is not in it.
