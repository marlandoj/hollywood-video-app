# HV-026-07 — A finished cut can be graded, and a grade that clips is not offered

epic: HV-026
release: 2 "Voice and crew depth"
gate: none new
spend_usd: 0

## Why

HV-026's Release 2 slice is "Grade and LUTs, QC checks. ffmpeg only". Six increments built the QC
half: a finished film is measured, and every deliverable carries the report (HV-027-06). The grade
half was not built. A creator could crop a cut and wrap it as a mezzanine, but could not change what
it looked like. Nothing in the studio applied a colour decision or a LUT, and `PICTURE-QC.md` said
so: "the grade and LUT half is not built".

## What this increment does

- **A grade is a deliverable: a delivery kind, `grade`.** It is a new job that names the
  cut's sealed output revision. The cut is never written, so a grade is non-destructive by
  construction. It also reuses everything a deliverable already has:
  - admission at zero cost;
  - the cut's cast and source permission, and its retention (HV-027-14);
  - the worker, reading the master alone;
  - sealing, with the picture quality check.
- **The decision is eight bounded numbers and a look.** The numbers are exposure, temperature, tint,
  saturation, lift, gain, contrast and gamma, in hundredths. The decision is hashed into the grade's
  plan revision, and that revision joins the deliverable's idempotency key. The other kinds' keys
  and plans are unchanged.
- **The ffmpeg chain is derived from the decision and re-derived on every read.** It grades in
  16-bit RGB: `colorchannelmixer`, then `lutrgb`, then `lut3d` (tetrahedral), back to Rec.709 limited
  range. The picture is H.264 and the soundtrack is copied. The RGB is packed `rgb48le`, because
  `lutrgb` on planar 16-bit RGB does not map a channel to itself in this ffmpeg.
- **Four looks are authored here and shipped as `.cube` files.** They are `neutral-709`, `warm`,
  `cool` and `film-contrast`. `bun scripts/color-looks.ts` writes them, and a grade is bound to its
  look's SHA-256. Every look keeps its ends, so a look never clips on its own.
- **A grade is checked, and gated, before it is offered.** In the decode that grades it, masks count
  the pixels the grade put at 0 or full scale that the cut had not. The graded file is then read back
  for the share of each frame's luma outside 14–241 (EBU R 103's −1%/103%). The grade is withheld if
  either of these is true:
  - more than 5% of frames have 1% or more newly clipped;
  - any frame has 1% or more outside the tolerance.
- **A withheld grade is sealed and never served.** The list shows it unavailable, with every reason
  and remedy, the decision and the check. The artifact route answers 404. The check is bound to the
  plan, the file's digest and the quality check's levels, and is re-derived on every read.
- **The routes.** `GET …/deliveries/:cut` answers a `grade` offer and the library.
  `POST … {kind:"grade",grade:{…}}` admits a grade.
- **Docs.** New `docs/COLOR-GRADE.md`. `PICTURE-QC.md` and `DELIVERY-JOBS.md` point to it.

## Acceptance criteria

1. **Decision.** A decision out of range, off its step, with an unknown control, or with a look
   outside the library is refused by name. A plan whose chain was edited is refused.
2. **Idempotency.** The same decision on the same cut has one idempotency key. A changed decision,
   look or cut has another. A grade without a decision, and a decision on another kind, are refused.
   The reframe and mezzanine keys are the pre-grade formula.
3. **Looks.** Each shipped `.cube` equals its authoring function and its recorded SHA-256 and size.
   Each look keeps 0 and 1 and rises monotonically.
4. **Render.** On a real cut:
   - a neutral grade clips nothing new, keeps the size, length and soundtrack, and is offered;
   - gain 1.6 clips the highlights in every frame and is withheld;
   - lift −0.2 with contrast 1.5 crushes the blacks and is withheld;
   - warm and cool move Cb and Cr in opposite directions and clip nothing;
   - a plan for another size is refused before anything is encoded.
5. **Gate.** A tallied measurement is withheld past 5% of frames clipped, or on any frame with 1% of
   its luma outside tolerance, and each reason names the remedy. Below that is a note. A check edited
   to read "offered" is refused. Every tally the reduction makes is one the check accepts: a share
   that rounds to nothing names no frame, so one stray pixel over 241 in a 1080p frame (a share of
   about 4.8e-7) is tallied as nothing rather than refused.
6. **Route, offered.** A finished cut offers `grade` with the library. A decision is admitted at zero
   cost, bound to the cut's sealed output. The worker makes it, it is listed offered, and it is
   served (200). The cut's sealed output is unchanged.
7. **Route, withheld.** A grade with gain 2 and exposure 1 completes and is listed with no link, and
   `unavailable` names the clipping and the remedy. Its file answers 404 to a valid artifact token.
   The same decision asked for again under a new key returns the withheld job and queues nothing; a
   changed decision is a new job.
8. Reverting the source files fails criteria 2, 6 and 7:
   - reverting `packages/api/src/server.ts` alone fails criterion 7, because the withheld grade is
     served (200);
   - reverting `packages/api/src/delivery-api.ts` fails criteria 6 and 7;
   - reverting `packages/planner/src/delivery-jobs.ts` fails criterion 2, and the route suite no
     longer loads;
   - reverting the tally fix in `packages/planner/src/color-grade.ts` fails criterion 5 (the tally
     test);
   - reverting the withheld-grade lookup in `packages/api/src/delivery-api.ts` fails criterion 7,
     because a new key renders the same withheld grade again.

## Known gaps this leaves

- **No frontend.** There is no grading panel; the routes answer JSON.
- **One decision covers the whole cut.** There is no per-shot grade, no shot matching and no look
  lock.
- **A graded cut cannot be reframed, made a mezzanine or have its captions burned in,** because a
  deliverable is not a delivery source.
- **Only luma is judged against the tolerance.** Chroma levels and RGB gamut in the delivered file
  are not, and a hue shift inside the RGB cube is not counted as clipping.
- **"Reviewed" means checked and versioned.** There is no approval step on a grade; per-stage
  approvals are HV-029's.
- **The PostgreSQL worker path is not driven by a grade test.** It runs the same render and seal
  functions as every deliverable, but only the JSON path is exercised end to end.
