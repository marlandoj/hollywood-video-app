# HV-030-25 — A paid crew answer is not thrown away for one long question, and the studio says why an answer was unusable

epic: HV-030
release: 2 "Voice and crew depth"
gate: none new
spend_usd: 0

## Why

The Release 2 exit run on staging (2026-10-01 22:36 UTC) used `HV_CREW_PROVIDER=openrouter` with
`anthropic/claude-sonnet-5.5`. The plan step answered live (`plan.source: "openrouter"`). The
read-through was paid for, $0.022 on the crew line, and then fell back to the stand-in with
`fallbackReason: "model_unusable"`. Nothing was logged, so we couldn't see why.

Reading the code, there were four ways in:

- **Any one defect threw the whole answer away.** `validateCrewVoice` refused the answer for one
  question over 300 characters, one proposal over 400, one unknown crew member or one fourth question
  from a crew member. The prompt states the limits, but they are tight, and a model can miss one.
- **The read-through asked for 2000 output tokens. The plan step asks for 6000.** The longest answer
  the read-through's own prompt allows is a 200-character logline, a 1200-character summary and
  fifteen questions of up to 700 characters, about 12,000 characters of JSON. That's roughly 3,000
  tokens, before any reasoning tokens OpenRouter may count against `max_tokens`. The bill fits a
  cut-off: at $2 in and $10 out per million tokens, $0.022 is, for example, 1,000 tokens in and the
  full 2,000 out. That's consistent with `finish_reason: "length"`, though it doesn't prove it.
- **Fences and prose around the JSON** were already handled: the JSON is read from the first `{` to
  the last `}`.
- **Crew member ids** had to match exactly. "Director" wasn't the director.

## What this increment does

- **The read-through drops a defective question, not the answer.** A question that's too long, isn't
  text, names an unknown crew member, or is a crew member's fourth or later is left out whole. Nothing
  is cut short into the creator's view. The rest keep their order and are numbered `q1`, `q2`, ...
- **It says how many were dropped.** The answer carries `dropped`, a count. The studio tells the
  creator: "1 of the crew's questions couldn't be used and was left out."
- **Some answers are still unusable as a whole:**
  - questions were asked and none survives;
  - the logline or summary is empty or over its limit;
  - the answer isn't JSON of the asked shape.
  A model that asks no questions is still used, as the prompt allows.
- **Safety doesn't shrink.** Every string in the answer passes the gate first, including a question
  that would be dropped for length. If the gate refuses any of it, the whole answer is discarded, as
  before.
- **Crew member ids are read without regard to case or surrounding spaces.**
- **The read-through asks for 6000 output tokens**, the plan step's budget (`READ_THROUGH_MAX_TOKENS`,
  `CREW_PLAN_MAX_TOKENS`). Spend admission is unchanged: `assertCanSpend` against the crew line, as before.
- **Every crew path says why.** A paid answer the studio can't use carries one fixed code,
  `unusableReason`:
  - from the vendor: `cut_off`, `empty`, `refused_by_model`, `bad_shape`. The adapters now attach these
    to `CrewModelUnusable`, and `askCrewModel` returns the reason.
  - from the studio reading the text: `no_json`, `bad_shape`, `gate_refused`, `too_long`,
    `unknown_persona`.
  - The Anthropic class now reports `stop_reason: "max_tokens"` as `cut_off`, with its cost. This
    closes a gap HV-030-24 left.
- **The API logs it.** `crew.answer_unusable` is logged at `warn` from the read-through, plan and
  line-notes routes, with `step`, `vendor`, `model`, `reason`, `costUsd` and `projectId`. Each of the
  four new log keys takes only closed values: the three steps, the crew vendors, the crew price
  table's ids and the eight reasons. Free text in any of them is dropped by the logger's allow-list.
  The model's text, the prompt and the key never reach a log line.
- **The plan step stays all or nothing.** A plan is applied as the film's cast and direction:
  - dropping a cast entry would leave a speaking character with no look;
  - dropping a shot would leave it undirected, under a plan credited to the model.
  The stand-in's plan is whole, so a defective plan still falls back to it. It now says why (`too_long`
  for an over-limit field, `gate_refused`, `bad_shape` for an unknown shot or character, `no_json`).
- **Line notes keep their per-note dropping**, which they already had. Each note is a separate edit
  the writer takes or skips. A note the gate refuses is still dropped and never shown. An unusable
  answer now carries its reason.

## Acceptance criteria

1. **One over-limit question.** The read-through keeps the answer, drops that question whole,
   renumbers the rest and reports `dropped: 1`. A live read-through with one long question answers
   with `source: "openrouter"` and spends once.
2. **Unknown crew member.** That question is dropped. " Director " is read as the director.
3. **A fourth question from one crew member** is dropped, and the first three are kept.
4. **Gate refusal.** Text the gate refuses anywhere makes the whole answer unusable (`gate_refused`),
   even in a question that would have been dropped for length.
5. **No surviving questions.** The answer is unusable, with the first question's defect as the reason.
   An answer with no questions at all is used.
6. **Cut-off.** An OpenRouter `finish_reason: "length"` and an Anthropic `stop_reason: "max_tokens"`
   each give `unusableReason: "cut_off"`, with the cost recorded on the crew line.
7. **Other reasons.** Each path returns its reason: `empty`, `refused_by_model`, `no_json`,
   `gate_refused`, `unknown_persona` and `too_long` from the read-through; `too_long`, `bad_shape`,
   `gate_refused` and `no_json` from the plan; `cut_off`, `no_json` and `bad_shape` from line notes.
   Line notes still drop a bad note and keep the rest.
8. **The log.** A cut-off read-through, plan and line notes each log one `crew.answer_unusable` line,
   with the right step, vendor, model, reason and cost. No log line holds the model's text, the
   script, the tone or the key. A usable answer logs nothing. The log keys accept only their closed
   values.
9. **max tokens.** The read-through asks for 6000 output tokens, the same as the plan step.
10. **The studio** tells the creator how many questions were left out, and says nothing when none
    were.
11. Reverting the change fails the named tests:
    - `read-through.ts` (keeping its new constant) fails 11 of the 13 planner tests, criteria 1–7 and 9;
    - `production-plan.ts` fails the plan's reasons;
    - `line-notes.ts` fails the line notes' reasons;
    - `crew-model.ts` (keeping the new exports) fails the cut-off, vendor-reason, line-notes and
      route-log tests;
    - `logs.ts` fails all three log tests;
    - `server.ts` fails the two route-log tests;
    - `studio.js` fails the frontend test.

- Tests: `packages/planner/test/crew-read-through-tolerance.test.ts` (criteria 1–7, 9),
  `packages/api/test/crew-answer-unusable-log.test.ts` (criterion 8),
  `packages/frontend/test/studio-dropped-questions.test.js` (criterion 10). The existing crew suites
  pass: `packages/planner/test/crew-*.test.ts`, `packages/generator/test/crew-model*.test.ts`,
  `packages/api/test/crew-*.test.ts`, `packages/observability/test/*.test.ts`,
  `packages/frontend/test/studio*.test.js`. `crew-read-through.test.ts` changed in two places:
  - four questions from the director are no longer a whole-answer refusal;
  - `validateCrewVoice` now returns `dropped`.

## Known gaps this leaves

- **The exit run's actual cause isn't confirmed.** `spend_usd` is 0, and nothing here calls a vendor.
  The next live read-through logs `crew.answer_unusable` with its reason if it still falls back.
  The bill points at `cut_off`, and the read-through now asks for 6000 tokens.
- **The plan step is still all or nothing.** A single over-limit light or transition still discards
  a paid plan. Filling a defective shot from the stand-in's plan would keep the rest, but would credit
  a mixed plan to the model. That needs its own decision.
- **The voice is gated string by string, not as a whole.** A proposal and a question that pass alone
  are gated together only when the creator's answers reach the plan step (HV-030-21). There, the
  creator is asked to reword them.
- **`dropped` doesn't say which kind of defect each dropped question had.** The log names only the
  reason of an answer that couldn't be used at all.
