# HV-024-02 — The Composer scores the film

epic: HV-024 (sound), with HV-030 (the crew)
release: 1 "Studio" (build order step 7, first part)
gate: G13-202609192200
spend_usd: 0

## Why

The studio's films have dialogue and no music. Release 1 has no music vendor, which is a new vendor and is Release 2 under G3. Bundling third-party tracks would need a human licence review. So the Composer writes its own bed with code.

## What this increment does

- **`packages/frontend/src/score.js`:**
  - `scoreDirection` makes the Composer's decision from the tone and the creator's answers. "No music" means no score.
  - `composeScore` renders a deterministic, seamless 48 kHz stereo pad and bass loop, with peaks at −12 dBFS.
  - `scoreRecord` gives the library record, whose rights say the recording is the application's own.
- **The studio flow.** `approveRoughCut` → `scoreFinal`:
  1. the sound quote for the finished (voiced) cut;
  2. the score uploaded once to the sound library and reused;
  3. one looping music cue under the whole film, with fades and ducking under the measured voice windows;
  4. a `sound-mix` with the fixed key `crew-score-<cut id>`.

  The scored cut is shared. A failed mix keeps the film and says so on the last approval.
- **The API** serves `score.js` beside the studio at `/api/studio/score.js`.
- **Docs:** `docs/CREW.md`.

## Acceptance criteria

1. **The score.**
   - The same bytes every time, in the WAV format.
   - Seamless ends and a −12 dBFS peak.
   - The Composer's table, and "no music" respected.
   - A rights record that says what the recording is.
   - Test: `packages/frontend/test/score.test.js`.
2. **The flow.**
   - The score is uploaded once and then reused.
   - The cue covers the whole film, with the fixed key.
   - The scored cut is shared.
   - A failed mix keeps the film with a note.
   - "No music" skips it.
   - Test: `packages/frontend/test/studio.test.js`.
3. **End to end.** With a real server, the worker and the fal fixture:
   - the scored cut is a `sound-mix` of the final;
   - the final's silent shot is silent;
   - the scored cut has the score there.
   - Test: `packages/api/test/final-dialogue.test.ts`.
4. **The module is served beside the studio.**
   - Test: `packages/api/test/frontend-modules.test.ts`.

## Risks

- **The music is modest.** It is a procedural bed, not a composed score. Better music is Release 2 (a vendor under G3) or curated public-domain tracks after a human licence review.
- **The rights attestation on the upload is the application's.** It is stated truthfully in the record. The creator's rights attestation at the pitch covers the script, not this music.
