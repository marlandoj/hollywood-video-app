# HV-016-24 — A mixed job's envelope, its runtime switch and its proof plan, still refused at the door

## Why

PR #83 ("Run mixed films with owned proof and atomic recovery") is being rebuilt on main as small
HV-016 increments, because the original branch is 648 commits behind and cannot be merged.
HV-016-23 brought over the planner's mixed-film contracts. The next layer up is the job itself: what
a V3 job envelope must hold, how the runtime tells V2 from V3, and which historical jobs, previews and
approvals a mixed film has to carry as proof before a worker may prepare it. Once `Job` can name V3
fields, every V2-only reader also has to refuse a V3 body explicitly instead of misreading it.

This is still inert. Admission refuses V3, and no worker, storage or archive path produces any of the
new fields.

## What this increment does

Ported from PR #83:

- **The V3 job envelope** (`packages/planner/src/current-film-mixed-job-context.ts`): full
  historical validation of a mixed job, including admission and idempotency mirrors, held-input
  comparison, the immutable kept-originals inventory, mixed output and recorded files, and mixed
  preview review and final approval. A final may approve either a V2 or a V3 preview.
- **The runtime switch** (`current-film-runtime-context.ts`) dispatches ordinary, V2 and V3 bodies
  to the right validator and gives checked `currentFilmV3Job` and held views. Nothing on main calls
  it yet.
- **Proof planning:** `current-film-proof-target.ts`, `current-film-proof-closure.ts`,
  `current-film-proof-copies.ts` and `current-film-prepared-proof.ts`. These cover the target's
  stable identity and approval replay, the closure of historical jobs, previews and carriers a mixed
  film depends on, the deterministic copy specification, and the prepared-proof marker a held
  transaction would store.
- **V2-only guards** (`current-film-job-context.ts`). A V2 or ordinary body with
  `currentFilmOrigins` or `currentFilmProof` is refused, as is a checkpoint or output that is not
  version 2. Every V2 entry point narrows through `assertCurrentFilmMode`. The V2 preview review
  reuses the cached full completed-source validation.
- **Bounded validation caches** for current shot plans, current direction and cast rebinds
  (`living-script-current-plan.ts`, `living-script-current-direction.ts`,
  `living-script-cast-rebind.ts`). Only complete historical replay is cached, and every call still
  checks the whole input.
- **Optional V3 fields on `Job`** (`packages/queue/src/index.ts`, types only):
  `currentFilm` and `currentFilmCheckpoint` widen to V2 or V3; `currentFilmOrigins` and
  `currentFilmProof` are added, and both are auto fields a caller cannot supply; and
  `output.currentFilm` may be V2 or V3. `validateCurrentFilmJob` in admission is unchanged.

Adapted to main:

- **The V3 `conflicts` list includes main's delivery fields** (`delivery`, `deliveryCheckpoint`,
  `deliveryOutput`), as the V2 list already does. A mixed job cannot also be a delivery job.
- **Existing V2 readers narrow explicitly** now that `Job` names V3. `assertCurrentFilmMode` or
  `currentFilmV2Job` was added in the worker's resume path, `verifyCurrentFilmMedia`, edit-source
  verification, adoption media, the artifact store's current-film file check and snapshot
  recovery. At runtime this only refuses bodies that admission already refuses. The PR's
  `assertCurrentFilmMode` and `currentFilmV2Job` narrowing edits to the existing queue and storage
  tests come along.
- **The source fixture** seeds its queue with the authority fixture's film, as in the PR.
- **One proof-copies test used to enqueue a V3 target.** Admission refuses that now, so the test
  checks the refusal and builds the exact queued record `enqueue` would have made. Discovery must
  still drop that record.
- **Main's reference-read lock** (`sheet-reference-lock.test.ts`) lists the proof modules' reads.
  Those reads are the recovery copies of reference images the history already named, and a count
  cap. None of them resolves a character for a render.
- **The storage transaction test** expects the V2 discriminator message, which is now raised first,
  as in the PR.

## Acceptance criteria

1. **Mixed job envelope.** A complete V3 request and its output bind real frame facts, the exact
   journal and the prepared-original metadata. Admission and held-input checks refuse forged progress
   and changed request mirrors. Reused progress needs the whole direct-source inventory first. Output
   cannot drop origins, change counts, lose journal custody or replace output identities.
2. **Runtime switch.** Held views refuse a changed version, mismatched siblings and hostile markers
   before any field is read. The historical cache rechecks content, descriptors and caller time,
   and returns detached plans.
3. **Preview approval.** A mixed final binds an explicit, completed V2 or V3 preview. A changed
   review or decision, or a late approval, is refused. Historical replay survives later expiry.
4. **Proof target.** The target projection stays the same across input and worker progress. Resealed
   changes to the plan, owner, stage or preview relationship are refused. Portable bounds are
   checked before any getter is read, even when the cache is warm.
5. **Proof closure.** A final and its target share one bootstrap, and preview, approval and source
   keep their original identities. A review hash cannot stand in for the preview, the approval or
   the saved prefix. Carriers, the original inventory and same-ID bodies cannot be substituted.
6. **Proof copies.** Copies keep the original hierarchy and preview inventory and drop unrelated jobs.
   Resealed incomplete or redirected copies cannot change the frozen requirements. The cached
   resolution is bounded and returns detached results, and a warm cache still refuses changed jobs,
   approvals and copies.
7. **Living-script caches.** Warm caches return independent values and refuse resealed plans, missing
   ancestry, substituted receipts and changed physical settings. They read no accessors and hold no
   current grant, time or document authority.
8. **V2 preview review.** Repeated reviews are exact and detached. Changed output metadata gets its
   own review. Changed status, stage, lifetime, capture or accessor bodies are refused even when the
   source validation is warm.
9. **V3 is still refused at admission.** Enqueueing a complete V3 mixed plan throws. So does a V2
   request that carries `currentFilmOrigins` or `currentFilmProof`. The store gains no job.
