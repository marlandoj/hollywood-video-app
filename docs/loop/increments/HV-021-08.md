# HV-021-08 — A scene marked CONTINUOUS is checked against the scene it continues

epic: HV-021
release: 2 "Voice and crew depth"
gate: none new
spend_usd: 0

## Why

The continuity report compared every scene only with itself. `INT. STAIRWELL - CONTINUOUS` picks up
in the same moment the scene before it ends, and the report could not see that:

- MARGUERITE's cast record gives her an oilskin coat in scene 1 and a wet jumper in scene 2. Scene 2
  is CONTINUOUS. She changes clothes between one step and the next, and the report said nothing.
- Scene 1 is headed `- DAY`. Scene 2 is CONTINUOUS and its shot is directed "night". Scene 2's own
  heading states no time, so `time-contradicts-heading` had nothing to compare it with, and the report
  said nothing.

Both are contradictions between things the project already declares. They are the drift detection
Release 2 promises, and finding them needs no picture.

The repair needed a guard too. It holds each look field to the first shot in the scene that states
it. Take scene 1 directed "morning" then "afternoon", and scene 2 (CONTINUOUS) directed "night" then
"day". The repair turned scene 2's "day" shot to "night", so the scene that contradicted the one
before it in one shot now did in two. That is the HV-021-06 defect, one scene boundary over.

## What this increment does

- **`continuityHeadingContinuous`** in `packages/planner/src/continuity.ts`. A heading is CONTINUOUS
  when a segment after the location is exactly that (`INT. HALL - CONTINUOUS`, `INT. HALL-CONTINUOUS`,
  `INT. HALL - CONTINUOUS.`), or when a parenthetical after the location is (`INT. HALL (CONTINUOUS)`,
  `INT. HALL - DAY (CONTINUOUS)`). A trailing Fountain scene number (`#2#`) is ignored. "LATER",
  "MOMENTS LATER", "SAME", "CONT'D" and `- CONTINUOUS (CONT'D)` are not read as continuous.
- **`time-contradicts-previous`** (warning). A CONTINUOUS scene's time against the time of the scene
  before it in the screenplay. A scene's time is its heading's, where the heading states one, and
  otherwise what its shots are directed. A time opposes only when its family is one the scene before
  never declares: a scene before directed both day and night already contradicts itself
  (`look-changed`), and either time agrees with something in it. A heading that opposes names the
  whole scene. Directed
  times name only the shots that oppose. A shot against its own heading is already
  `time-contradicts-heading` and is not reported twice.
- **`wardrobe-contradicts-previous`** (warning). Every character in both scenes whose resolved
  wardrobe (the scene's own entry, else the default) is stated in both and differs. One finding per
  scene, naming each character, both wardrobes and both scenes. Case, spacing, curly against straight
  quotes and a closing full stop are not a change.
- **`continuousComparisons`** on each scene and on the totals. Additive. It counts what could be
  compared, so an empty result reads as "nothing declared" when that is what it is.
- **The repair** (`continuity-repair.ts`) repairs neither new kind. Both are added to
  `CONTINUITY_REPAIR_CONTRADICTIONS`, so the summary names them, and each gets a note. The repair
  compares every CONTINUOUS pair before and after its time-of-day edits, using the same
  `continuityContinuousTime` the report uses. If the edits would make the CONTINUOUS scene oppose
  the scene before in a shot or heading that does not oppose now, both scenes' time edits are held
  back, with a note for each scene that lost one. It repeats until nothing more is held, because
  holding one scene back changes the pair on its other side. Holding back only returns a scene to
  what the report compared, so the check always ends. Other look fields are still held.
- `docs/CONTINUITY.md` describes the check, its limits and what the repair leaves.

The API needs no change: `GET /direction` and the repair review already serve `continuityReport` and
`continuityRepair`.

## Acceptance criteria

1. **A wardrobe that changes across a CONTINUOUS heading is a warning** on the continuing scene. It
   names the character, both wardrobes and both scenes, and marks a project default as one. A
   character wearing the same default in both is not named.
2. **An undeclared value is not a contradiction.** A wardrobe one scene does not state, a time of
   "dusk", and a character in only one of the two scenes produce no cross-scene finding, and no
   comparison is counted.
3. **A time of day that contradicts the scene before is a warning.** It names the opposing shots, or
   the whole scene when the heading opposes. An earlier scene whose heading states no time is read by
   its directed shots.
4. **One defect is reported once.** A shot against its own heading is not reported again against the
   scene before. Two changed characters and two opposing shots are still one finding of each kind.
5. **Headings are read in the forms listed above and no others**, and the same wardrobe typed
   differently ("A fisherman's smock." against "a  FISHERMAN’S smock") is compared and agrees.
   **Nothing is compared where nothing continues.** A CONTINUOUS first scene, a CONTINUOUS scene with
   no character in common with the one before, and a MOMENTS LATER heading produce nothing.
6. **The findings are deterministic and counted.** The same inputs give an equal report. The totals'
   warnings equal the scenes' warnings, and `continuousComparisons` sums the scenes'. Changing only
   the heading to MOMENTS LATER (same cast, same settings) removes both findings, takes two warnings
   off the totals and moves the revision.
7. **The repair does not make a cross-scene contradiction worse, or create one.** On the
   morning/afternoon, night/day film it proposes no time-of-day edit in either scene and still holds
   the key light. Applying it leaves both cross-scene findings exactly as they were. A pair that
   agrees is still repaired as before. A scene before directed "day" then "night", continued by
   "day", is no contradiction, and its night shot is still held to day as on main. Continued by
   "night", that hold would create one, so it is not proposed, and the note says why.
8. **The repair says what it leaves.** Both kinds are in `refused` and in
   `CONTINUITY_REPAIR_CONTRADICTIONS`, each has a note, and the summary names both, with or without
   edits beside them.
9. **The desk serves it.** Over the real `GET /direction`, a cast wardrobe change and then a saved
   night direction each add one warning to the CONTINUOUS scene. The totals and the revision move with
   them, and the repair review names both.
10. Reverting `continuity.ts` and `continuity-repair.ts` fails `continuity-continuous.test.ts` (it
    cannot load), the repair tests below, and the new `continuity-route.test.ts` test. Reverting only
    `continuity-repair.ts` fails three of the five tests in `continuity-repair-continuous.test.ts`:
    the morning/afternoon-night/day hold-back, the notes and summary, and the hold that would create a
    contradiction. Two tests pass either way, as they should, because main repairs them correctly:
    "a CONTINUOUS pair that agrees is still repaired as before" and the day-then-night scene before.
    Against this branch's first cut, five fail: the mixed scene before (twice), the heading forms,
    the typed-differently wardrobe, and the notes.

- Tests: `packages/planner/test/continuity-continuous.test.ts` (1–6),
  `packages/planner/test/continuity-repair-continuous.test.ts` (7–8),
  `packages/api/test/continuity-route.test.ts` (9). The existing continuity, repair, heading,
  contradiction and stand-in heading-time suites pass unchanged.

## Known gaps

- **The Director's desk panel has no words for the new kinds.** HV-021-07 (PR #323,
  `packages/frontend/src/continuity.js`) maps each code to a sentence in `CONTINUITY_KINDS`. Read
  from `origin/loop/HV-021-07`, it falls back to the raw code
  (`CONTINUITY_KINDS[finding.code]??finding.code`), so an unknown kind still shows, with its message
  and shots. It shows as `time-contradicts-previous`, not a sentence. Once both are on main, the
  panel needs two entries. Its "nothing to fix across N comparisons" line adds up only the three older
  counters. A film whose only comparisons are across a CONTINUOUS heading would read as "nothing is
  declared yet", which is unlikely but possible.
- **Only the immediately preceding scene, and only CONTINUOUS.** A chain of CONTINUOUS scenes is
  checked one pair at a time. "SAME", "CONT'D" in a heading, and intercuts are not read as continuous.
  "MOMENTS LATER" is a jump in story time on purpose: time cannot flip day to night in moments, but a
  costume can change, and guessing which applies is what this check must not do.
- **The repair resolves neither new kind on purpose.** Wardrobe is a cast record. For time, choosing
  between two scenes is the creator's call. A hold within a scene can still settle a cross-scene
  contradiction as a side effect, when the scene's first shot agrees with the scene before. The
  hold-back is per pair, not per edit. When one time edit would spread a contradiction, the pair's
  other harmless time edits are held back too, such as "afternoon" to "morning" in the scene before.
- **`rulesVersion` stays 1.** The same inputs now give a different report, which the revision already
  shows. Bumping the literal would change the report's type for every consumer, for a signal nothing
  reads yet.
- **Review corrections (2026-10-01).** The first cut counted a time as opposed when the scene before
  declared any other family. So a scene before directed day and night made every timed shot after it
  a false warning with a self-contradicting message. Its blanket hold-back then lost the night-to-day
  repair main proposes. It also missed `(CONTINUOUS)`, `-CONTINUOUS` and `#n#`, and warned on a
  wardrobe that differed only in typography. All four are fixed and tested as above.
- **Still nothing visual.** This compares declarations across a scene boundary. It does not look at
  a frame.
