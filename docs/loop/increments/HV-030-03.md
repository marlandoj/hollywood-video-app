# HV-030-03 — The studio front door

epic: HV-030 (with HV-039 for the new screens)
release: 1 "Studio" (build order step 3)
gate: G13-202609192200
spend_usd: 0

## Goal

The creator's experience the operator asked for: paste a script, answer the crew, approve three times, share. The detailed panels become the Director's desk, hidden behind an **Advanced** switch.

## What this increment does

**`packages/frontend/src/studio.js`**

- `createStudioFlow` is the logic, with the network injected. It runs pitch → questions → plan → approval 1 (plan and cast) → approval 2 (storyboard and rough cut) → approval 3 (film) → share, plus "ask for changes", and it reports a failed render.
- `initStudio` draws it.
  - Labelled controls, and a polite live status region.
  - Radio pairs for "Sounds good" / "Something else".
  - Text assigned as text, never as markup.

**`packages/frontend/src/index.html`**

- The page opens on `#studio`.
- `#creator-flow` and the aside are the Director's desk behind the **Advanced** switch. The switch is remembered in `localStorage` and never sent anywhere, and a resumed project link opens the desk.
- The review and actor-share pages are unchanged.

**The API**

- `GET /api/studio/app.js`.
- `POST /api/projects/:projectId/crew/approve-cast`, backed by `ProjectService.permitPendingCast` and the PostgreSQL wrapper.
  - It needs the creator's attestation and the reviewed cast version.
  - It permits every pending **original** character in one version.
  - It never permits a real person.
- The read-through answer now carries `expected` versions for the plan step.

**`docs/CREW.md`** gains "The studio front door".

## What this increment does not do

- **Resuming inside the studio.** A reopened project link lands on the Director's desk.
- **Music, production voice, live providers.** Those are later steps of Release 1. Staging renders on mock providers.
- **An accessibility audit.** The new screens follow the existing patterns (labels, live status, 44 px targets), and a WCAG pass on them is part of the release run.

## Acceptance criteria

1. **The whole flow in order,** with the exact sequence of requests. The token is on every call, answers are sent in full with their `expected` versions, and cast approval, the final and the share carry their parameters. Tests: `packages/frontend/test/studio.test.js`.
2. **Nothing is sent without a script and the rights attestation.**
3. **A blocking concern keeps the creator at the pitch,** with the reason and the script kept. `over_format` does not block.
4. **Nothing renders until the creator permits the cast.** The crew never does.
5. **"Ask for changes"** records the decision and goes back to a fresh read-through.
6. **A failed render is reported,** and the flow stays where it was.
7. **The page opens on the studio,** with the desk behind Advanced and the module served. Tests: the same file, and `packages/api/test/frontend-modules.test.ts`, whose pinned sets gain `/api/studio/app.js`.
8. **Cast approval needs the attestation and the version.** It permits in one version, is idempotent, and never permits a real person. The read-through reports its versions. Tests: `packages/api/test/crew-plan-route.test.ts`.
