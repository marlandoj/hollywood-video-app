# HV-022-19 — A render request whose key belonged to another kind of job got that job back

epic: HV-022
release: 2 "Voice and crew depth"
gate: none new
spend_usd: 0

## Why

`POST /projects/:id/jobs` looks the request's idempotency key up among the project's jobs. When it
finds one, it answers with that job (`admitted: false`, HV-030-10) before either backend's admission
runs. It refused a match only for a dialogue replacement, a screenplay proposal or a take plan. Every
other kind of job came back as though it were the render asked for: a voice audition, a sound mix, a
motion graphic, a deliverable, or a render of the other stage. For example:

    {"jobId":"<the audition>","stage":"audio-take","status":"done","admitted":false}  202

Nothing was rendered, and the caller was told its render already existed. A final asked for with
the key its rough cut had used was answered with the rough cut. The audio-take, dialogue, lip-sync,
sound, graphic and delivery routes each refuse a key that belongs to another request.

## What this increment does

- **A key held by a job of another stage is refused with 409**: *"This idempotency key belongs to
  another kind of job. Use a new key."*
- **The same render asked for again with its own key** is still answered with it, as before.

## Acceptance criteria

1. **An audition's key.** A render asked for with a voice audition's key is refused with 409, and the
   audition is not handed back.
2. **A rough cut's key.** A final asked for with its rough cut's key is refused rather than answered
   with the rough cut.
3. **The render's own key.** The same render asked for again with its own key is answered with it:
   202, `admitted: false`.
4. Reverting `server.ts` fails criteria 1 and 2.

- Tests: `packages/api/test/jobs-key-other-kind.test.ts`.

## Known gaps this leaves

- **Two character sheets can share a key.** A character sheet asked for with the key of another
  sheet (same stage, another character or settings) is still answered with that sheet. Sheets have
  no revision this route compares yet.
