# HV-039-04 — The studio replaced the step under the creator and left their focus behind

epic: HV-039
release: 2 "Voice and crew depth"
gate: none new
spend_usd: 0

## Why

The front door has five steps and one `<div>`. Every step is drawn by `body.replaceChildren(...)`,
so the control the creator pressed stops existing the moment it works:

```js
try {await action(); render(); tell("");}
catch (error) {tell(error.message || "Something went wrong. Try again.", true); render();}
```

A browser has nowhere to put focus when the focused element is removed, so it drops it to the start
of the document. Someone who pressed **Approve and draw the storyboard** with a keyboard was
returned to the top of the page, above the heading, with the storyboard several tab stops away —
and with nothing said, because `tell("")` empties the live region on success. A screen-reader user
heard "Starting the storyboard.", then silence, and was left in a document whose contents had
changed underneath them.

That is WCAG 2.4.3 Focus Order (A), and it happened at every step, including the one that matters
most: a pitch the crew **refused** stays on the pitch step, so the refusal was drawn above a form
the creator was no longer standing in.

HV-039-02 gave the studio its live region and HV-039-03 fixed the mask viewport's role. Neither
looked at what happens to focus when a step is replaced, and nothing in the repository did.

## What this increment does

- **One table of step titles**, `STEP_TITLES`, and `arrivalOf(state)` over it — because a refused
  pitch is still the pitch step and must not be announced as a fresh invitation to start.
- **A step's heading is its destination.** `heading(text)` builds the `<h2>`, gives it
  `tabIndex = -1` and records it; the pitch's heading is the page's own `<h1>`, which is given the
  same treatment where it is created. A step that drew no heading of its own would land the creator
  on the page heading, which is the old behaviour rather than a worse one.
- **`run` moves focus there once the step is rebuilt**, on the failing path as well as the
  succeeding one, because the body is replaced either way.
- **The live region is left empty on success.** The heading the creator has just been moved to says
  the step's name; a polite region saying the same words a moment later is the announcement twice.
  What is left for the region is what the heading cannot say — the busy message during the step, and
  the reason when it failed.

## Acceptance criteria

1. **The creator ends each step on the heading of the step they arrived at**: through pitch →
   questions → plan → rough cut, focus is on an `<h2>` that is on the page, carries the name
   `STEP_TITLES` gives that step, and is `tabIndex = -1`.
2. **Nothing is focused before the creator presses anything**: the studio does not steal focus when
   it mounts.
3. **A script the crew refused lands on the refusal**, not on "Bring your script to the studio."
4. **A step that failed leaves the creator on the page with the reason**: focus on the heading of
   what is shown, the error in the live region, and the two not the same words.
5. **The step's name is written once.** Each title in `STEP_TITLES`, and `BLOCKED_TITLE`, appears
   exactly once in `studio.js`; every one of them is made a destination by `heading(...)` — which is
   the whole of the claim about the `final` step, which no test drives as far as.
6. Removing `arrived.focus()` fails criteria 1, 3 and 4; keeping it and making `heading` an ordinary
   `<h2>` again fails 1, 3 and 4 as well, for a different reason.

- Tests: `packages/frontend/test/studio-focus.test.js`, driving `initStudio` against a DOM stub that
  records what was focused. The flow is not involved: `createStudioFlow` was always correct here, and
  it is the drawing that dropped the creator.

## Known gaps this leaves

- **A step that does not change still sends the creator to the top of it.** Sharing the film leaves
  the `final` step in place and rebuilds it, so focus goes back to "Approval 3 of 3: your film" and
  the review link that was just created is several stops below. It is predictable and it is where
  the old code left nobody at all, but the link is what the creator wanted.
- **`.status[data-state="success"]` is still an unused rule.** The stylesheet has carried a success
  colour for the status line since HV-039-01 and nothing has ever set that state; this increment
  deliberately does not, because the success message it would carry is the one the heading now says.
- **Only the front door is covered.** The Director's desk panels each replace their own subtrees and
  none of them was looked at here; `busy.js` is the only a11y helper they share, and it is about
  `aria-busy` rather than focus.
- **Nothing tests the live region's announcement itself**, only what is written into it. That it is
  a `role="status"` with `aria-live="polite"` is asserted; that a screen reader speaks it is not
  something this repository can assert.
- **The `<h1>` is the pitch step's heading and the page's heading at once.** It says "Bring your
  script to the studio." above every step, including the final film. That was true before and is now
  also what the creator is returned to when a pitch fails.
