# HV-030-04 — The Release 1 run

epic: HV-030 (the studio), closing Release 1 "Studio" (build order step 8)

## Goal

Release 1's exit criteria, run on private staging:

1. A creator takes a pasted script to a shared film, for one reel and one short, by conversation and three approvals, without opening the Director's desk.
2. A reviewer on a second device opens the review link, watches, and approves or requests changes.
3. Consented casting and the public-figure refusal hold for everything the crew writes.
4. Generation spend stays under the $450 alert. Crew spend is reported on its own line.
5. The operator acknowledges the release (G6).

## What this increment does

- **The scripts.** Two original scripts written for the run, in `docs/evidence/release-1/scripts/`: "The Last Bus" (reel) and "Paper Boats" (short).
- **The driver.** `scripts/studio-run.ts` reports the finishing notes (voices, score, titles) and saves the project token the moment the project exists, because that token is the only key to the film and the share link is made from it after the run.
- **The runs.** Both films run on staging with `live-film-anchored`, Azure voices on and titles on.
- **The evidence** goes in `docs/evidence/release-1/release-run.json`: the jobs and their timings, the cast and its voices, the ledger before and after (spend and holds, with what the holds are), the crew's own line, every attempt that stopped and why, the fixes the run forced, the finishing notes, and the review links' decisions. Criterion 2 is completed by the operator on his phone over the tailnet.

## Acceptance criteria

1. **Script to shared film, twice, through the front door.** Both films have a `done` shared job,
   the three approvals in order, and never opened the Director's desk.
2. **What the crew could not finish, the crew says.** Every finishing note in the record matches a
   gap the record itself reports; nothing is quietly dropped.
3. **A reviewer decided.** Each review link is bound to the shared job, was opened, and carries a
   decision of `approved` or `changes_requested`.
4. **Consented casting.** Every character is `original-fictional`, and the safety tests the record
   names exist in the repository.
5. **Spend.** Spend plus holds stay under the $450 alert, every attempt is counted, each film stays
   inside its own cap, and the crew's line is $0 (the stand-in crew).
   - Test for all five: `test/release-1-run.test.ts`.
