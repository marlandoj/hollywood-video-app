# HV-022-01 — The final speaks with the rough cut's approved dialogue

epic: HV-022 (performance: voice and dialogue)
release: 1 "Studio" (build order step 6, first part)
gate: G13-202609192200
spend_usd: 0

## Why

Both live reels (`docs/evidence/release-1/`) had silent finals. Kling is asked for picture only, and only the rough cut speaks (eSpeak today, Azure from the next increment). The creator approves a rough cut that talks and then gets a film that doesn't.

## What this increment does

- **`packages/queue/src/final-dialogue.ts`:**
  - `roughCutDialogue` decides whether a final shot qualifies:
    - the shot has no sound of its own;
    - the approved rough cut's record for the same shot has a speech receipt and a voice file;
    - the frame counts are equal;
    - the record validates, and its lines match this shot's (`assertSpeechInput`).
  - `carryRoughCutDialogue` gives any other silent final shot a silent track and marks it `silent-captioned`, so a cut that mixes voiced and silent shots assembles and can be re-voiced. For a qualifying shot it copies the voice file, verifying size and SHA-256. It reads the local file inside the source job, or goes through the artifact store on S3. It then muxes the voice under the final picture and sets the speech receipt and `audioMode: "provided"`.
- **`packages/queue/src/worker.ts`:** ordinary finals call it before sealing each shot. Living-script, current-film, take groups and character sheets are unchanged.
- **Docs:** `docs/CREW.md`.

## What this increment does not do

- **No Azure voices yet.** That is the next increment: the crew casts voices, and takes plus dialogue replacement run.
- **No new route, schema or migration.**

## Acceptance criteria

1. **Through the studio flow** (real server, worker, closed fal fixture, eSpeak):
   - The spoken final shot's speech receipt and voice file SHA-256 equal the rough cut's.
   - The shot without lines gets a silent track and is marked `silent-captioned`, as the rough cut's silent shots are.
   - The voiced final is accepted as a source for dialogue replacement, where HV-022-03 lays the production voices.
   - The exported film has an AAC track with the voice audible.
   - Test: `packages/api/test/final-dialogue.test.ts`.
2. **Only an exact match is carried.** Another length, a shot with its own sound, or no approved rough cut each leave the shot unchanged.
   - Test: `packages/api/test/final-dialogue.test.ts`.
3. **Nothing else changes.** Because `packages/queue` changed, the full suite runs locally.
