# HV-027-14 — Deliverables were still served, listed and made after their film was withdrawn

epic: HV-027
release: 2 "Voice and crew depth"
gate: none new
spend_usd: 0

## Why

A deliverable (a 1:1 or 9:16 reframe, or a mezzanine) is made of the frames of a picture edit or an
assembly. Its permission check, `assertDeliveryPermission`, read only the project's permission:
rights attested and deletion date not passed. It never looked at the film the deliverable was made
from. Every sibling that reuses a finished cut (sound mixes, editorial, lip-sync) runs the cut's own
check. The result:

- **After a character's likeness permission was revoked,** the cut's own media link answered 404,
  but its reframe still downloaded (200). The deliverables list still showed the reframe as
  available, and a new mezzanine of the same cut was admitted and rendered.
- **After the cut's retention link had lapsed,** the sound-mix and editorial routes refused the cut,
  but delivery still offered it and admitted and made a mezzanine. That mezzanine was a fresh 30-day
  copy of a film the studio had stopped keeping.

## What this increment does

- **`assertDeliverySourcePermission`** runs the source film's own edit or assembly permission check.
  It is read everywhere a deliverable is:
  - listed (the view shows the deliverable as unavailable, with the reason);
  - served (the artifact route);
  - offered and admitted;
  - rendered (on both the JSON and the PostgreSQL worker paths).
- **`assertDeliverySourceRetained`**: a new deliverable is offered or admitted only from a film that
  is still retained.
- **An existing deliverable keeps its own link.** Its expiry is unchanged by the source's.

## Acceptance criteria

1. **Revoked character.** After a character's permission is revoked:
   - the cut's deliverable answers 404;
   - it is listed with no output and a reason;
   - a new deliverable is refused, and nothing is queued.
2. **Lapsed retention.** A cut whose retention link has lapsed is not offered (409). A new
   deliverable of it is refused (409), and nothing is queued.
3. **Retained cut.** A retained cut with its permission intact is offered and delivered, and the
   deliverable is served, as before.
4. Reverting the source files fails criteria 1 and 2.

- Tests: `packages/api/test/delivery-source-permission.test.ts`.

## Known gaps this leaves

- **The PostgreSQL worker's new check has no test here.** It runs the same planner function inside
  its existing transaction, but only the JSON paths are exercised by the test.
