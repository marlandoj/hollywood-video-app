# HV-025-06 — Dialogue replacement and assembly renders keep the same clock

epic: HV-025
release: 1 "Studio"
gate: none new
spend_usd: 0

## Why

HV-025-05 found the picture edit re-running its whole lease-and-permission check once per verified
file, and put that check on a one-second clock. Two workers were left with the old shape, and the
Release 1 run hit one of them.

On staging, "Paper Boats" (short, 10 shots, 11 spoken lines, Azure voices) reached
`dialogue-replacement` at 15:42 UTC and was still running 30 minutes later, with no FFmpeg process
and the worker's bun process burning CPU. The reel ("The Last Bus", 4 shots, 3 lines) took 14 s for
the same stage the same morning. `replaceLockedDialogue` calls its `access()` gate for every shot,
every retained line and every converted read, and each call ran:

- `PostgresCostLedger.assertDialoguePermission` (or, on the JSON store, a project read, the source
  job's availability, `assertDialogueAccess` and every audition input), and
- a lease heartbeat.

So the stage grew roughly with the square of the film's lines, exactly as the picture edit did with
graphic frames. `processEditAssemblyJob` has the same `access()` shape, called per retained file by
`verifyEditAssemblyMedia` and `renderEditAssemblyJob`.

## What this increment does

- **`processDialogueJob`** (`packages/queue/src/dialogue-worker.ts`) and **`processEditAssemblyJob`**
  (`packages/queue/src/edit-assembly-worker.ts`) take their gate from `throttledEditAccess`
  (HV-025-05, unchanged):
  - the abort and deadline checks still run on every call;
  - the lease renewal and the permission check run at most once a second;
  - the check is forced at admission, before a checkpoint and before completion;
  - a failed check is never remembered as passed, and concurrent callers share one in-flight check.

Every checksum is still verified, per file and per line. Revoking a project's permission or taking
its source down still stops the job within about a second, and always before its checkpoint or
completion. No storage, plan or output shape changes.

## Acceptance criteria

1. **The dialogue worker's check runs on a clock, not once per line.** The ADR job's heartbeats are
   counted over a real worker run: at most its elapsed seconds plus 8, and at least one. The job
   still completes with the replaced line and the retained line's PCM.
   - Test: `packages/api/test/dialogue-replacement.test.ts`, "the dialogue worker's lease and
     permission check runs on a clock, not once per line".
2. **The assembly worker's check runs on the same clock.** The PostgreSQL/S3 assembly render counts
   the interrupted run's heartbeats under the same bound, and still checkpoints and recovers.
   - Test: `packages/storage/test/assembly-renders.test.ts`.
3. **Nothing else changes.** The dialogue, narration, dubbing, editorial and assembly suites pass
   unchanged.
