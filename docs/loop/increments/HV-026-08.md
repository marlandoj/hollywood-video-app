# HV-026-08 — A creator can grade a cut from the studio

epic: HV-026
release: 2 "Voice and crew depth"
gate: none new
spend_usd: 0

## Why

HV-026-07 let the API make, check and withhold a grade, but nothing on the page could ask for one.
The delivery routes also took a cut's job id and assumed the caller had it: nothing listed the cuts a
deliverable could be made from, so a page had nothing to offer the creator.

## What this increment does

- **`GET …/deliveries` lists `sources`.** These are every finished picture edit and assembly, newest
  first. A cut that can be delivered from carries its size and length. One that cannot (lapsed
  retention, revoked cast permission, a seal a deliverable cannot bind to) is listed with the reason
  the offer route would give, rather than left out.
- **A "Color grade" panel** (`packages/frontend/src/color-grade.js`, served at `/api/color-grade.js`):
  - lists the cuts, and a cut that cannot be graded is shown with its reason and cannot be chosen;
  - loads one;
  - reads eight labelled number fields, bounded and stepped as the API says, each with its hint
    attached by `aria-describedby`, and a look with its description;
  - asks for the grade.
- **Request keys.** A request key is kept per decision until the server answers, so a retry after a
  dropped connection is the same grade. A changed decision is a new request. A field that is not a
  number is refused on the page.
- **The cut's grades are listed.** Each one shows its look and the controls it moved:
  - offered: a download link;
  - withheld: no link, and every reason and remedy;
  - notes either way.

  A grade in progress is polled until it finishes, on the sound studio's poll clock and stall limit.
- **Wiring.** The page wires the panel beside "Titles and graphics", with 44-pixel controls. Closing
  it returns focus to its opener.
- **Docs.** `COLOR-GRADE.md` gains the panel and the sources list. `DELIVERY-JOBS.md` notes the
  first delivery frontend.

## Acceptance criteria

1. **Sources.** `GET …/deliveries` lists a finished picture edit, with its 640 by 360 size and one
   second, and does not list the film it was cut from. After the cut's retention lapses it is still
   listed, `unavailable`, with the same sentence the offer route answers 409 with.
2. **Choosing a cut.** The panel lists the cuts and disables the unavailable one. With no gradeable
   cut, "Load cut" is disabled and the status says why, before any request.
3. **Grading.** Every control is a labelled number field at neutral with the API's bounds and step.
   "Grade this cut" posts `{kind:"grade", grade}` exactly as the fields read:
   - a retry after a failed request reuses the request key;
   - a changed decision uses a new one;
   - an empty field is refused with nothing sent.
4. **Listing grades.** A withheld grade shows every withholding reason and no link. An offered grade
   links its file and shows its notes. A mezzanine of the same cut is not listed among the grades.
5. **Polling.** A running grade is polled until it is done, and the poll then stops.
6. The page and the module walk still pass:
   - the module is served, and it is in the pinned lists of reached modules;
   - the contrast check finds no offender among the four new operable rules.
7. Reverting `packages/frontend/src/color-grade.js` fails criteria 2 to 5. Reverting
   `packages/api/src/delivery-api.ts` fails criterion 1.

- Tests: `packages/frontend/test/color-grade.test.js`, `packages/api/test/delivery-sources.test.ts`.
  Updated: `packages/api/test/frontend-modules.test.ts`, `packages/frontend/test/contrast.test.ts`.

## Known gaps this leaves

- **The panel shows no picture.** There is no before-and-after still and no scope; a grade is judged
  by its check and by downloading it.
- **The page wiring has no browser test.** The module is tested on the repository's small DOM, and
  the page's import of it is covered by the module walk, not by a rendered page.
- **Reframes, mezzanines and burned captions still have no panel,** though `sources` now lists what they can be made
  from.
