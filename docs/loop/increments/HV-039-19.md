# HV-039-19 — Opening subject motion for a second shot showed, saved and exported the first shot's plan

epic: HV-039
release: 2 "Voice and crew depth"
gate: none new
spend_usd: 0

## Why

Each shot's **Plan subject motion for …** button calls `open(plan)`. That points the panel at the
new shot and reads its context:

    shotId=plan.source.id;maxShots=limit;panel.hidden=false;if(!dirty)await reload(false);

`reload` runs inside `run()`, and `run()` drops any step that arrives while another is in flight:

    async function run(action){if(busy)return;busy=true;…

Suppose the owner pressed shot B's button while shot A's context was still loading. `shotId` became
B, and the read for B was dropped. A's reply arrived after that and became `context`. From then on,
the panel showed A but every request went to B:

| What the owner saw or did | What happened |
|---|---|
| The source line | said "Current source · A: …" and showed A's subjects and points |
| **Save movement plan** | sent A's source hash, image and points to `/B/subject-motion` |
| **Download native inputs** | asked for B's packet under A's saved revision |

Nothing on screen said that B had never loaded. The same thing happened if B was opened while any
other step, such as a download, held the panel.

## What this increment does

- **Each context read names the shot it was made for.** If the panel has moved on to another shot
  by the time the reply comes back, the reply is dropped.
- **A read that `open` asks for while the panel is busy is remembered.** It runs as soon as the
  current step finishes, so the shot that was opened last is the one the panel loads. As with
  `open` itself, this only happens when there is no unsaved movement draft.

## Acceptance criteria

1. **A late reply for the first shot does not become the second shot's context.** The panel says
   "Current source · B".
2. **The second shot's context is read once the first read finishes, and only then.** The panel
   then says "Movement plan loaded." and has no unsaved work.
3. **Download native inputs after the switch** asks for B under B's saved revision.
4. **Save movement plan after the switch** sends B's source hash, image and prompt to
   `/B/subject-motion`.
5. **Opening B while A's download is in flight** still finishes A's download, then reads and shows
   B's context.
6. Reverting `subject-motion.js` fails criteria 1 to 5.

- Tests: `packages/frontend/test/subject-motion-shot-switch.test.js`.

## Known gaps this leaves

- **Steps other than a context read can still reach across the switch.** Suppose the owner opens
  another shot mid-step:
  - during the `prepare()` of **Prepare private image**, the upload goes to the newly opened shot;
  - during **Remove saved movement plan**, the removal goes to the first shot, but its follow-up
    read and **Restore removed plan as draft** land on the second.

  `open` could refuse while the panel is busy instead, but that would turn a quick second click into
  an error.
- **The shared test DOM still lacks `options`, `checkValidity` and `click`.** This test adds them for
  its own run, as the other subject-motion tests do.
