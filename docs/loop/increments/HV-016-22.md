# HV-016-22 — A wrapped parenthetical in a PDF turned the whole speech into action

epic: HV-016
release: 2 "Voice and crew depth"
gate: none new
spend_usd: 0

## Why

The PDF importer recognised a parenthetical by its text alone, one line at a time, `/^\(.*\)$/`. A
parenthetical too long for its column is printed over two lines — `(quietly, looking at` / `the door)`
— and neither half is bracketed at both ends. Both fell through to the last rule, "everything else is
action", which opens a new paragraph. That cut the speech in two:

- the cue had nothing under it, so the parser read it as action (HV-016-06);
- the lines said after the parenthetical were appended to the action paragraph;
- MAYA said nothing, and the film gained action shots reading her cue, her direction and her line as
  scene description.

## What this increment does

- **A run at the parenthetical margin, from an opener to a line ending `)`, is one parenthetical**
  (`packages/parser/src/pdf.ts`). It is dropped and reported, as every parenthetical already is: the
  studio's screenplay has no parenthetical element, and one left in a speech would be spoken and
  captioned. The speech around it stays whole.
- **An opener that never closes at that margin is read as before.**

## Acceptance criteria

1. **Two lines.** A cue, a two-line parenthetical and a line of dialogue import as MAYA's speech of that
   line. The scene's action is only the action, the transition survives, the parenthetical's words
   are not in the text, and the "parentheticals" note is given.
2. **Three lines, mid-speech.** A three-line parenthetical between two lines of dialogue leaves one
   speech of both lines and no action.
3. **Unclosed.** An opener followed by a dialogue-margin line keeps both lines' words and gives no
   parenthetical note.
4. Reverting the source file fails criteria 1–2; criterion 3 is the old behaviour and holds either way.

- Tests: `packages/parser/test/pdf-wrapped-parenthetical.test.ts`.

## Known gaps this leaves

- **Parentheticals are still dropped, not kept.** The Fountain the importer writes has no place for
  them outside the speech; HV-016-21 reads a wrapped one written by hand.
- **A wrap across a page break,** with a page number or header between the halves, is still two
  lines of action.
- **A parenthetical printed at the dialogue margin** is recognised only when it fits on one line.
