# HV-030-31 — Release 3's run driver and contract (Release 3 step 14)

epic: HV-030
release: 3 "Features"
gate: G20-202610031349 (Release 3's exit criteria, parts, spend lines and build order agreed; a feature's film limit of $150; the anchored picture profile; the look approved once, then rough cut and final per sequence)

## Why

Build-order row 14: "As HV-030-22 did for Release 2: the driver, the record's schema and the contract
test, proved on a synthetic fixture". Release 3's criteria were agreed at G20, and the roadmap said a
contract test "will hold that record". Nothing could yet:

- **`scripts/studio-run.ts` made one render.** A feature needs the look approved once, then each
  sequence's rough cut and final, then the join, and the one joined film shared.
- **Locks and continuity come too late after the front door.** Criterion 2 needs every shot of a
  locked character, in every sequence, rendered from its lock, and each continuity finding repaired
  before the final. Release 2 locked a look after its films were made. A feature's first sequence
  renders the moment its look is approved, so both have to happen between the plan and that approval.
- **Nothing read a feature back**: its sequences, the joined film, each final's providers and
  routing, the style bible, the locks per shot, the sequence boundaries, a hero render, or which
  sequence a reviewer's comment falls in.
- **Nothing held a record to Release 3's criteria.**

This increment builds the tooling and the test, and a screenplay for the rehearsal and the run. It
runs nothing on staging and spends nothing. Step 15 (the mock rehearsal) and step 16 (the release run)
use it, with the runbook below.

## What this increment does

  original, with five fictional speaking parts (WREN, OSWIN, DEV, MARISOL, HOLLIS) and no real person,
  brand or company. It has 34 scenes and 46 lines of dialogue (1,847 characters). The feature's
  own shot plan (`featureShots`) reads it as 202 shots, and the Showrunner's stand-in splits it into
  ten sequences of 9 to 24 shots. Sequence 5 opens `INT. RUINED CHAPEL - DAWN - CONTINUOUS` from
  sequence 4's `EXT. SANDBANK - DAWN`, so the Supervisor has a boundary to compare across.
  `test/release-3-scripts.test.ts` checks it against the parser, every shot prompt against the safety
  gate, the read-through's facts as a feature, the stand-in split, and the Supervisor's report.
- **`scripts/studio-run.ts --format feature`** drives the whole feature through the front door, as
  the studio page does (`createStudioFlow`):
  - the pitch, the read-through and the plan;
  - the look, approved once;
  - each sequence's rough cut and final in turn ("Approve sequence k and make sequence k+1's rough
    cut");
  - the Editor's join with title and credits, after the last sequence;
  - `--share N`, which shares the one joined film.

  The report gains the read-through's quote (format, scenes, shots, runtime, limit, cost), the
  Showrunner's split and the style bible by revision, and each sequence's rough cut and finished film.
  A join that stops is a stopped run, never the last sequence shared as the feature. A reel's and a
  short's report is unchanged apart from the read-through's quote.
- **Two desk steps before the look** (`scripts/release-3-desk.ts`, surface `desk-api`, reported as
  `deskBeforeLook`):
  - `--lock NAME[,NAME]` approves the cast (the look approval's own first step, with the version the
    plan read) and locks each named character's look. A turnaround character sheet comes first when
    the character has no reference image, as the desk's "Generate character sheets" does.
  - `--continuity-repair` reviews the Supervisor's repair and applies exactly the edits it shows.

  After its own permission step, the studio flow is told the cast is approved, so it doesn't send the
  step again with a cast version the lock has moved on.
- **`scripts/release-3-run.ts`** reads the feature back and drives the desk parts. It writes the
  record, schema `hv-release-run/3`.
  - **The front door, read back** (`front-door`):
    - the joined film's job, its plan of finals and films, its title and credits, and its language;
    - its `provenance.json`, for each sequence's start and the credits' length;
    - each sequence's final, for its rough cut, its style-bible revision, its provider plan and its
      route decisions. A shot's provider is the last decision that selected one.
    - Steps: `pitch-to-shared-feature`, `feature-format`, `showrunner-sequences`,
      `feature-assembly`, `titles-credits`, `style-bible` (`GET /style-bible`: every sequence made

      shots rendered from a lock), `quality-routing` (every final's plan pins a results file, with no

    - A live shot not on fal stops `second-vendor` unless `--second-vendor-gate` names the G3 entry.

    - `--continuity`: the Supervisor's report, with each sequence boundary. `--continuity-apply`

    - `--hero [shot] --hero-sequence N`: a hero render of one shot of a sequence's final, waited for.

    - `--camera`: each final's camera paths, native or cropped, and why.
    - `--vfx-job <id>`: a finished composite job of this feature.
    - `--provenance --verify-c2pa`: the joined film's sidecar against its record, verified with

    - the per-film and operator snapshots;
    - `--acknowledged G6-…` once the operator has acknowledged.

## Runbook

On the host, from the active release. Every output goes under `R`, which only its owner can read. No
command prints a key, a token or a link. Long steps run detached and are polled.

```sh
. /etc/rough-cut/host.env
REL=$(cat "$RC_RUNTIME/active-release.txt"); BUN="$RC_RUNTIME/bin/bun"; R="$RC_RUNTIME/runs/release-3"   # release-3-rehearsal for step 15
install -d -m 700 "$R"; umask 077; cd "$REL"
S=docs/evidence/release-3/scripts; BASE=http://127.0.0.1:8081
lines() { ( set -a; . "$RC_RUNTIME/storage-api.env"; set +a; "$BUN" scripts/release-2-lines.ts --out "$R/lines-$1.json" ); }
```

1. **Lines before:** `lines before`.
2. **The feature, at the front door.** WREN and OSWIN, the two parts in most scenes, are locked
   before the look. The Supervisor's repair is applied before the look.

   ```sh
   nohup "$BUN" scripts/studio-run.ts --base $BASE --script $S/feature.fountain --format feature \
     --lock WREN,OSWIN --continuity-repair --share 5 --out "$R/f.json" > "$R/f.log" 2>&1 &
   echo $! > "$R/f.pid"
   ```

   Poll with `tail -n 2 "$R/f.log"; kill -0 $(cat "$R/f.pid") 2>/dev/null && echo running`. When done:
   - `outcome` is `completed`;
   - `feature.joined` is `true`, and there are ten sequences;
   - `readThrough.facts.shots` is 202;
   - `deskBeforeLook.locks` has two locks.

   Between sequences, compare `lines` readings. If the generation line's spent passes $100 before the
   last sequence (step 16), or moves at all (step 15), stop.
3. **A diagnostics credential:** `( set -a; . "$RC_RUNTIME/secrets.env"; set +a; "$BUN" scripts/operator-diagnostics.ts "$R/operator-1.json" $BASE )`.
4. **Behind the front door:**

   ```sh
   "$BUN" scripts/release-3-run.ts --base $BASE --out "$R/run-1.json" \
     --feature "$R/f.token" --studio "$R/f.json" --script $S/feature.fountain \
     --spend-declared 120 --lines-before "$R/lines-before.json" \
     --continuity --interchange --hero --camera --provenance --verify-c2pa \
     --operator-token "$R/operator-1.json"
   ```

   For step 15, use `--spend-declared 0`. List the steps with
   `python3 -c 'import json; [print(s["step"], s["outcome"], s.get("note","")) for s in json.load(open("'"$R"'/run-1.json"))["steps"]]'`.
   An `unavailable` names its reason. A `stopped` is a defect, fixed in its own increment before going
   on.
5. **Lines after:** `lines after`.
6. **Kevin reviews on his phone** (below). Step 15 may skip this.
7. **Read back the review, and make the record:**

   ```sh
   ( set -a; . "$RC_RUNTIME/secrets.env"; set +a; "$BUN" scripts/operator-diagnostics.ts "$R/operator-2.json" $BASE )
   "$BUN" scripts/release-3-run.ts --base $BASE --merge "$R/run-1.json" --out "$R/run-2.json" \
     --feature "$R/f.token" --studio "$R/f.json" --script $S/feature.fountain \
     --reviews --lines-after "$R/lines-after.json" --operator-token "$R/operator-2.json" \
     --evidence HV-037.paid-benchmark=docs/evidence/release-3/<the paid benchmark's record> \
     --defer <part>=<gate> ...
   ```

   Each deferral cites an entry that exists. Expected:
   - `HV-019.second-vendor` to G20-202610031349, unless a G3 entry approves a vendor;
   - `HV-020.native-camera` and `HV-025.vfx-composite` to the entry recording Kevin's decision;
   - `HV-023.interchange`, unless a saved cut of the feature exists (Known gaps);
   - any part whose increment hasn't deployed.
8. **Check it.** For step 15: `"$BUN" test/release-3-contract.ts "$R/run-2.json" --rehearsal`. Its
   findings, and anything that stopped, are what step 15 is for. For step 16, the build session copies
   the record to `docs/evidence/release-3/release-run.json`, and `bun test test/release-3-run.test.ts`
   must pass locally before anything is pushed. If it fails, the record is not edited to pass. The
   cause is fixed, or the run is redone.

`f.token`, `f.review`, `operator-*.json` and the lines files stay in `R`. They never enter the
repository or the chat.
