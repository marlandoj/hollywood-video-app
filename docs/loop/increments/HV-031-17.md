# HV-031-17 — The films the studio shares are signed too

epic: HV-031
release: 2 "Voice and crew depth"
gate: none new
spend_usd: 0

## Why

The Release 2 exit run on staging (2026-10-01 23:15 UTC) had `HV_C2PA_SIGNING_KEY` and
`HV_C2PA_SIGNING_CERT` set, and the worker's startup check passed. Even so, every shared film's
export came back with `credentialType: null` and no `provenance.c2pa`.

The crew flow shares the Editor's titled, credited final. That is a `picture-edit` job, with HV-025
titles from the graphics renderer. HV-031-15 signed only the assembler's exports: the film, the mixed
film and each take. The picture edit writes its own `provenance.json` schema, and that record carried
no credential block at all, signed or unsigned.

A review link isn't limited to the picture edit, either. It can be bound to an assembly, a sound mix,
or a dialogue or lip-sync version. Each of those writes its own record too, and none was signed.
Release 2's exit criterion 5 needs every shared film's export to carry a `provenance.c2pa` whose bytes
match its record and which `scripts/verify-c2pa.ts` reads as valid.

## What this increment does

- **Five more stages sign their exports, the same way the assembler does.** They are the picture
  edit, the assembly, the sound mix, the dialogue replacement and the lip-sync version.
  - Each signs with the same signer. The sidecar is a C2PA manifest store beside the stage's own
    `provenance.json`, bound by BMFF hash to the stage's MP4, and the MP4 is never rewritten.
  - The signed assertion names the stage's own record schema (`hv-edit-result/1`,
    `hv-edit-assembly-result/1`, `hv-sound-result/n`, `hv-dialogue-replacement-result/n`,
    `hv-lipsync-result/1`) and the MP4's sha256.
  - One call does it: `exportCredentials` in `packages/assembler/src/export-credentials.ts`. It signs
    with `signC2paSidecar`, off the event loop and under the job's cancellation signal.
- **Each record carries the same credential block as the assembler's.** It is the signed form naming
  the sidecar's sha256 when the host holds the key, and the unsigned C2PA-style form when it holds
  none. A picture edit made without a key now says it is unsigned. Before, it said nothing at all.
- **The key is checked before anything is encoded.** Each stage loads the signer at its entry
  (`exportC2paSigner`). An expired or EKU-less certificate refuses the job before it renders, naming
  the fix, in addition to the worker's startup check. The lip-sync worker checks again before its
  paid provider call, so a lapsed certificate doesn't pay for a version it then can't sign.
- **The sealed output names the sidecar.** Each stage's output gets `c2paPath`, beside its own
  `provenance.json`, and the sidecar is in the stage's own file inventory. So the sidecar goes
  wherever the stage's media already goes:
  - checkpoint and publish;
  - the artifact route (served as `application/c2pa`);
  - the `c2paUrl` link on the job view and on a review link;
  - artifact import and restore (with the digest comparison HV-031-15 added);
  - snapshots, and the retained-cut receipt.
  A sound mix's or picture edit's retained copy of a signed source carries that source's sidecar too.
- **Every validator checks the record against the output.** A stage's output validator calls
  `exportSidecarProblem` (in `packages/planner/src/provenance.ts`). The validators are
  `validateEditOutput`, `validateEditAssemblyOutput`, `validateSoundOutput`, `validateDialogueOutput`
  and `validateLipSyncOutput`. They accept exactly two forms: signed with the sidecar beside the
  record, or unsigned with none. They refuse a sidecar that is dropped, moved, or named by other bytes,
  a signed record with no sidecar, and an unsigned record with one. Keys are compared as a set,
  because jsonb reorders them.
- **Older records keep working.** A record made before this increment has no credential block. It is
  accepted only when its output claims no sidecar, so retained jobs and snapshots from before this
  increment still load.
- **Later stages accept a signed source.** `validateDialogueReplacementReport` accepts the block, so
  a lip-sync over a signed dialogue version, and a dialogue version over another, read it back.
- **`scripts/verify-c2pa.ts` reads an editorial export's directory.** It is the directory holding
  `provenance.json`, whose MP4 is one level down in `conform/export.mp4`.

## Stages deliberately left unsigned

- **Deliverables** (`delivery`: reframes, open captions, grade, mezzanine, SDH). A delivery job
  writes exactly one file and no provenance record, by design (HV-027-04). A review link can't be
  bound to one, because `assertSelectedOutput` refuses it, so it isn't a shared film under exit
  criterion 5. The mezzanine is an FFV1 master, which C2PA's BMFF binding does not cover. Signing
  deliverables needs a record format for them, which is its own increment.
- **Motion graphics** (`motion-graphic`). A graphic is an element, not a film: an RGBA master and its
  `graphic.json`. It can't be bound to a review link. Its pixels reach a shared film only inside a
  picture edit, and that edit is signed.
- **Character sheets and audio takes** are not films.

## Acceptance criteria

1. **The picture edit a review link shares serves a signed sidecar that verifies against the film it
   serves.** The record's credentials are the signed form naming the sidecar's sha256. The review
   link's `c2paUrl` is served as `application/c2pa`. Read against the MP4 the link serves, with the
   issuing root as anchor, it is `Trusted` with no other status. The signed assertion names
   `hv-edit-result/1` and that MP4's sha256.
2. **`scripts/verify-c2pa.ts` passes the picture edit's directory** as `Trusted` with the anchor and
   as `Valid` with only `signingCredential.untrusted` without it.
3. **A signed picture edit's output is refused** with its sidecar dropped, moved beside the MP4,
   named by other bytes, or with its record made unsigned. A deliverable is still made from the
   signed cut.
4. **An assembly is signed beside its own record**, and its review link serves a sidecar that reads
   `Trusted` against the MP4 it serves. Its output is refused with the sidecar dropped.
5. **A dialogue version, a lip-sync over it and a sound mix over that are each signed beside their
   own record.**
   - Each reads `Trusted`.
   - The lip-sync's admitted source carries the dialogue's signed credentials.
   - The sound mix's retained copy of its base includes the lip-sync's sidecar.
   - The mix's review link serves a verifying sidecar, and the other two are linked by `c2paUrl`.
   - Each of the three outputs is refused with its sidecar dropped.
6. **Without a key, nothing is signed and the records say so.** The picture edit, the dialogue
   version and the lip-sync version each carry the unsigned C2PA-style block naming their MP4. None
   has a sidecar, a `c2paPath` or a `c2paUrl`.
7. **A certificate without a C2PA EKU refuses the picture edit before any export is encoded**, naming
   documentSigning (1.3.6.1.5.5.7.3.36).
8. **`exportCredentials` behaves as the stages rely on.**
   - It signs with the host's key from the two variables, and the sidecar reads `Trusted`.
   - Without a key it writes nothing, and its credentials say the export is unsigned.
   - A half-set host or an EKU-less certificate refuses.
   - A cancelled job writes no sidecar.
9. **A stage's record and its sealed output agree exactly when signed or unsigned.** A record with no
   block agrees only with an output claiming no sidecar.
10. **The credential literals are still spelled in exactly one source file.** The provenance importer
    census names the ten new importers.
11. **Reverting the integration fails 7 tests.** The integration is the five stage writers, their six
    validators and `scripts/verify-c2pa.ts`, with `provenance.ts`, `c2pa.ts` and
    `export-credentials.ts` kept. The 7 are every test in `shared-film-c2pa.test.ts` (criteria 1–4, 6,
    7) and `voice-versions-c2pa.test.ts` (5, 6), plus the census (10). A full revert also fails
    `export-credentials.test.ts` (8) and `provenance.test.ts` (9) to load.

- Tests: `packages/api/test/shared-film-c2pa.test.ts` (4), `packages/api/test/voice-versions-c2pa.test.ts`
  (2), `packages/assembler/test/export-credentials.test.ts` (3), and the new case and census in
  `packages/planner/test/provenance.test.ts`. Each signed test uses the throwaway root and leaf HV-031-15's
  `c2pa-fixture.ts` generates with openssl at test time.
  - The suites that import a changed module pass, PostgreSQL-backed ones skipped (no local
    PostgreSQL), in seven batches: 338 pass / 30 skip / 0 fail. They are:
    - the API editorial, assembly, delivery, sound, dialogue, dubbing, lip-sync, narration, graphics,
      takes and zero-cost suites;
    - the generator edit, assembly, dialogue, lip-sync, narration, sound and performance suites;
    - the planner provenance, delivery and edit suites;
    - queue `c2pa-export`, `idle-poll`, `current-film-proof` and `current-film-mixed-media`;
    - assembler `c2pa-sidecar`;
    - storage `snapshots`, `artifacts`, `backups`, `audio-jobs`, `graphics`, `lipsync` and
      `sound-sessions`.
  - `bun run typecheck` and `bun run lint` are clean apart from one warning already on main.

## Operator steps

None new. A host already set up for HV-031-15 signs these exports on its next job. To check one, point
`bun scripts/verify-c2pa.ts` at the directory that holds the job's `provenance.json`
(`<artifact root>/<dirname of output.manifestPath>`). For a picture edit that is
`<project>/<job>/edit-<id>`.

## Known gaps this leaves

- **Deliverables and motion graphics stay unsigned**, for the reasons above.
- **Exports made before this stay unsigned and carry no credential block.** No record is rewritten
  after the fact, and an older record is accepted as unsigned.
- **The signed assertion's `assembledAt` is the signing time for these stages.** None of them records
  an assembly time of its own.
- **Readers check the sidecar's digest, not its signature**, as in HV-031-15. The stage validators
  compare the record to the inventory, and each stage's media verifier checks the inventory's bytes.
  `scripts/verify-c2pa.ts` is the full check.
- **The PostgreSQL and object-store paths were not run locally.** Their checkpoint, publish, import
  and restore are driven by each stage's own file inventory, which now holds the sidecar, and restore
  compares it to its record. CI runs them.
- **The lip-sync worker's check before the provider call has no local test.** That worker needs
  PostgreSQL accounting. The check is the same `exportC2paSigner` call criterion 8 tests.
- **Lip-sync refuses a dropped sidecar through its inventory check** ("unexpected artifact"), which
  runs before the credential check.
- **A refused job is retried under its own policy.** A picture edit refused for its certificate is
  re-queued under its retry policy, and each attempt refuses again before encoding. The worker's
  startup check keeps a misconfigured host from starting at all.
- **The crew's titled picture edit is tested through a film-only sequence.** The signing step is after
  the conform and doesn't depend on the sources. The graphics renderer's titles reach the same
  `renderEditJob`.
- **Signing a stage's export reads its MP4 three times**: one digest here, and the before and after
  digests `signC2paSidecar` already takes.
