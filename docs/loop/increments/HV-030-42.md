# HV-030-42 — A shot reused twice is counted under the job that rendered it

epic: HV-030
release: 3 "Features"
gate: G24-202610062100 (Kevin deferred HV-025.vfx-composite)
spend_usd: 0

## Why

Release 3's live run was joined, shared and approved. Its record (`release-3-run.ts`, then the
contract) said "sequence 1 carries a mock slate in the shared film". `picture.byProvider` for that
sequence was `{"unknown": 8, "fal:kling-o3-standard-reference": 15}`.

Those 8 shots were rendered live on fal. Sequence 1's final was retried three times (HV-030-37
resumes):

- The last retry copied the 8 shots from the retry before it (`reusedFrom`).
- That retry had copied them from the final that rendered them.
- So the middle job holds no route decision for them. `routes()` looked only at `reusedFrom` and found
  nothing.

Every copy keeps the shot's `origin`: the job and shot that rendered it. The API returns it in
`shotRenders`.

The same run also asked for the Editor's VFX composite (`--vfx`). That picture edit ran over three
hours and exhausted the staging VM's memory. Kevin deferred `HV-025.vfx-composite` (G24), and this
branch logs that entry.

## What this increment does

- **`scripts/release-3-run.ts`:**
  - `reusedSources` fetches each reused shot's `origin` job, and its `reusedFrom` job for an older
    record without an origin.
  - `routes` reads a reused shot's decision from its origin first.
- **`test/release-3-driver.test.ts`:**
  - a shot reused twice is counted under its origin's provider;
  - reading only the job it was copied from leaves it unknown, as before;
  - a record without an origin still reads the job it was copied from.
- **`docs/loop/HUMAN-GATES.md`:** G24, Kevin's deferral of the VFX composite.

## Acceptance criteria

1. A final whose shot was reused through a middle job attributes that shot to the provider that
   rendered it.
2. Records without an `origin` read as before.

- Tests: `test/release-3-driver.test.ts`.

## Known gaps

- The slow picture edit behind the VFX composite is not fixed here (G24).
