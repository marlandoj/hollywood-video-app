# HV-024-10 — The generated-music line

epic: HV-024
release: 2 "Voice and crew depth"
gate: G3, approved by Kevin 2026-09-30 and recorded as G15 in docs/loop/HUMAN-GATES.md (ElevenLabs Music on the existing ElevenLabs account; its own $10 line with alerts at $3 and $7)
spend_usd: 0

## Why

Release 2's sound slice promises generated music, and until now the score was only the Composer's
deterministic bed. The operator chose ElevenLabs Music on the account the voice line already
uses: no new vendor, account or key. He gave it a line of its own, so that a score can never spend
the voice budget, and the voice budget can never spend a score's.

## What this increment does

- **`packages/operator/src/music-vendor-budget.ts`** mirrors the voice line:
  - `HV_MUSIC_VENDOR_CAP_USD` defaults to $10 and is never above the monthly cap.
  - `assertMusicVendorBudget` counts spent plus held plus the cue, names the vendor when it
    refuses, and lets a free cue through.
  - `musicVendorAlerts` crosses each of $3 and $7 once, with the cue that crosses it.
- **`musicCueHoldUsd`** prices a cue's hold from the vendor's published rate: $0.15 per generated
  minute on its API pricing page, 2026-09-30. It is a declared constant, not captured evidence. It
  counts whole seconds and rounds up to the cent, and allows at most 600 s.
- **The API reads the setting at startup**, so a nonsense value fails there, next to the voice line.

It raises nothing. The monthly, per-film and per-shot caps are unchanged.

## Acceptance criteria

1. The line is $10 by default, settable, never above the monthly cap, and refuses a nonsense value.
2. It is not the voice line: setting either variable leaves the other's cap unchanged.
3. Spent plus held plus the cue may not pass the line; the refusal names the vendor; a free cue passes.
4. Each alert is crossed once, by the cue that crosses it.
5. A cue's hold follows the published rate, rounded up, and a zero, negative or over-long cue is refused.

- Tests: `packages/operator/test/music-vendor-budget.test.ts`; `voice-vendor-budget.test.ts`
  still passes unchanged.

## Known gaps this leaves

- **Nothing calls the line yet.** Generated music has no admission path. HV-024-11 (the contract,
  the adapter and admission) checks the line inside the reserving transaction and raises the
  alerts as a log warning. It will carry a test that the alerts are read, which is the lesson of
  HV-022-13.
- **The price is declared, not captured.** Before the first live cue, the operator's account and
  price are captured on the host, as the voice catalogue was.
- **Operator to confirm usage-based billing is on**, or music draws on Starter's monthly credits.
