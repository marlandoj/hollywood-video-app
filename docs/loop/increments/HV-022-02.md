# HV-022-02 — The crew casts production voices

epic: HV-022 (performance: voice and dialogue), with HV-030 (the crew)
release: 1 "Studio" (build order step 6, second part)
gate: G11-202609191100 (Azure Speech), G13-202609192200
spend_usd: 0 (voices are assigned, not rendered; synthetic policies only in tests)

## Why

The studio's films speak with the free eSpeak placeholder. G11 chose Azure's standard neural voices. The creator should not have to pick one voice per character, so the Sound persona does it.

## What this increment does

- **`castVoices`** (`packages/planner/src/crew/voice-casting.ts`) picks deterministically from the valid Azure policies (see `docs/CREW.md`). Each assignment is an `hv-audio-voice/2` profile at speed 1.1, and the crew adds a note.
- **The plan route** casts voices with `castVoices` and the script introductions. `applyCrewChanges` sets `audioVoice` only on characters without one, in the same cast version. The response lists `voices`.
- **An unreadable policy file** never stops the plan.

## What this increment does not do

- **No takes or replacement.** Rendering the takes and laying them into the rough cut and final is HV-022-03. That needs the operator's policy file, which needs the Azure resource ID and pricing tier from Kevin.
- **It never sets the eSpeak `voice` or scene performances.** Either one would make every silent final route ineligible.

## Acceptance criteria

1. **The lighthouse script** gives NORA Jane and TEO Guy, with the crew's pace and a Sound note.
   - Test: `packages/planner/test/crew-voices.test.ts`.
2. **Casting is even and deterministic,** and a character of unstated sex may take any voice.
   - Test: `packages/planner/test/crew-voices.test.ts`.
3. **The creator's voice is kept and counted,** and a consented real person gets no synthetic voice.
   - Test: `packages/planner/test/crew-voices.test.ts`.
4. **No voice of the stated sex:** the temporary voice is kept, with a note. A child gets a note too.
   - Test: `packages/planner/test/crew-voices.test.ts`.
5. **Expired, not-yet-valid or missing policies** are never cast.
   - Test: `packages/planner/test/crew-voices.test.ts`.
6. **Over HTTP:**
   - The voices are in the same cast version and listed in the response.
   - With no catalogue, or an unreadable one, the plan works and casts no voice.
   - Test: `packages/api/test/crew-voices-route.test.ts`.
