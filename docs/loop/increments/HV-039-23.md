# HV-039-23 — Every credit row in the graphics desk had the same name, and removing one dropped focus

epic: HV-039
release: 2 "Voice and crew depth"
gate: none new
spend_usd: 0

## Why

Found by the HV-039 accessibility audit (`docs/ACCESSIBILITY-AUDIT.md`, 1.3.1, 2.4.6 and 2.4.3,
Graphics).

A credits graphic is edited as rows. Each row is a `fieldset` holding a "Role" field, a "Name" field
and a "Remove credit" button:

    const row=node('fieldset'),role=field(row,'Role',...),name=field(row,'Name',...);
    row.append(button('Remove credit',()=>{credits.splice(credits.indexOf(record),1);row.remove();changed();}));   // graphic-studio.js

The fieldset has no legend. Take a creator using a screen reader who is editing twelve credits.
They meet twelve "Role" fields, twelve "Name" fields and twelve "Remove credit" buttons, and nothing
says which row any of them is in. Listed by the screen reader's form-controls view, the buttons
cannot be told apart at all.

"Remove credit" also removes the row it sits in, and the focused button with it. Focus falls to the
page body, so a keyboard user who removes three credits starts from the top of the page three times.

## What this increment does

- **Each row is a group named "Credit N"**, a `legend` as the fieldset's first child, and its button
  is "Remove credit N". Rows are renumbered when one goes, so the names always match the order on
  screen and in the render.
- **Removing a row moves focus to the Role field of the row that took its place.** When the last row
  goes, focus moves to the row before it. When no row is left, it moves to Add credit.
- **Adding a row moves focus to its Role field.**
- Focus moves after `run` finishes. `run` disables the editor's fieldset while it works, so focus
  set any earlier would land on a disabled control.
- What is saved is unchanged: each row's role and name, in order. The numbers are only names.
- The DOM stub `audio-studio-dom.js` gains `remove()`, which takes an element off its parent as a
  browser does.

## Acceptance criteria

1. **Named rows.** A credits graphic with three credits shows groups "Credit 1" to "Credit 3", each
   with its Role and Name and a "Remove credit N" button.
2. **Removing a middle row.** Pressing "Remove credit 2" from the keyboard leaves focus on the Role
   field of the former third row, now "Credit 2", never on the removed button. The rows are
   renumbered.
3. **Removing the last row, then the only row.** Focus moves to the row before, and finally to Add
   credit.
4. **Adding a row.** Add credit makes "Credit 4", with empty Role and Name, and focuses its Role.
5. **The save is unchanged.** After a removal and an edit, Save graphic sends the remaining rows'
   roles and names in order.
6. Reverting `graphic-studio.js` fails criteria 1, 2, 3 and 4.

- Tests: `packages/frontend/test/graphic-credits-a11y.test.js`. It opens the real graphics desk on a
  saved credits graphic over the `audio-studio-dom.js` DOM and presses the buttons as a keyboard
  user does: focus, then click.

## Known gaps this leaves

- **The desk's other actions still disable their section while they run.** `syncControls` sets
  `disabled` on the navigation, editor and render fieldsets. Where focus goes when the focused
  control is disabled mid-action is up to the browser, and the audit keeps Graphics 2.4.3 as not
  verifiable for everything except the credit rows.
- **The four section fieldsets still have no legend.** They exist so `disabled` can lock a section,
  not to group fields for a screen reader. Whether each needs a name was not decided here.
- **Focus after a refused addition is not moved.** At 200 rows, Add credit reports the limit
  in the status line and does not move focus, so focus is wherever the browser left it after the
  editor was re-enabled.
