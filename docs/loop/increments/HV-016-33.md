# HV-016-33 — The writer sees the crew's line notes and takes them one at a time in the studio

epic: HV-016
release: 2 "Voice and crew depth"
gate: none new
spend_usd: 0

## Why

HV-016-32 let the crew suggest one-line script changes and let the writer accept them into a new
script version. It built only the routes. Its known gap was the studio:

- The Director's desk, where the screenplay is edited, didn't list the notes.
- There was no Accept or Skip per note, and no "Apply accepted".

So the release-2 promise, "the crew suggests script revisions the writer accepts line by line", still
had no screen.

## What this increment does

- **A "Line notes from the crew" panel under the screenplay** (`packages/frontend/src/line-notes.js`,
  served as `/api/line-notes.js`, wired in `index.html`).
  - **"Ask the crew for line notes"** saves the box first, as the desk's other buttons do. It then
    asks, with the optional "What should the crew work on?" (300 characters).
  - **Each note** shows the line as it is now, the proposed line, the crew member and the reason.
  - **Accept and Skip** are a pair of buttons with `aria-pressed`. Every note starts skipped, and
    pressing them sends nothing.
  - **Without a crew model** the stand-in's own message is shown ("No crew model is connected…"),
    with nothing to take. Dropped notes are counted, never shown.
- **"Apply accepted notes"** is off until a note is accepted.
  - It sends `{version, sha256, notes, acceptedIds}` to `…/crew/line-notes/accept`.
  - One accept is in flight at a time. A second click does nothing.
  - While it is out, the panel is busy (`aria-busy` through `busy.js`, never on the live region) and
    its buttons are off.
  - On success the desk reads the project and reloads the box to the new version.
  - **A 409** shows the server's message, sets the notes aside and is not retried. Any other refusal
    keeps the notes, so the writer can skip the one the server refused.
- **Notes are bound to what they were asked for**: the saved version and the text in the box.
  - The desk now tracks the saved version: from `PUT /script`, from the project on resume, and after
    an editorial screenplay acceptance.
  - Typing in the box, or a new version saved anywhere in the desk, sets the notes aside before
    anything is sent.
  - Notes written against a version the box isn't showing are never shown.
- **Text, never markup.** The crew's words are set with `textContent`.
- This closes HV-016-32's known gap, "No studio panel yet".

## Acceptance criteria

1. **Asking.** The box is saved before the crew is asked, and the trimmed request is sent.
   - Each note shows its line, its proposed line, its crew member and its reason, and starts skipped.
   - Accept and Skip toggle `aria-pressed` and send nothing.
   - The stand-in's message is shown with no notes and no Apply.
   - A refused request shows the server's reason.
2. **Applying.**
   - Apply posts the bound version, its SHA-256, the notes and the accepted ids.
   - The box then reloads to the new version.
   - A double click sends one accept. The panel's parts are `aria-busy` while it is out, and its
     live region is not.
   - A 409 shows the server's message, discards the notes, and sends nothing more.
   - Apply sends nothing while no note is accepted.
3. **Stale notes.**
   - Typing in the box, or a new saved version, sets the notes aside.
   - An apply after either sends nothing.
   - Notes bound to a version the box isn't showing are not shown.
4. **Accessible.**
   - The panel is a section labelled by its heading. The request field has a label.
   - The toggles are real buttons with `aria-pressed`, and the pressed one is filled.
   - Status is in a polite live region.
   - The field and the buttons are at least 44px tall and draw the product's focus ring.
   - Markup in a note is shown as text.
5. **Served.** `/api/line-notes.js` is served, and `frontend-modules.test.ts` pins it as a page entry.
6. Reverting `index.html` fails the two desk tests and the stylesheet test. Removing `line-notes.js`
   fails every test in `line-notes.test.js`. Reverting the route in `server.ts` fails
   `frontend-modules.test.ts` (`/api/line-notes.js -> 404`). Removing, one at a time, the panel's
   stale check, its in-flight guard on Apply, or its 409 handling fails criteria 3, 2 and 2.

- Tests: `packages/frontend/test/line-notes.test.js`. Updated pins: `packages/api/test/frontend-modules.test.ts`,
  `packages/frontend/test/busy.test.js`, `packages/frontend/test/contrast.test.ts` and
  `packages/frontend/test/focus-target.test.ts`. The desk harnesses (`desk-page.js`,
  `studio-resume-desk.test.js`) load the new module.

## Known gaps this leaves

- **Only on the Director's desk.** The studio front door has no script editor after the pitch, so the
  panel lives where the screenplay is edited. Offering line notes between the read-through and the
  plan is not built.
- **A note's line isn't highlighted in the box.** The note names the line number and quotes it; the
  textarea doesn't scroll to it.
- **Notes don't survive a reload.** They are the crew's answer and aren't stored; the writer asks
  again.
