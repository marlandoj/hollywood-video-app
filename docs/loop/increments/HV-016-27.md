# HV-016-27 — Restoring a mixed film from its own bytes, and preparing its proof locally

epic: HV-016
release: 2 "Voice and crew depth"
gate: none new
spend_usd: 0

## Why

PR #83 ("Run mixed films with owned proof and atomic recovery") is being rebuilt on main as small
HV-016 increments. HV-016-25 copies a mixed (V3) film's originals into its own namespace, and
HV-016-26 assembles the film. Two queue-side pieces are still missing. A worker has to rebuild a
mixed checkpoint's clips from the owned rows. And the film's media has to be verifiable from the new
job's bytes alone, with no original or carrier directory present.

The film's proof also has to be prepared somewhere. That proof is the historical jobs, previews and
reference images the film depends on, copied into owned roots and verified. For the local
(file-backed) studio, that happens in the queue.

Admission and claiming still refuse V3. So everything here is reachable only through the pure
functions and a held V3 record placed in a store directly.

## What this increment does

Ported from PR #83:

- **Mixed clips** (`packages/queue/src/current-film-mixed-clips.ts`). The generated and adopted rows
  of a mixed checkpoint become the worker's clips, with no clip manifest or cost receipt. A reader
  serves the selected adopted roles from the prepared originals.
- **Mixed media verification** (`current-film-mixed-media.ts`). The originals, reused roles, fresh
  rows, the assembled picture, captions and compact `hv-provenance/3.0` are checked from the owned
  bytes alone.
- **Local proof** (`current-film-proof.ts`). This covers measuring local proof files one bounded read
  at a time with access checks, preparing the local proof specification and its copies, and a
  synchronous guard that the selected originals and project history are unchanged before the
  checkpoint.
- **Proof media verification** (`packages/generator/src/current-film-proof-media.ts`). The bootstrap,
  V2 final, reviewed preview and reference images are verified from their owned roots, and the
  preview's segment inventory is checked against its real playlist.
- **`snapshotCurrentFilmVersions`** is exported from `current-film-authority.ts` for the proof guard.
- **Held V3 custody in the local store** (`packages/queue/src/index.ts`).
  - `checkpointCurrentFilmOrigins` and `checkpointCurrentFilmProof` save a held V3 job's original
    inventory and prepared proof.
  - A new job carrying `currentFilmProof` is refused.
  - `fail`, `refuse` and the claim check keep the original `startedAt` when only origins or proof
    have been saved.
  - `enqueue` and `claimNext` still refuse V3.

The `current-film-media.ts` edits the PR made came earlier, in HV-016-24 and HV-016-25.

Adapted to main:

- **A held-V3 test fixture** (`packages/queue/test/current-film-v3-held.fixture.ts`). The PR's
  tests enqueued and claimed V3 jobs. This fixture checks that the store refuses both, runs the full
  V3 admission check, and then builds the exact queued or running record the store would have made.
- **The proof lifecycle test** keeps retry (`fail`), `refuse` and cancellation. It now shows that
  reclaiming a prepared V3 job is refused. The expired lease is still recovered first, and the proof
  and `startedAt` survive that. A late holder is refused. The reclaim itself waits for V3 claiming.
- **An accessor-bearing `currentFilmProof` on a request** is refused either by the accessor check or
  by the V2 prepared-proof guard. Either way, no accessor is read.
- **The mixed-media test** passes a real `assembledAt` (HV-016-26).

## Acceptance criteria

1. **Mixed clips.** A generated prefix rebuilds the same clips after a JSON reload, with no costs or
   clip manifest. Adoption reads complete prepared originals after the old carriers are gone. The
   reader refuses unselected tuples, changed bytes, revocation and cancellation. Continuity reads only
   the previous fingerprint.
2. **Mixed media.** A generated prefix, the whole original custody and the reused roles restore with
   no original or carrier namespace. A V3 export verifies its picture, captions and exact provenance.
   Corrupt unselected originals, missing adopted audio and revoked access are refused.
3. **Local proof measurement.** Access is checked on every bounded MiB. A replaced pathname, growth,
   linked parents, wrong sizes, oversized roles and an abort are refused. Only an exact measured
   current-film MP4 reaches the larger size limit.
4. **Local proof guard and checkpoint.** The guard accepts exact selected originals and a live
   `VersionStore`. It refuses a changed selected record, rewritten project history and revoked rights,
   with no checkpoint or state change. A proof-only checkpoint is immutable, detached and restorable.
   A wrong holder, a preparation time in the future or a malformed marker leaves state unchanged.
   Retry, refusal and cancellation keep `startedAt`. A request carrying proof is refused.
5. **Proof media.** Verification uses only the owned roots, and source records and files are not
   rewritten. Missing or bit-flipped roles are refused and never repaired. A resealed preview
   inventory cannot drop or add segments. A shared preview is copied once. Reference PNGs decode
   exactly. Revocation, cancellation, unknown files and linked paths fail without mutation.
6. **V3 is still refused at admission and claim.** The held-V3 fixture checks both on every use, and
   the HV-016-24 admission test still passes.
