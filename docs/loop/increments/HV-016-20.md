# HV-016-20 — Page breaks, sections and synopses became shots, and `>` lines kept their markers

epic: HV-016
release: 2 "Voice and crew depth"
gate: none new
spend_usd: 0

## Why

The parser's only rule for Fountain's marked lines was `/^[<>~_*]{3,}/` (an unparseable construct);
everything else outside a speech was action. So:

- a page break `===`, a section `# ACT ONE` and a synopsis `= She is nervous.` were each an action
  beat, and `planShots` makes every action beat a shot. The film gained shots whose prompts read
  `INT. BAR - NIGHT. ===` or `INT. BAR - NIGHT. ## Sequence two`, and paid for them;
- a section before the first scene heading was reported as an unparseable construct;
- a forced transition `> BURN TO WHITE.` was a shot of those words, and one ending in `TO:` was a
  transition that kept its `>`;
- centred text `>THE END<` was shot with its markers in the prompt.

## What this increment does

- **Outside a speech, a line starting `#` or `=` is skipped** (`packages/parser/src/index.ts`):
  sections, synopses and page breaks are the writer's outline, not the film.
- **`> X` is a transition "X"** outside a speech, and a `TO:` transition written `> CUT TO:` loses its
  `>` too. **`>X<` is action "X".** A run of three marker characters is still unparseable, and inside a
  speech every line is spoken as written.
- **`printedText`** is exported, and the living script document's evidence check reads a marked line
  through it, as the parser does. Without it, a document holding `>THE END<` was refused whole.
- **The PDF and Final Draft importers refuse a line starting `#`, `=` or `>`,** by line number, the way
  HV-016-12 made them refuse `/*` and `[[`: the importer's text is the writer's literal words, and the
  parser would now drop or re-mark that line. Fountain has no escape for any of them.

## Acceptance criteria

1. **Page break.** `===` between two action lines is neither a beat nor a shot.
2. **Sections and synopses.** `#`, `##` and `=` lines are neither action nor shots, inside a scene or
   before the first heading, and raise no warning.
3. **Forced transition.** `> BURN TO WHITE.` and `> CUT TO:` are transitions without the `>`.
4. **Centred text.** `>THE END<` and `> INTERMISSION <` are action without markers; no shot prompt
   holds `<` or `>`; `>>>~~~ broken markup ~~~<<<` is still unparseable; inside a speech, `= Two.`,
   `# Three.` and `> Four.` are still spoken lines.
5. **Importers.** A Final Draft or PDF line starting `#`, `=` or `>` is refused with its line number;
   a marker elsewhere in a line is imported.
6. **Living script.** A document holding a page break, a synopsis, a section, centred text and a
   forced transition binds the action and transition beats and puts the rest in no beat.
7. Reverting the source files fails criteria 1–6; reverting only the living script's check fails 6.

- Tests: `packages/parser/test/non-printing-elements.test.ts`.

## Known gaps this leaves

- **The importers refuse more than the parser would drop.** The check is by line, so a speech line
  starting `#`, `=` or `>` (which the parser would still speak) is refused too. A refusal names the
  line; a silent drop would not.
- **Forced action `!`, lyrics `~`, emphasis and notes across lines** are still not read; `!` and `~`
  lines are action with the mark in the text, as before.
- **Sections and synopses are discarded,** not kept as outline metadata for the editor.
- **A page break does not start a page** in the page estimate, which still counts lines.
