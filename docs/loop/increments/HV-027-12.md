# HV-027-12 — A dialogue replacement was refused once another project spent the month

epic: HV-027
release: 2 "Voice and crew depth"
gate: none new
spend_usd: 0

## Why

HV-027-11 taught `CapacityController.decide` what a job will hold. Every zero-cost route it found
passes `requestedUsd:0`, so another project's paid renders no longer refuse them: deliverables,
sound mixes, editorial and assembly exports, and motion graphics. The dialogue replacement route in
`server.ts` was not in that list. Its job holds nothing either (`costCapUsd:0,budgetReservedUsd:0`),
but it asked capacity with only the month's spend. Once the studio's paid renders reached the
budget, a creator could not dub or replace a line in a film they had already made. They were told
*"We're at capacity right now. Your script is saved"*.

## What this increment does

- **The dialogue replacement route's capacity check passes `requestedUsd:0`,** like its siblings.
  Concurrency and the shot limit still apply to it.

## Acceptance criteria

1. **The route admits after the month is spent.** A $0 dialogue replacement is admitted (202,
   `costUsd` 0) after another project's spend reaches the month's budget.
2. **The source says so.** The capacity check just before the dialogue job's admission passes
   `requestedUsd:0`, and the job it admits holds nothing.
3. Reverting `server.ts` fails criteria 1 and 2.

- Tests: `packages/api/test/dialogue-zero-cost-capacity.test.ts`.

## Known gaps this leaves

- **The zero-cost route list in `zero-cost-capacity.test.ts` is still per file.** `server.ts` holds
  paid and zero-cost admissions side by side, so it is checked here by locating the dialogue
  admission rather than by that list.
