# HV-021-09 — The Continuity Supervisor joins the crew

epic: HV-021
release: 2 "Voice and crew depth"
gate: none new
spend_usd: 0 — no provider or model is called for anything this increment adds.

## Why

Release 2's HV-021 slice is "a continuity persona: drift detection and one-click repair". The
detection and the repair exist (HV-021-01 to -06). The persona didn't. HV-021-02's Known gaps said so:
"There is still no Continuity Supervisor persona."

So the crew planned a film and said nothing about continuity. Take the two-scene kitchen and garden
script the crew tests use. After "Plan the film", the continuity report `GET /direction` serves
already says MAYA and LEO have no reference image and a kitchen shot starts without the frame before
it. The crew's notes listed Casting, the Cinematographer, the Director, Sound and the Editor, and
nobody mentioned either finding.

**This increment depends on HV-021-07 (PR #323) landing first.** On main today, nothing in the studio
shows that report or the repair. HV-021-07 adds the desk's Continuity panel, with "Review continuity
repair" and "Apply continuity repair". The Supervisor's notes point there, so they are true only once
that panel is on main. This PR is opened after #323 merges.

## What this increment does

- **A seventh crew member, `continuity`, "Continuity Supervisor"** (`packages/planner/src/crew/personas.ts`).
  It is on the roster (`CREW`) with `speaks: "continuity-report"`.
  - It is kept out of `PERSONAS`, the list the read-through tells the model about. That list is also
    the only set of personas a question, an answer or a style-card choice may name.
  - So the model is never invited to ask continuity questions in its name, and no answer can be
    attributed to it. The read-through prompt is unchanged.
- **It speaks in the plan's notes** (`packages/planner/src/crew/continuity-supervisor.ts`). Once
  `POST /crew/plan` has applied the crew's changes, the route builds the continuity report over the
  new cast and direction. This is the same `continuityReport` call `GET /direction` makes, at its
  default 24 shots. The Supervisor's notes go last in `notes`.
  - **One sentence per kind of finding**, naming the scenes. For wardrobe and reference images it
    names the cast the report names. Ten scenes with drifting key light are one sentence.
  - **Capped.** A note lists up to six scenes or names and counts the rest. There are at most six
    notes; past that, the last one counts the kinds left over.
  - **Only what the report finds.** A kind this file doesn't know yet is said in the report's own
    words.
  - **Nothing to compare is not a pass.** No planned shots, or nothing stated twice: "Nothing to
    compare yet". Comparisons with no findings: how many were compared, and that it reads declarations,
    not pictures.
  - **Marked as its own.** Each note is `{persona: "continuity", change, source: "continuity-report"}`.
    No other note has `source`.
  - **Pointing at the desk.** A drifting look's note names "Review continuity repair" under
    Continuity at the Director's desk (HV-021-07's panel). The overflow note points to the same
    section.
  - **How much it checked.** The answer carries `continuityComparisons`, the number of comparisons
    the report could make.
- **Why the plan step.** The read-through runs before the crew has directed anything, so its report
  would be empty. The plan step is the first with the cast and direction the report compares. Its
  notes are already shown at the look approval and pass through the same `CrewNote` list, so how
  they are shown is unchanged.
- **The studio names it** (`packages/frontend/src/titles.js`). `PERSONA_TITLES.continuity` is
  "Continuity Supervisor", so its notes read "Continuity Supervisor: …" rather than "undefined: …".
  The credits gain "Continuity by: Continuity Supervisor (AI crew)" only for a film it checked: the
  plan answered with its `continuity-report` notes and at least one comparison (`continuityChecked`).
  "Nothing to compare yet", or a resumed film whose plan wasn't retained, earns no credit.

## Acceptance criteria

1. **The Supervisor is on the roster and outside the model's crew.** `CREW` is the six personas plus
   `continuity`, titled "Continuity Supervisor". The read-through prompt doesn't mention it. A model
   question and a creator answer in its name are refused.
2. **Its notes are the report's findings.** For a film with a heading contradiction, two drifting
   look fields, unstated wardrobe, unanchored cast and absent handoffs, the notes are exactly one
   sentence per kind, in a fixed order. They name exactly the report's scenes and cast. The same
   report gives the same notes, and a finding the report drops is no longer mentioned.
3. **Deduplicated and capped.** Ten findings of one kind are one note listing six scenes "and 4
   more". Seven kinds give six notes, the last counting the two left over. An unknown kind is given in
   the report's own words.
4. **No findings is said honestly.** No planned shots, nothing stated twice, and comparisons with
   nothing contradicting each get their own sentence.
5. **The plan route returns them, exactly as the desk's report reads.** After `POST /crew/plan`, the
   notes with persona `continuity` equal the notes made from the `continuity` that `GET /direction`
   returns. They carry `source: "continuity-report"`, come last, and no other note has a `source`. A
   second pass says the same. `continuityComparisons` is that report's comparison count.
6. **They cost nothing.** With a crew model that fails the test if it is asked for anything but the
   plan, the plan route asks it once, and the crew's spend is that one request.
7. **The studio shows it, and credits it only for a film it checked.** At the look approval, its note
   reads "Continuity Supervisor: …". A plan with its report notes and comparisons above zero gives a
   "Continuity by: Continuity Supervisor (AI crew)" credit. A plan with zero comparisons, without its
   notes, with a note that lacks the report's mark, or with no plan at all gives none.
8. Reverting `continuity-supervisor.ts` fails criteria 1–6. Reverting `personas.ts` fails criteria
   1–4. Reverting the route change in `server.ts` fails criteria 5 and 6. Reverting `titles.js`, or only the
   `continuity` flag `studio.js` passes to the credits, fails criterion 7.

- Tests: `packages/planner/test/crew-continuity-supervisor.test.ts` (1–4),
  `packages/api/test/crew-continuity-supervisor-route.test.ts` (5–6),
  `packages/frontend/test/studio-continuity-notes.test.js` and the last test in
  `packages/frontend/test/studio.test.js` (7). The roster check in `studio.test.js` now counts seven
  titles. The default credits in `titles.test.js` are unchanged, at six crew rows. The
  planner's crew and continuity tests pass (82 / 0), as do the frontend suite (378 / 0) and the API's
  crew, continuity and graphics tests (33 / 0, with 4 graphics renders skipped as before).

## Known gaps this leaves

- **Depends on HV-021-07.** The notes point to the desk's Continuity panel and its "Review continuity
  repair", which are HV-021-07's (PR #323). Until that merges, nothing in the studio shows them, so
  this PR waits for it.
- **The crew step repairs nothing.** The Supervisor reports. The one-click repair stays at the
  Director's desk, behind its review and exact-match accept (HV-021-02, shown by HV-021-07).
- **It speaks once, at the plan.** A later edit at the desk, a re-plan or a pinned still changes the
  report. The Supervisor says nothing again until the crew plans again. The rough cut and final
  approvals carry no continuity note.
- **It asks no questions.** It has no read-through voice and nothing for a style card to remember. A
  continuity question (for example "hold the coat across the scene?") would need the model's crew
  list and the answer validators to grow. That is a model-facing change, not taken here.
- **It reads the 24-shot plan.** That is the plan step's own limit and the desk's default. A creator
  who reviews the 60-shot plan at the desk can see findings the note didn't cover.
- **Still declarations, not pictures.** Its notes are only as visual as the report, which compares
  what the film states and examines no frame (docs/CONTINUITY.md).
