# HV-031-10 — The minor-sexual-content rule missed common ways of writing a minor

epic: HV-031
release: 2 "Voice and crew depth"
gate: none new
spend_usd: 0

## Why

Minor sexual content is the one FR-054 category this studio must never let reach a provider. The rule
works by pairing a word for a minor with a sexual term, and its list of words for a minor was:

```
child(?:ren)?|minors?|underage|pre-?teens?|teen(?:ager)?s?|(?:[0-9]|1[0-7])[-\s]+years?[-\s]+old
```

That list caught some forms of a word and missed others:

| Caught | Missed |
|---|---|
| "teenager" | "teenage" |
| "children" | "kids" |
| "15-year-old" | "15yo", "fifteen-year-old" |
| — | "schoolgirl", "toddler", "adolescent" (none caught at all) |

Every one of these went through `checkPrompt` as allowed:

```
"a teenage girl, nude, explicit"                  -> ALLOWED
"nude kids on the beach"                          -> ALLOWED
"a fifteen-year-old in an explicit sexual scene"  -> ALLOWED
"a 15yo, naked"                                   -> ALLOWED
"a schoolgirl, naked, explicit"                   -> ALLOWED
```

## What this increment does

- **The list of words for a minor grows.** It now includes:
  - "kid(s)", "juvenile(s)", "adolescent(s)", "(pre)pubescent";
  - "teenage", "teenaged";
  - "schoolgirl" and "schoolboy" (with or without a space), "infant", "toddler", "baby";
  - "little girl" and "little boy", "young girl" and "young boy";
  - the slang terms for sexualised minors;
  - ages 0–17 written as "15yo", "15 y.o." or "15yr old", and ages one to seventeen spelled out.
- **Nothing else changes.** Each word still has to pair with a sexual term anywhere in the text,
  in either order, and no word refuses on its own. The two-search form from HV-031-05 stays linear
  (see the timing test).
- **A planner test fixture changes.** In `crew-introductions.test.ts`, the fixture for "the
  stand-in never becomes what makes a script refused" read "The little girl, EVE, reads an explicit
  warning label." It needs a script that passes on its own, and "little girl" is now a word for a
  minor. It now reads "The girl, EVE, …", and the test's purpose is unchanged. The rule's header
  says this is the trade it makes: a false refusal costs a rewrite, while a false pass costs a
  provider submission the policy forbids.

## Acceptance criteria

1. **Each wording that was allowed is now refused** as `minor_sexual_content` with no provider
   call. There are nineteen prompts covering every added form.
2. **The pairing still works in either order across the whole text.**
3. **A minor word alone is still allowed, and so is an adult age with a sexual term.** This covers
   "The kids build a raft.", "A teenage band plays…", "a 25-year-old, naked" and "a 25yo nude model".
4. **The widened list stays linear.** A string of minor words repeated to about 194,000 characters
   is checked in under a second.
5. Reverting `packages/safety/src/index.ts` fails criteria 1 and 2.

- Tests: `packages/safety/test/minor-terms.test.ts`. `packages/safety`, `packages/planner` and
  `packages/parser` pass, with one fixture updated as described above.

## Known gaps this leaves

- **It is still a keyword rule.** It closes observed gaps, and a determined writer will find others:
  other languages, deliberate misspellings, euphemism. The image and audio moderation in HV-031's
  Release 2 slice is the second line.
- **"girl" and "boy" alone are not minor words**, because both are used for adults. "little" and
  "young" are needed to qualify them.
- **Over-inclusion is real.** "The kids gave explicit instructions" is now refused. The rule has
  always refused "children" beside "explicit" in the same way.
