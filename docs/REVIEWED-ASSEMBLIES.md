# Reviewed alternate assemblies

This is the active local P8 increment after script-linked navigation, as of 2026-09-08. Saved proposals and acceptance, project persistence, child-clock preview, reviewed render admission, durable checkpoints, independent exports and recovery are implemented. Owner controls are integrated and have focused API/frontend tests. The final owner browser walkthrough, complete regression/CI gates and PostgreSQL/S3 archive qualification are still open for this increment. No merge or deployment qualification is claimed. The full P1–P18 program in [FULL-SCOPE.md](FULL-SCOPE.md) remains the completion criterion.

An assembly retains a complete immutable parent timeline, its sequence/history identity and ordered original receipt bindings. Its ordered half-open ranges address the parent's output frames. Repetition and overlap are intentional; they are not unioned, sorted or flattened into new ordinary clips. Every range has a stable identity and explanation. Joins are explicit cuts without added smoothing or speed changes.

Evaluating in parent time preserves partial dissolves and their borrowed handles, picture stacking order, original mask keys, independently timed mattes, fade phase, audio overlap, speed ramps, held frames, caption clocks and gaps. Removing or reanchoring parent clips would change these results. The child has independent output positions and duration; these positions are never presented as parent clip positions.

`hv-edit-assembly/1` seals the full snapshot and range map. It supports one to 256 ranges and up to 108,000 output frames. Strict validation checks portable data, source binding order, range bounds and content revisions. Frame/sample lookup, bounded output windows and inverse provenance projection retain every selected occurrence. These limits do not establish interactive performance at maximum scale.

The pure proposal library seals labels, purpose, plan and acceptance provenance. Optimistic library/proposal/parent checks reject stale edits. Acceptance copies the reviewed plan; an exact lost-response retry returns the original accepted identity, while a different destination or revision conflicts. The owner service derives parent snapshots from saved history, rechecks original carrier availability and permissions, and persists accepted copies independently. PostgreSQL mutations lock the project and original carriers; the local project service has same-process synchronous mutation semantics, without a shared interprocess lock. Short or long sixty-second proposals retain their actual target outcome, with no silent padding or truncation.

Structural review reports original coverage, omitted ranges, repeated duration, ordered joins and exact duration targets. Coverage union is used only for omission accounting. These facts do not establish visible action, audible speech, pacing or creative quality. Selected-boundary review maps discontinuous range edges through the original source clock to measured speech and unmeasured audio; existing parent trims remain separately identified. Held speech is explicitly marked as muted. The owner API and conform report retain these distinct reviews.

The independent conform evaluates selected picture spans at their original parent coordinates and emits them consecutively in child time. It prepares parent picture sources once and retains parts of at most 60 frames. The audio mixer evaluates parent sample windows and writes all six lanes plus the final PCM waveform with the existing gain, varispeed, rounding and clipping rules. Captions are derived from the full parent first, intersected in sample time, given occurrence-specific identities and rounded to WebVTT milliseconds afterward. Caption limits reject overflow without returning partial output.

`conformEditAssembly` produces lossless picture parts, seven PCM waveforms, WebVTT, a probed MP4 and HLS. Its separate `hv-edit-assembly-conform/1` report and `assembly.json` bind the exact parent and selected ranges. It does not disguise an assembly as an ordinary edit timeline or an existing archived render. Existing ordinary conform recipes and report schemas remain intact.

Proposal and accepted-assembly previews use the existing private session and page pools. Their 60-frame pages address child time; audio evaluates only the selected parent sample windows, and picture packets contain up to 16 ordered, finished `timeline-composite` PNGs. Contiguous parent spans share verified source decoding and final encoding; range seams and conservative resource estimates split internal batches. Sparse callers remain supported, with the existing 64 MiB/32-file picture workspace and shared cache limits. Film YUV, native alpha and mask/matte paths retain the full parent's recipe selection, including effects outside the selected range. Final preview resizing occurs once after the full parent frame is composed. The source key binds the plan, parent recipe, runtime and prepared original identities. Changed proposals invalidate old sessions; accepted previews preserve their frozen parent after later proposal or sequence edits. Exact captured-scope DELETE remains available, and current permission checks also guard cached media.

Render admission creates a distinct `assembly-edit` job with a sealed `hv-edit-assembly-render-plan/1`. Review binds the accepted assembly, structural and new-boundary findings, inherited parent review, current source carriers and capacity estimate. Explicit request identities support retry and lookup after an uncertain response. The worker prepares original media, renders, verifies and saves an owned checkpoint before completion. Checkpoint recovery reproduces retained originals, selected picture frames, PCM and delivery without requiring earlier carriers. Current original permissions still apply. The separate `hv-edit-assembly-output/1` receipt validates nested reports, exact parent-coordinate picture parts, seven child-duration waveforms, captions and the complete artifact inventory. These jobs make no new provider-generation dispatch or reservation; original performance and invoice receipts remain retained. Completed output has owner playback/download routes and explicit export selection.

Synthetic media checks compare child frames and decoded pixels against selected parent output, and compare every PCM waveform byte for byte. Cases include partial odd-length dissolves, native graphics, matte gaps, stacking order, ramps, holds, reordered/repeated ranges, inactive effects, permission withdrawal, cancellation and changed sources. An integrated conform exercises MP4 frame count, caption round-trip, HLS completion and the saved report. Lossless picture/PCM comparisons establish parent-range equivalence; the delivery encoder has its own child context.

Two caption re-admission fixes support both existing edits and assemblies: the parser accepts the exact millisecond ceiling at a final frame boundary, and bounds decoded text separately from escaped entity expansion. It still clamps final cues to the true frame end and preserves previously valid caption identities.

Capacity estimates use actual selected picture parts and child duration, including repeated ranges and seams, while retaining every original, canonical source waveform, full dependency graph and parent processing scratch. Existing limits remain 48 GiB of retained output, 128 GiB of workspace, 80,000 files and a 512 MiB free-space reserve. Full admitted output dimensions remain at most 1920×1080. Screenplay navigation first projects through the parent clock, then clips and translates each selected occurrence into child time. Repeats have independent identities and source endpoints are recomputed through the original ramp/hold clock.

Schema `hv-state/7` preserves proposed and accepted assemblies, including an older accepted parent after its current sequence is removed, plus assembly jobs, checkpoints and completed output. Retained jobs require schema seven even when the project assembly library is absent. Empty or absent libraries preserve historical schema selection only when no assembly job state remains. Schema-seven portable archive validation uses the canonical application validator and requires Bun plus the installed source/dependencies; `HV_BUN_PATH` can select an absolute Bun executable. Older archive schemas remain Python-only. Local fixtures exercise snapshot custody and portable archive validation. PostgreSQL/S3 checkpoint and separate-destination archive tests are implemented and wired into CI; their current CI qualification remains outstanding.
