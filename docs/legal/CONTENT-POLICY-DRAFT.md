# Content Policy (DRAFT — NOT counsel-reviewed; publication fail-closed per ADR-0020)

Four prohibitions. Prompts or scripts that fall in these categories are refused
before any provider call, with no charge and no account consequence:

1. Sexual content involving minors, in any form.
2. Non-consensual intimate imagery or deepfakes of real people.
3. Incitement, glorification, or instruction of violence or terrorism.
4. Hate or dehumanization targeting protected groups.

Takedown: verified requests complete within 24 hours, are logged, and are
irreversible (packages/api ProjectService.takedown).
